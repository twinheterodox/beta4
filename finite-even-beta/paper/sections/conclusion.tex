\section{The irrationality contradiction}\label{sec:conclusion}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Suppose at most two of $\beta(2),\beta(4),\ldots,\beta(364)$ are
irrational. Choose distinct even indices $i,j$ in $[2,364]$ such that
every other value in the list is rational.
The columns $v_i=(1,2^i,3^i)^{\mathsf T}$ and $v_j$ are independent.
Their cross product gives a nonzero integer vector
$w=(w_1,w_2,w_3)$ with $w\cdot v_i=w\cdot v_j=0$.
By Lemma~\ref{lem:seed},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:seed-nonzero}
 q_w(2):=\sum_{b=1}^3w_bq_b(2)\ne0.
\end{equation}
Fix this vector for the rest of the argument and put
$H_n=\sum_{b=1}^3w_br_{n,b}$.

Equation~\eqref{eq:forms} expresses $H_n$ as a rational constant plus
a rational linear combination of the remaining beta values. All those
values are rational by assumption. Let $D$ be a positive common
denominator of these finitely many rational values. Proposition
\ref{prop:denominators} shows that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:integer-contradiction}
 D\delta_n^{365}H_n\in\Z.
\end{equation}
Because the weights are fixed, Proposition~\ref{prop:decay} makes these
integers tend to zero exponentially along even $n$.

It remains to show that infinitely many are nonzero. Take $n=2p$,
where $p$ is a sufficiently large prime, coprime to $D$ and to the
numerator and denominator of $q_w(2)$.
By \eqref{eq:q-dilation},
\[
 p^{364}\sum_{b=1}^3w_bq_b(2p)
       \equiv\ch(p)3^{-365}q_w(2)\not\equiv0\pmod p.
\]
For each remaining index $k$, the rational number $\beta(k)$ is
$p$-integral because $p\nmid D$. After the same scaling, its coefficient
is divisible by $p^k$ by \eqref{eq:rho-dilation} and the integrality of
the weights. Thus every remaining beta term reduces to zero modulo
$p$, while the constant term does not. Hence $H_{2p}\ne0$.

The integers in \eqref{eq:integer-contradiction} are therefore nonzero
for infinitely many even $n$, although they tend to zero. This
contradiction proves the theorem.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Reproduction and review status}

Run \texttt{python3 checks/verify.py} from the paper directory. It uses
only the Python standard library and writes \texttt{checks/verification.json}.
The finite seed, all elimination pairs, the two decay comparisons and
the normalized seed denominators are checked exactly. It also tests
the dilation identity at selected primes in all four residue classes
modulo $12$; these sampled evaluations are consistency checks, whereas
Section~\ref{sec:dilation} proves the general congruence.

An independent proof audit supplied on 3 November 2026 reported no
substantive mathematical gap in the preceding plain-text proof and
independently reconstructed its finite certificate without using the
supplied verification scripts. This manuscript includes the auditor's
requested local-power-series explanation and reorganizes the exposition.
The audit applies to the recorded plain-text version; this typeset
revision has not received a new independent audit. The review record
identifies the exact version and scope. Neither the computations nor
the reported audit constitute formal verification.
