Correctness review
------------------
The manuscript was assembled from the completed mathematical records,
with the following distinctions preserved:

* The all-subset dyadic formula is an equality, not a termwise lower
  estimate. Its finite orthogonal system, support closure, unit
  multiplier and basis determinant are all included.
* The necessary monic denominator applies before specializing the beta
  variable. The final obstruction concerns the q^-2 upper envelope,
  uniformly over q, and is not a lower bound for actual approximants.
* Both cofactor normalizations are declared. Full-radical compression
  retains degree K; the rational added block can be removed, but its
  Schur correction remains. The height cost is relative to the same
  cofactor basis and is not a monomial coefficient bound.
* The three low-degree content factors have disjoint prime supports.
  The two-layer factor replaces its first-layer part. Exact rational
  rank, rather than rank only modulo p, supplies the three-power gain
  in the tail window. Rank-deficient beta coefficients are allowed.
* The thirteen-value Gram polynomial is normalized after rational-block
  removal. Its two-sided residual estimate is separate from unresolved
  primitive arithmetic; the six finite cases prove no growth law.
* Formal rank and the classification of zero multipliers are separate
  from actual nonvanishing. The eleven-row result uses the same full
  clearer and controlled multipliers; column clearing is used only
  for lattice height. The complete fifteen-row target is still open.
* The fixed-direction observation and Loewner screen retain their
  specific hypotheses. The latter is an inconclusive cost comparison.
