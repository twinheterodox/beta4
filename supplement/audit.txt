SUPPLEMENT ASSEMBLY REVIEW RECORD
Date: 31 October 2026

Status and scope
----------------
The first complete companion supplement is 29 pages, including its claim
register and references. It complements the 14-page determinant paper.
Both remain local and unpublished.

This is a review by the model that assembled the supplement, supported by
exact calculations and selected certificate replays. It is not a fresh
independent model audit, a human mathematical audit, or proof-assistant
verification. The archived reviews retain their own recorded scopes.

Eight claim groups have selected complete statements rewritten here:
S01, S03, S04, S05, S06, S09, E04 and P07. Two further groups, H01 and H02,
have partial source-level results rewritten. The eight C records belong
to the companion paper. The remaining 39 records are catalogued with their
archived status, not certified as newly audited theorems. Even within a
rewritten group, assertions outside the selected statement retain their
archived status; coverage.json gives the distinction entry by entry.

Mathematical review
-------------------
1. Normalization. Actual primitive pairs and primitive polynomials are
   distinguished from a chosen sufficient denominator. The irrationality
   criterion retains the polynomial-degree cost. Formal coefficient
   identities do not assume independence of the periods involved.

2. Positive cone. The unrestricted rational nonnegative-polynomial cone
   and its degree law are proved. They are not obstructions to all positive
   sources. The fixed-degree boundary formulas remain archived, and the
   degree-16 witness is retained only as finite evidence.

3. Repeated quadratics. The exact Euler-source reversal, surviving fourth
   prime layers and real minimax bound apply to the specified even powers
   and subexponential coefficient schedules. They do not exclude arbitrary
   rational Euler sources or the separate two-endpoint construction.

4. Euler--Boole. The remainder lower bound is uniform in truncation order,
   and the reduced-denominator argument is uniform in that order while
   the endpoint grows. Bounded endpoints with increasing truncation are
   treated separately. The partial-fraction remainder is used on the whole
   integration ray; a local Taylor series is not integrated past its disk.

5. Differentiated continuants. The error asymptotic is justified on a
   complex neighborhood before differentiating. The prime valuation uses
   the actual reduced quotient and retains the excluded-prime polynomial.
   The obstruction fixes the endpoint and uses even depth. Growing
   endpoints and arithmetic at odd depth are outside the theorem.

6. Cyclotomic norm. Integrality, the principal error, all conjugates, the
   algebraic degree and coefficient content are handled for one specified
   sine normalization at odd prime levels. No assertion places beta(4)
   in a cyclotomic field without first assuming it rational. The primitive
   minimal polynomial and its degree factor are both retained.

7. Completed Mellin kernel. The rational-kernel lower bound uses exact
   polynomial division, a Sturm certificate and rational tail enclosures.
   Identification of its completed integral with beta(4) explicitly uses
   Broadhurst's printed ladder identities. The external Kummer/Lewin
   derivation was not independently re-audited. The rational-kernel theorem
   itself does not require that identification. Signed cancellation is
   outside its positive-source conclusion.

8. Monodromy. The nonzero residue after continuation forces a specified
   rational-coefficient linear operator to annihilate constants. This is
   a scoped obstruction to the proposed regularization, not a failure
   theorem for arithmetic holonomy or nonlinear constructions.

9. Ordinary first variation. The full common numerator gcd is removed.
   Positivity and the sharp scalar exponential rate have all-index proofs;
   the finite primitive pairs do not prove denominator growth. The weight
   is used as positive for 0 <= u < 1, not for all real |u| < 1. The weak
   arithmetic threshold is necessary and the strict threshold sufficient;
   equality is not settled.

10. Wider record. The repaired forward/dual identity is retained. The
    interleaved-frame result remains a limitation of a specified arithmetic
    estimate, with a narrower integer-clearing obstruction at one width.
    Neither is promoted to a general primitive-content theorem. Exact
    rediscoveries are identified instead of counted as additional families.

Computation and preservation
----------------------------
checks/supplement-reproduction.json records the successful run with Python
3.14.4, SymPy 1.14.0 and mpmath 1.3.0. Three archived scripts ran in temporary
copies and reproduced their complete stored JSON outputs:

  - the rational Mellin kernel, Sturm and 32-term tail certificates;
  - 160 Euler coefficients by three methods, 13 symbolic sech coefficients,
    eight geometric identities, 652 prime valuations, 43 reduced fractions
    and four primitive-product checks;
  - exact cyclotomic calculations at primes 3, 5, 7 and 11, alongside
    100-digit diagnostics at 11, 31 and 101.

The cyclotomic floating-point diagnostics are not interval certificates.
The additional exact implementation checked 28 reversal identities,
57 surviving quadratic prime valuations, 36 cone formal pairs,
80 continuant cases, 203 reduced prime exponents, all 24 already archived
ordinary-moment primitive pairs, twelve associated integral identities,
and the monodromy residue coefficient 31/24. No new ordinary-moment index
or interval evaluation was computed. These are finite checks, not proofs
of the asymptotic statements by extrapolation.

All 120 original frozen snapshots are unchanged. Seven verbatim source
files were added under a separate manifest, so all 127 can be checked
against the sibling research archive. The M03 navigation entry was repaired:
its original link pointed to the Euler--Boole report, rather than the
separate modular-pole note. No historical report was rewritten.

Sources and priority
--------------------
The cumulative ledger has twelve locally stored, hash-checked external
PDFs. Eleven are cited in the supplement, alongside the companion paper
and three sections of DLMF under one bibliography entry. The supplement
ledger records actual reading scopes, including the declared Broadhurst
dependency and the limits of contextual readings. This writing pass does
not apply a recent global irrationality theorem.

The general positivity, interpolation, orthogonal-polynomial,
continued-fraction and coefficient-content mechanisms remain classical.
Selected explicit obstruction statements are candidates for external
scrutiny; assembling their proofs is not a certificate of priority.
