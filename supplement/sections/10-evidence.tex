\section{Computations, corrections, and review status}\label{sec:evidence}
The repository separates three layers: the mathematical exposition,
the inventory and method catalog, and verbatim research snapshots.
The latter are historical evidence. A tentative conclusion inside an old
note is not a current theorem if its later review corrected it.
The register covers all 57 grouped entries and all twelve method groups;
this is coverage of the selected archive, not a claim that every idea
ever proposed for $\beta(4)$ has been investigated.

The companion paper's core exact and Arb checks have their own runner.
For this supplement, a separate runner executes preserved scripts in a
temporary directory, records fresh results, and checks the frozen source
hashes. It includes the rational Sturm and tail certificate used in
Theorem~\ref{thm:mellin}. Additional finite algebra checks cover the
rewritten scalar, Euler--Boole, continuant and monodromy formulas.
The precise cases, counts, dependencies and results are in
\path{checks/supplement-reproduction.json}. The expanded case studies
have an additional independent implementation of finite exact formulas
in \path{checks/case_studies.py}, including partial fractions, prime
reductions, endpoint reconstruction and determinant identities. Its
report is \path{checks/case-studies-result.json}. Here ``independent
implementation'' means a separate calculation by the assembling model,
not an independent model or human audit.

The paired-identity directory is self-contained: its rational matrices,
polynomial problem definitions and exact solver reconstruct all ten
certificates, rather than depending on the original research environment.
It records exact matrix transport, regularity, exceptional initial cases
and the closed normalizing scalar. Separate scripts reproduce the floor
integration and enclose the numerical rates with Arb. The written proof
in Section~\ref{subsec:paired-proof} explains the transfer from algebraic
certificates to finite sums and differentiated tails. Computation does
not replace those analytic boundary arguments. No large parameter search
is part of this writing pass. Archived tests outside that runner retain
their original status and are not described as rerun.

Three corrections are particularly consequential. The quarter
forward/dual identity mismatch was repaired in the later deformation
record. The ordinary first-variation examples require removal of the
\emph{full} common numerator gcd; their original clearing-only table
is superseded. Finally, the interleaved-frame negative Smith layers and
integer-clearing obstruction do not bound every rational primitive
rescaling. The final method record preserves these distinctions.

All work was prepared with Codex. The main paper, this supplement and
accompanying code have passed an independent model review. The historical
record also contains model cross-checks and calculations. The independent
model review is not a human mathematical audit or proof-assistant
verification. Finite exact
calculations check identities and specified instances; they do not
prove asymptotic statements by extrapolation. The Sturm and tail
calculation is explicitly distinguished because it certifies finite
inequalities used in one proof.

The source ledger records versions, local PDFs, hashes, actual reading
scope and unchecked dependencies. The specific obstruction statements
are candidates for external scrutiny, not priority claims inferred from
a failed literature search. Classical positivity, interpolation,
orthogonal-polynomial, continued-fraction and content-removal mechanisms
remain classical even when applied in a new experiment.
