\begin{abstract}
This supplement records an investigation of rational approximation and
related constructions for $\beta(4)$. It proves scoped obstructions for
repeated-quadratic Mellin sources, Euler--Boole truncations,
fixed-endpoint differentiated continued fractions, and one cyclotomic
minimal-polynomial family. It also gives a positive-source inequality for
a completed Mellin kernel and a monodromy obstruction to a specified
linear regularization. Two complementary results delimit these failures:
unrestricted positive polynomial sources encode every positive rational
linear form, while an ordinary-moment first variation has a positive error
with a sharp exponential rate but unresolved primitive arithmetic.
A method-by-method record covers the other constructions, finite tests,
exact rediscoveries and repaired claims. The two determinant obstructions
of the companion paper are referenced rather than reproved. Nothing here
proves rationality or irrationality of $\beta(4)$, or rules out an entire
method such as determinants, modular forms or arithmetic holonomy.
\end{abstract}
