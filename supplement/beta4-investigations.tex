\title{Investigations of irrationality constructions for $\beta(4)$\\
\large Technical supplement: obstructions, reductions, and unresolved arithmetic}
\author{\normalsize Research note prepared with Codex}
\date{31 October 2026}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
An accurate rational approximation to a constant need not help prove
its irrationality: multiplying the error by the denominator may make
it large. This supplement examines that difficulty for
$\beta(4)=1-3^{-4}+5^{-4}-\cdots$ through several natural constructions.
It proves failure results for specified polynomial integral sources,
asymptotic corrections to partial sums, differentiated continued
fractions, and algebraic approximations built from roots of unity.
Further arguments identify limitations imposed by positivity constraints
and by analytic continuation. The conclusions concern the stated
families and operations. Two results explain the limits of a broader
negative conclusion: unrestricted positive polynomial sources can
represent every positive rational linear form in $\beta(4)$, and a
parameter-dependent moment construction gives positive errors with a
sharp decay rate while its denominator growth remains unresolved.
Selected complete proofs are followed by a guide to the wider
investigation, distinguishing proved obstructions, insufficient
estimates and finite computations. The companion paper develops two
determinant obstructions in detail. Neither document settles the
rationality or irrationality of $\beta(4)$.
\end{abstract}
