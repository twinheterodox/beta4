\begin{abstract}
For every positive odd integer $m$, we prove that at least one of
\[
 \beta(m+1),\beta(m+3),\ldots,\beta(19m-1)
\]
is irrational, where $\beta$ is the Dirichlet beta function.
More generally, for each positive integer $B$ we give an explicit
constant $C_B$ such that at least $B$ even beta values with indices
between $m+1$ and $C_Bm-1$ are irrational, for every positive odd $m$.
Specific parameter choices give at least two irrational values among
$\beta(2),\beta(4),\ldots,\beta(70)$, at least three through
$\beta(186)$, and at least four through $\beta(360)$.
\end{abstract}
