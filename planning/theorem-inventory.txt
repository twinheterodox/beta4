CLASSIFICATIONS
===============
primitive_value_obstruction: An archived proof bounds the actual primitive integer
  value or reduced denominator in the specified family; reading status is separate.

arithmetic_theorem: A local valuation, denominator or content statement; not
  necessarily a global primitive-value obstruction.

analytic_theorem: A real-size theorem; arithmetic may still be missing.

structural_obstruction: A proved limitation of a particular construction or
  implication.

structural_theorem: An exact structural description, not necessarily an obstruction.

estimate_limitation: The stated bound or normalization fails; actual primitive values
  may remain unsettled.

finite_evidence: Exact or interval calculations at finitely many prescribed instances.

positive_construction: An identity or positive/nonzero family; this alone does not
  prove irrationality.

partial_construction: Some proof ingredients are available, with an explicit missing
  ingredient.

attributed_identity: Prior published or externally attributed input.

equivalence: An exact reduction to a known or previously archived construction.

standard_fact: General established algebra, not an originality claim.

C01 — A positive cubic-tail kernel isolating beta(4)
====================================================
Class: positive_construction
Placement: main
Statement: Write theta=beta(4) and S_l(a)=sum_{j=0}^{a-1}((4j+1)^(-l)-(4j+3)^(-l)).
  For positive integer a,b, K(a^2,b^2)=[b S_3(b)-a S_3(a)]/[6(b^2-a^2)] off the

C02 — Uniform two-sided comparison with a Cauchy determinant

C08 — Primitive invariance under full rational basis changes
============================================================
Class: standard_fact
Placement: main
Statement: Multiplying a full rational matrix pencil on either side by an invertible
  rational matrix multiplies its determinant by a nonzero rational scalar. Its
  primitive integer polynomial is therefore unchanged up to sign. Multiplying a
  rational linear form by a common nonzero rational scalar likewise leaves its
  primitive pair unchanged.
Limits: Selecting a proper subspace, changing the measure or changing the pencil is
  not such a basis change.
Novelty: Standard framework or elementary consequence; do not present as an original
