Final editorial reading of the main manuscript
2 November 2026

Scope

The entire main manuscript was read, from the abstract through the
appendix and bibliography. The review covered the explanation of the
irrationality strategy, the distinction between the two constructions,
notation, proof transitions, quantifiers, cross-references and the scope
of the conclusions. All 21 pages of the rebuilt PDF were visually
inspected. The supplement was not revised or reread in this pass.

Edits

- Clarify that accurate rational approximation alone is insufficient:
  the nonzero integer linear forms themselves must tend to zero.
- Explicitly restrict the primitive polynomial's minimality to rational
  rescalings that retain integer coefficients.
- Identify the repeated kernel formula as a recall of the same
  construction, now extended to zero.
- Distinguish a relative offset m/n tending to zero from an integer
  offset tending to zero, and make the Riemann-sum parametrization precise.
- In the second construction, distinguish monomials with equal or
  unequal exponents from diagonal or off-diagonal entries of the final
  moment matrix. The latter has theta throughout its entries.
- Use a direct heading for the determinant-size estimates, repeat the
  definition of chi_4 where the second arithmetic proof needs it, and
  state that rational basis changes preserve primitive polynomials up
  to sign.
- Record the 2016 revision date of the cited Zudilin v2 manuscript.

Minor proof qualification

The zero-node appendix now explicitly restricts its local prime argument
to p >= 5, matching Lemma 4.1. The previous wording omitted that inherited
condition. It is necessary at small sizes: for nodes 0,1, the coefficient
of X is -49/27, whose 3-adic valuation is -3 rather than -4. This was
checked by exact rational arithmetic during the reading. Excluding these
small primes does not affect the asymptotic prime sum or the corollary.
