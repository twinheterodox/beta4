FINAL READING OF THE TECHNICAL SUPPLEMENT
2 November 2026

Scope
-----
Read the complete 67-page supplement, including the case studies,
method catalog, claim register and references. This was an editorial
and consistency review by the assembling model. It was not a new
independent mathematical audit or formal verification, and did not
resume the research investigation.

The organization remains appropriate: the introductory tables separate
proved obstructions from unfinished constructions, the substantial cases
state their definitions and dependencies locally, and the catalog keeps
shorter archived variants separate. The supplement is necessarily more
technical than the focused paper. Its section introductions and explicit
remaining questions support reading by method without reconstructing
the research chronology. No further structural rewrite was needed.

Corrections and clarifications
-----------------------------
1. The introduction now compares the error with the denominator of the
   rational approximant. The generally irrational error itself has no
   reduced denominator. The definition of a p-unit explicitly refers
   to its valuation being zero.
2. Section 2's derivative estimate explicitly assumes positive degree.
   Section 3 specifies rational functions and rational polynomials over
   Q; its source-level integrality statement now says P,Q are in Z[t],
   rather than ambiguously saying the rational function has integer
   coefficients. The scope statement spells out what differing numbers
   of cancelled Taylor coefficients mean.
3. Section 9's variational minimum is explicitly over real polynomials.
   Section 10 states the representative range 0 <= a <= p for the
   reflection congruence before extending it by periodicity.
4. Section 11 explains that squared and unsquared poles refer to their
   locations, not pole multiplicities. Both constructions have simple
   poles. The degree bound in the partial-fraction argument explicitly
   refers to the numerator.
5. Section 12 restricts the dyadic estimate to n >= 1 and corrects the
   functional in (12.8) from I(P) to I_U(P), as defined in (7.5).
   The scalar functional I in Section 2 is a different functional.
6. Section 13 specifies that N,a,b are integers and that k >= 1 in the
   sparse family. Its factorial-normalization sentence now refers
   unambiguously to the primitive endpoint pair.
7. Section 16.5 explicitly takes its minima over r in {0,...,14} and
   r in {3,...,9}. These are selected integer terms, not continuous
   intervals. This is the meaning used in the preserved calculation;
   the proof and numerical ceiling are unchanged.
8. Section 18 includes the qualification "up to sign" for primitive
   invariance under rational basis changes, consistent with the main
   paper. Minor wording was simplified in Sections 14 and 18.
9. Eight damaged parity labels in Sections 3, 6, 10 and 13 were repaired.
   Six contained literal carriage returns where a LaTeX command was
   intended, and two contained an embedded newline followed by "m odd".
   They now use explicit text commands for odd/even. These errors did
   not produce LaTeX compilation warnings.
10. Zudilin's cited v2 now records the 2016 revision as well as the
    original 2015 date, matching the companion bibliography.
