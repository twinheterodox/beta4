SELF-CONTAINED SUPPLEMENT REVISION
1 November 2026

Purpose and scope

The user asked for research notes that other mathematicians can use as a
starting point. The former supplement gave complete proofs of selected
obstructions but left several substantial constructions as summaries with
archive pointers. This revision develops those constructions in the text.
It is a writing and reconstruction pass, not a resumed parameter search or
a proof of the irrationality of beta(4).

The resulting supplement has 67 pages. Eight new case-study sections cover
interleaved determinants; squared and unsquared quarter poles; the
common-base polylogarithm ladder; endpoint interpolation and prime deletion;
modular transformations; coupled period reductions and Niven-type sources;
quarter-shifted paired identities; and compatible pole multipliers and slow
logarithmic dilations. Each gives motivation, explicit definitions,
arguments for the established conclusions, and the precise remaining
arithmetic or nonvanishing question.

The introduction now gives separate reading guides to proved results and
unfinished constructions. The wider catalog retains shorter variants and
bounded experiments without promoting their archived classifications to
newly audited theorems. The source register states the treatment of each of
the 57 grouped records. Original research logs are supporting material for
the case studies, rather than a source of missing definitions or proof
steps.

Declared inputs

Self-contained exposition does not mean re-proving every classical input.
The interleaved case states the companion paper's kernel comparison; the
ladder cases explicitly use Broadhurst's printed functional identity.
Hermite's formula, the classical Hankel criterion, the one-variable Polya
determinant estimate and the modular transformation are identified where
used. The paired case states its asymptotic recurrence input, explains its
application to the shifted system, and supplies its algebraic certificates
as standalone data and code. The source ledger records the relevant
reading scopes and dependencies that were not independently re-audited.

Reproducible algebra

checks/paired/ contains exact rational matrices, complete polynomial
problem definitions and a solver that regenerates and substitutes all ten
telescoping certificates. The replay checks matrix transport, regularity,
boundary-degree conditions, exceptional initial indices, the closed scalar
normalization and initial coefficient readouts. It requires python-flint
but not the original research environment, upstream notebooks, a network
connection or the original large expanded certificate files. Separate
scripts reproduce the exact 854-cell floor integration and interval bounds
for the numerical rates. The text supplies the analytic arguments that
transfer the polynomial identities to the infinite sums.

checks/case_studies.py provides 120 bounded exact or interval checks of the
newly printed formulas. These include partial fractions, prime reductions,
Hermite endpoint reconstruction, determinant identities and denominator
bounds. They support the written arguments; they do not turn finite data
into asymptotic theorems.

The existing supplement suite was also rerun successfully. Its three
archived certificate replays and exact identity checks pass, and all 127
frozen evidence files retain their recorded hashes. New results are in
checks/case-studies-result.json and checks/paired/*-result.json, with the
identity replay in checks/paired/result.json. The existing suite's report
is checks/supplement-reproduction.json.

Preservation and limits

The baseline was commit local-prepublication-revision. The main
paper and its canonical PDF were not changed. Original supplement proof
sections 02 through 08, and the cone section following the introduction,
are byte-for-byte unchanged. The smaller catalog was shortened where a
new case study now supplies the argument. Frozen source evidence was not
edited, including superseded stages of the research record.

The distinctions between an integer clearing multiplier and the primitive
rational multiplier, between an insufficient bound and a failed
construction, and between finite observations and all-index results remain
explicit. In particular, no useful primitive estimate or projected
nonvanishing theorem is newly asserted for the paired construction.

Final checks

The final PDF compiles with resolved references and no overfull or underfull
box warnings. All 67 pages were rendered with Poppler at 100 dpi and
inspected in four-page sheets. After the final wording correction, all
pages were rendered again: only page 31 changed, and that page was inspected
separately. No clipping, overlap, broken formulas or illegible layout was
observed. Final hashes, source and local-link checks, claim navigation,
preservation comparisons and reproduction results are recorded in
supplement/validation.json.

This revision was assembled and checked by the writing model. It is not a
fresh independent-model audit, a human mathematical audit or formal
verification. Earlier audit records retain their historical scope. The
revision remains local and unpublished.
