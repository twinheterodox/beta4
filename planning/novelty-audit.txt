FOCUSED NOVELTY AND ATTRIBUTION AUDIT
31 October 2026 — writing stage, local and unpublished

Verdict
-------

There is a plausible focused paper in the exact arithmetic of the two
selected determinant families. The present source comparison does not
establish priority. The defensible provisional claim is:

  For the two explicitly defined families, we prove lower bounds for
  actual primitive polynomial values, including every common coefficient
  factor, by locating coefficients with forced prime valuations.

The paper should not claim the first investigation of beta(4) determinants,
the first positive representation of beta(4), the invention of rational
moment modifications, or a general barrier to proving irrationality.

Scope of this audit
===================

All eighteen integrated research reports and the archive's twelve method
groups were reviewed for selection. The underlying proofs of C01-C08 were
read, with particular attention to coefficient content, uniformity in the
offset, exact prime ranges and lower real bounds. Other entries preserve
their own reading status; many have not been independently re-audited here.

The primary-source comparison is concentrated on the proposed main paper.
It is not a complete priority audit of all 57 inventory entries or a new
reading of the whole literature library. The local library has broader
historical reading records, whose scopes are not silently credited to this
pass. Each cited PDF exists locally and has a recorded version and hash.

1. Established ingredients and closest primary comparisons
=========================================================

Zudilin, A determinantal approach to irrationality, 1507.05697v2
[ZUDILIN2015; https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.05697v2].

Sections 1-2 develop the positive determinant criterion and its integral
identity; later sections explicitly discuss the competing arithmetic and
real-size budgets. This is direct precedent for the organizing framework.
It also cautions against treating smaller real determinants as an automatic
arithmetic gain. Cite it in the introduction and where the general
determinant viewpoint is introduced. Its quantitative integer-Chebyshev
input is not needed for the selected proofs.

Prévost, Remainder Padé approximants for the Hurwitz zeta function,
1709.05389v1 [PREVOST2017; https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.05389v1].

The paper gives orthogonal-polynomial Padé remainder formulas and positive
weights derived from Hurwitz zeta representations. The displayed integral
proofs and the positive-weight framework predate our scalar/variation
constructions. This comparison rules out a broad originality claim for
positive remainders obtained from rational moments. It does not identify
either selected arithmetic theorem with a theorem in that paper.

Krattenthaler, A determinant identity for moments of orthogonal polynomials
that implies Uvarov's formula for the orthogonal polynomials of rationally
related densities, 2103.03969v1
[KRATTENTHALER2021; https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03969v1].

Theorem 1 treats rationally modified moment determinants. Proposition 13
handles repeated parameters, and Section 6 connects the formula to Uvarov.
Thus rational weight changes, determinant formulas in orthogonal polynomials
and confluence should not be presented as new general machinery. The
selected Cauchy comparison has its own short proof and specific uniform
constants. Its exact match, if any, to earlier specializations remains a
separate priority question. The k=0 proof dependency and some confluence
details refer to another paper, not independently audited in this pass.

Carreira, Determinant constructions for the Dirichlet beta value at four,
source commit 974a6e6c7b4bce67538aa1b9bf5883b931789d1f [CARREIRA2026].
https://github.com/MarcosCarreira/catalan-designs/blob/974a6e6c7b4bce67538aa1b9bf5883b931789d1f/paper/beta4_determinants.pdf

This is the closest beta(4)-specific comparison. Its positive/signed
functionals, full primitive polynomial computations and discussion of the
cost of denominator clearing must be acknowledged. Sections 10-12 were
reread for this audit, alongside the Section 6 reading from round 18.
The GitHub API tree check on 31 October matches the locally pinned commit.
Neither first use of positive beta(4) functionals nor first observation of
unfavorable primitive examples belongs to our proposed contribution.

The selected proofs go beyond finite numerical failure for their own
families: C03 treats every consecutive offset schedule, and C06 determines
an exact local primitive exponent for every prime p>4n. The comparison
performed here has not located those precise statements in Carreira's
paper. That observation does not establish worldwide priority. The final
draft should explicitly compare the kernel formulas, rather than relying
on names such as 'positive kernel' to distinguish constructions.

Brown, Mellin transforms, transfinite diameter and rational approximations
of integrals, 2604.20741v1
[BROWN2026; https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20741v1].

Section 7.4 expressly treats denominator clearing, common divisors and
congruences. Removing numerator content is therefore part of the known
method, not a new principle introduced by this investigation. Cite that
discussion for context. The library records a degree-versus-decay issue
with the stated Lemma 7.8; none of our proofs relies on it. The elementary
rationality test in the paper plan keeps the degree factor explicit.

2. What may be original, and what still needs checking
=====================================================

C01-C02: specific positive cubic-tail representation and uniform comparison.
The general integral/determinant identities are standard. The candidate
claim is the exact representation and its uniform comparison for these
nodes, including nonprincipal row/column sets. A formula-level comparison
with Carreira and related Stieltjes specializations remains appropriate.

C03: actual primitive growth for every consecutive principal offset.
The strong point is the unique surviving coefficient after reduction,
combined with a lower real bound and a proof covering arbitrary offset
schedules. Neither a large chosen denominator nor a finite scan would be
enough. This is a main-theorem candidate with a relatively short proof.

C04-C07: the difference-variable Gram construction and exact prime law.
Moment positivity and central-binomial Hankel determinants are classical.
The candidate contribution is their use in this specified beta(4) pencil,
the exact third-layer exponent after primitive normalization, and the
resulting all-index asymptotic obstruction. The leading coefficient being
a power of two is essential to controlling all omitted primes.

C08: rational basis invariance is elementary algebra. State it clearly as
a convention/lemma, without treating it as an original theorem.

S01 and P07: positive-polynomial extremality, Schur complements, Padé error
integrals and differentiation of a minimizing quadratic form are established
mechanisms. Their beta(4) specializations can be useful exposition, but the
arithmetic of the ordinary first-variation family is unresolved. These are
scope/context material, not an additional primitive obstruction.

Other supplemental entries: the exact formulas, local arithmetic or
structural arguments may deserve separate statements. This pass has not
completed their source-level priority checks. In particular the scalar
Euler-Boole, fixed-endpoint continued-fraction, cyclotomic norm, constrained
Mellin and monodromy results should not inherit a novelty verdict from the
determinant audit.

3. Attribution and corrections in the wider archive
==================================================

The completed ladder is an attributed Broadhurst input [BROADHURST1998];
Gangl's functional equation is attributed in L05 [GANGL2002]. Their use and
specialization are separate from inventing an identity. The paired-identity
work must cite the source of that architecture [LEIBTAG2026] at the point
where the forward/dual construction is introduced, then distinguish our
quarter-argument deformation and its proved arithmetic limitation.

The round-12 numerator mismatch is no longer the final conclusion: Q04
records its repair. The round-18 values must use the complete gcd reduction,
not a raw common denominator. The dimensional ladder and arcsine parameter
family were identified with earlier archive constructions; they must not
be counted as new families. Round 17 is an estimate limitation, because
possible positive numerator content was not bounded.

No rationality or linear independence of the periods is assumed to justify
formal coefficient manipulations. A coefficient map that produces a
rational polynomial remains an explicitly defined map, even if theta's
arithmetic nature is unknown.

4. Search limits and publication language
========================================

The exact-phrase and topic queries are recorded in sources/search-log.json.
Most results were noisy or silent. The Calegari blog supplied a bibliography
lead to primary sources; its global irrationality or verification claims
were not used as mathematical inputs. No Google Scholar requests were made.

Use 'we prove the following obstruction for this family' after the proof
has been assembled and checked. Do not write 'the first', 'all determinant
methods fail', 'no known method can work', or 'independently verified' as
an unqualified description of a model cross-check.

A public draft can make a useful, modest contribution without establishing
that no one has ever derived a related special case. The priority question
should remain explicit while the precise theorem statements are circulated.
