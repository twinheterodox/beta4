Accessibility revision, 31 October 2026

Scope
This is an editorial review by the assembling model, undertaken to make
both drafts accessible to expert mathematicians unfamiliar with these
constructions. It is not a fresh independent mathematical audit, a human
review, a priority assessment or formal verification. The drafts remain
local and unpublished.

Changes
Both abstracts and introductions now begin with the irrationality
criterion and the comparison between approximation error and arithmetic
normalization. The main paper explains the degree cost, primitive
normalization and the need for lower bounds, then gives a shared
three-step proof outline and a table of the two growth comparisons.
Each family and major proof stage has an explanation of its purpose.
A three-node, prime-seven example illustrates why a surviving principal
minor forces a denominator even after coefficient cancellation.

Following reader feedback, the introduction now derives the first
matrix before displaying its entries. It identifies S_l(a) as the
first 2a beta-series terms, introduces the shifted cubic tail, and
computes the rational values and the derivative that contains beta(4).
The squared nodes and factors 6 and 12 are explained through the
divided difference and the chain rule. These extra displays are
unnumbered, preserving equations (1.3) and (1.4). The opening paragraph
also consistently uses A/B and B theta - A, matching theta = a/b.

The supplement adds a table of its proved results and a suggested
reading order. Its sections explain the motivation and mechanism of
each construction before introducing the formulas. Definitions include
continuants, norm polynomials, polylogarithms and monodromy. The moment
variation is explicitly distinguished from the failure results: its
analytic estimates succeed, but its reduced-denominator growth remains
unresolved. The wider method guide retains the distinctions between
proofs, insufficient estimates, finite evidence and archived claims.

Mathematical preservation and checks
The baseline is commit local-prepublication-revision;
planning/accessibility-baseline.json records the earlier source/PDF
hashes. Exact comparison finds all 39 existing theorem, lemma,
proposition, corollary, definition and proof environments unchanged.
The comparison is in planning/accessibility-proof-comparison.json.
This protects the original proof text; it does not independently
certify its correctness.

The added explanations were checked against the hypotheses and scope
of the statements they describe. In particular, the first kernel's
diagonal description uses positive integer nodes; the continued-fraction
summary specifies fixed endpoints and even depth; and its error is
described as an asymptotic, not an all-index nonvanishing assertion.
The Mellin interpretation retains its declared external identity input.

The new arithmetic example was recomputed with exact Python fractions
using checks/expository_example.py. It verifies the scaled matrix, all
three principal-minor residues, and valuation -8 of the coefficient of
X at the prime seven. It proves a lower bound of 8 for the multiplier
valuation, not an equality for the full multiplier. The result is in
checks/expository-example.json.

The earlier main-paper and supplement calculation suites were not
rerun for these editorial changes. Their inputs, outputs and report
hashes were rechecked and remain unchanged. All 127 evidence files
were also compared with the original archive, and all 12 local PDFs
in the source ledger were checked against their recorded hashes.
No new literature search, mathematical parameter scan or independent
model audit was commissioned.

Build and visual review
Both drafts compile without unresolved references or overfull/underfull
box warnings. The final main paper has 17 pages; the supplement has 31.
All pages were rendered with Poppler at 100 dpi and visually inspected
for legible mathematics, spacing, table layout, clipping and overlap.
For the earlier incremental wording/layout edits, fresh renders were
compared with the inspected pages; changed pages were inspected again.
The later introduction expansion received a fresh inspection of all
17 main-paper pages. An unnecessary
page break before the supplement's bibliography was removed to avoid
leaving a page with only the final register entry.

The current build records and validation.json files give the final
artifact hashes, reference/link checks and review status. The original
paper/audit.txt and supplement/audit.txt remain unchanged as historical
records of the assembly passes; their page counts describe those drafts.
The pre-existing untracked PDF in paper/ was left untouched. The
deliverables remain the two PDFs in output/pdf/.
