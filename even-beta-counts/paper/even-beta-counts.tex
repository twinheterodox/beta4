\begin{abstract}
For every even integer $s\ge160$, we prove that at least
\(\left\lfloor\pi\sqrt{2/135}\,\sqrt{s/\log s}\right\rfloor\)
of the numbers $\beta(2),\beta(4),\ldots,\beta(s)$ are irrational,
where $\beta$ is the Dirichlet beta function. We also give sharper
finite endpoints for specified counts of irrational values.
\end{abstract}
