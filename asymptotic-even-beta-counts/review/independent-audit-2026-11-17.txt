The auditor reviewed all 12 pages of the full-grid paper and found no gap in Theorem 1.1
with leading constant (2/3)sqrt(kappa). The review covered the grid count,
elimination, integrality of the finite Hurwitz tails, both basis changes,
the absolute estimate, the Hankel rank argument, the opposite residues, and
the order of the limits. Exact rational examples at n=2,4 were independently
calculated. The cited inverse-totient results were treated as literature
inputs, with their statements compared with the cited texts.

The auditor also supplied a strengthening. For any contributing j/q, only
an odd common factor can cancel. The reduced denominator remains divisible
by four and has totient at most B. Reflection preserves that denominator.
The auditor proposed using only these fractions in the numerator and
arithmetic factors, and identified the retained finite-tail and transition
zeros, witness 1/q_*, bound h+1 <= B^2, and totient mass (kappa/6+o(1)) B^2.
The resulting coefficient in the final rate is kappa lambda^2/12 - 1.
The strengthened constant is 2 sqrt(kappa/3). The suggested decimal is
correct when rounded to ten places.

The auditor also identified the factor i in the valuation in Lemma 4.2,
the referent of the determinant in Proposition 4.3, and the positive
integer hypothesis on M in Corollary 6.3. Two exposition changes were
suggested, one explaining the two bases and variable size before (3.7),
the other distinguishing reflection and periodicity in Lemma 6.2.

The independent audit was of the full-grid PDF. The later revised
source and PDF incorporate the auditor's mathematical suggestion and
are covered separately in restricted-grid-correctness.txt and
restricted-grid-exposition.txt.
