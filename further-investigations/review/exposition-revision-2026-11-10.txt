Changes
  1. Defined the mixed matrix and determinant before discussing completion
     invariance. Explained the column band, the free low coefficients, endpoint
     zeros, paired rows, degree cutoffs and fixed parameter proportions.
     Distinguished canonical completion from the later arithmetic corrections.
  2. Moved the row/repair/column contribution table to the start of the decay
     proof. Connected singular values to the logarithmic determinant sum.
     Added a worked two-degree Gram minor and its general interaction factor.
  3. Corrected the closing statement about coefficient bounds: what is needed
     is uniform control of attainable determinants. Coefficient-size bounds
     are one possible route, not an arithmetic requirement.
  4. Defined the alternating sum producing the isolation forms, the source
     2-adic scalar polynomial and its coefficient matrix, the digamma function,
     and the row and column node sets. Made the formal matrix parameter and
     the rational target explicit.
  5. Rewrote the abstract around the targets, established outcomes and remaining
     correction estimate. Removed the record-count detail from the abstract.
  6. Added retained beta values to the small-set table, explained the five-value
     comparison, and defined the multipliers, signs and normalizations in the
     digamma table and rounding comparison.
  7. Moved the complete record catalog to Appendix A and consolidated the full
     independent-audit and reproduction account in Appendix B. Updated guides,
     cross-references and catalog locations.
  8. Made the zero-residue choice in the first large-prime range explicit.
     At N=p>260, this explains why every admissible arithmetic correction is
     divisible by p^2, as required by the nonvanishing proposition.
  9. Displayed the determinant-preserving completion and its three row terms.
     Distinguished the Gram matrix of the replaced rows from the original one,
     and derived 4813/4 from the three operator bounds 25, 3/2 and 24.
  10. Defined the averaged Chebyshev moments and displayed their negative-square
      contribution. Explained the elementary square inequality, the resulting
      site contributions and the passage to the positive-part integral.
      Restored the source argument's finite diagonal-error bound 4h log(...),
      replacing the earlier 2h log(...). This term vanishes on the h^2 scale,
      so the limiting estimate and all certified constants are unchanged.
      Repaired three pre-existing malformed odd-index labels in the sums.
  11. Kept the actual ratio r_n=N/n in the Pade product estimate with its
      O(log n) error. Used joint continuity of the profile to pass uniformly
      to the limiting ratio r after division by n. This removes an implicit
      convergence-rate assumption; the stated theorem and constants are unchanged.
