Mathematical scope review
  The isolation theorems retain their fixed-multiplier and least-degree
  hypotheses. Finite adaptive kernels are not promoted to all-size results.
  The Pade gap concerns the explicit proved multiplier, not every possible
  primitive normalization. The signed real saddle and rational tail are
  included on the same normalization.
  The mixed construction distinguishes canonical endpoint repair from the
  later integer correction required by the arithmetic theorem. The audited
  canonical margin is not asserted to prove irrationality.
  The rank hypotheses for the lattice reductions are linked explicitly to
  the large-prime low-moment rank bound and prime-scale nonvanishing.
  Exact determinant attainment is distinguished from a height bound for a
  correction. The polynomial-left-inverse obstruction concerns that bound,
  and the chain example does not assert an obstruction for all moment data.
  Conditional density is not used as an input to an irrationality proof.
  Small-set forms retain all rational constants. The large-prime residue
  formula includes its possible vanishing and the recorded exception.

Exposition review
  Applied mathematical-exposition.txt. The introduction explains the integer
  contradiction, complete primitive normalization and intended prerequisites.
  Each construction begins with its purpose and cost. Worked examples show
  the period match, commutator, determinant progression and row-lattice scale.
  The source of the canonical margin is exposed in separate row, repair and
  column estimates, followed by the precise estimate still missing.
  Methods are attributed beside their use. The abstract states outcomes and
  scope without a detailed argument. Subsections are numbered; the title has
  no forced line break. Audit recommendations are not repeated in the paper.
