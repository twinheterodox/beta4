Outcome
  The audit identified one missing hypothesis in Section 6.5. The canonical
  determinant bound in Theorem 5.1 survives: its decay rate is at least
  1.87063289244902359295, exceeding the arithmetic leading cost by more than
  0.00001888937116500327 on the n^2 scale. Uniform control of the required
  integer corrections remains open. No irrationality or independence
  theorem follows from this audit.

Mathematical finding
  Equation (6.9) holds for the general lattice U=U_0+Z Gamma under the
  polynomial left-inverse hypothesis. The inference a_N C_E in Z_{>0}
  additionally requires a_N P_{N,U}(0) to be integral for every correction
  in that lattice. Integrality and invertibility of Gamma do not supply
  this property. Theorem 4.2 supplies it for the scalar congruence class;
  Section 4 of R08 supplies it for the specific enlarged admissible class.
  With that hypothesis, the obstruction follows for both classes.

Exposition findings
  Section 6.2: "If zero is unattainable" is the intended hypothesis.
  "If zero is excluded" could mean discarding an attainable zero; when
  c=1, the least nonzero absolute value is delta.

  Section 6.4: define Sigma and distinguish the Gram--Schmidt covariance
  upper bounds from actual rounding covariances, which depend on the
  fractional parts. The triangular-minor comparison concerns the bounds.
