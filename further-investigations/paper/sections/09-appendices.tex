\section{Reproduction and audit scope}\label{app:audit}

The companion directory contains the \LaTeX\ source, a build command,
rational trial data, certificate checkers and the coverage manifest.
The check runner copies inputs into a fresh working directory, so
replaying a certificate does not overwrite its recorded result.
Its featured checks are the $26$ row-potential bounds, the repair
integral, the degree-$200$ column certificate and root counts, the
three nonvanishing seed polynomials, and the complete small-set forms.
Additional finite checks cover the elementary determinant reductions.

The independent mathematical audit of 8 November 2026 reported no gap
affecting the canonical estimate and its specified arithmetic and
nonvanishing inputs. Its independently written arithmetic reproduced
the positive margin and checked $878800$ moment congruences, local
corrections in $70$ prime cases, $313$ seed determinant evaluations
and $2880$ Frobenius identities. It also checked the general repair
argument, the uniform limiting passages, the main-row estimate and
the complete column critical-point certificate. A public scope summary
and the numerical outcome accompany this supplement. The audit accepted
the stated classical results, including the factorial Schur tableau
identity, Stirling's formula and the prime number theorem. It did not use
the original Catalan irrationality claim or its published decay constant.

A further independent audit of the 41-page assembly on 10 November 2026
checked the isolation and prime-dilation arguments, the real Pad\'e
integral and rate, the determinant progression and rounding arguments,
the small-set residue formula, and digamma nonvanishing. It identified
one missing hypothesis in the left-inverse obstruction: the clearing
multiplier must work throughout the correction class. The arithmetic
results cited in Section~\ref{subsec:kernel-inverse} establish this
hypothesis for both admissible classes. The canonical estimate survived
this audit. Its mixed-moment arithmetic, nonvanishing and Gram-bound
dependencies were checked against the pinned 8 November audit.

All $13$ packaged checks passed in the auditor's scratch copy. Fresh
calculations checked $240$ Pad\'e clearing cases and $18$ product
integrals, both degree-$25$ small-set tables, and the stated
prime-valuation examples. The larger digamma table was compared with
its saved enclosures; the remaining historical experiments were outside
this replay. The audit accepted the cited Kawashima--Po\"els theorems
and classical factorial Schur identity as inputs. Its scope and the
subsequent corrections are recorded separately in the companion files.

Neither audit nor finite replay supplies the uniform correction estimate
\eqref{eq:missing-correction-bound} or \eqref{eq:rounding-target}.
