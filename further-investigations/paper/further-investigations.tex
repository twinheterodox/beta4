\begin{abstract}
This supplement examines attempts to prove irrationality of $\beta(4)$,
of at least one value in a small set of even Dirichlet beta values,
and of combinations of Catalan's constant with $\pi^2$. It gives
restrictions on canceling unwanted beta coefficients by polynomial
multipliers and determines the real error rates of a Pad\'e family.
For a determinant associated with $\beta(2)+\pi^2$, a certified decay
estimate exceeds the available denominator cost. The effect of the
integer adjustments required to obtain that cost remains uncontrolled.
Further results describe the attainable determinant values and explain
why several proposed bounds for those adjustments fall short. Exact
computations identify additional denominator and cancellation costs
for small beta sets. These results do not establish a new irrationality
or linear-independence theorem.
\end{abstract}
