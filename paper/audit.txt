MAIN-MANUSCRIPT REVIEW RECORD
Date: 31 October 2026

Status and scope
----------------
The first complete main-paper draft is 14 pages, including references.
It contains proofs of the two stated primitive-value obstructions and the
common-zero-node extension. This is a review by the model that assembled
the manuscript, supported by finite exact and interval calculations. It is
not a new independent model audit, a human mathematical audit, or formal
verification. Archived cross-checks retain their original, narrower scope.

The review found no substantive gap in the assembled proofs. That is a
draft-review judgment, not a claim that independent verification has taken
place. The two theorems concern the explicit polynomial families defined
in the paper; they do not establish a general obstruction to proving the
irrationality of beta(4).

Mathematical review
-------------------
1. Primitive normalization. The multiplier is determined by the minimum
   valuation of all coefficients, after removal of coefficient content.
   Monicity in the first family and the power-of-two leading coefficient
   in the second control contributions from primes omitted from the
   lower-bound sums. The degree factor in the irrationality criterion is
   explicit; decay alone for polynomials of growing degree is insufficient.

2. Cubic-tail kernel. The Laplace and Fourier normalizations, including the
   factor 4 in the exponential, were checked. The product-resolvent measure
   is positive, has infinite total mass, and nevertheless gives finite
   entries, including the zero-node limit. The determinant comparison uses
   a nonnegative determinant integrand, not entrywise matrix comparison.

3. Cauchy comparison. The rational interpolation identity, its numerator
   degree, residue at zero, and rank-one determinant factor are explicit.
   The resulting constants are uniform in integer node positions and gaps.
   The appendix uses a regularized comparison finite at a common zero.

4. Principal arithmetic and offsets. A unique high-node principal minor
   forces the stated coefficient valuation for 2M < p < 4M, p >= 5.
   The prime-number-theorem error is uniform on that interval. The proof
   treats finite limiting offset ratios, the logarithmic corner at zero,
   and ratios tending to infinity, then handles arbitrary sequences by a
   subsequence argument. There is no unmentioned upper bound on the offset.

5. Difference moments. The log-squared integral and parity cancellation of
   the cubic beta tails give the displayed rational coefficient formula.
   The coefficient matrix has determinant 2^(n-1). The positive measure,
   elementary lower density bound, Rodrigues formula and squared norms
   supply both real determinant exponents.

6. Difference arithmetic. For p > 4n, the first nonzero cubic residue and
   the anti-triangular block give the exact primitive multiplier valuation.
   Entries outside that block are already p-integral, a stronger fact than
   their scaled residues vanishing. The complementary pencil has a p-unit
   leading coefficient. The possible negative contribution at 2 is retained
   before summing the prime cost.

7. Scope. Rational invertible changes of basis preserve the primitive
   polynomial up to sign. Other subspaces, weights, sparse designs and
   factors are not covered by the two obstruction theorems. Incomplete
   archived constructions have not been promoted to impossibility results.

Computational evidence
----------------------
The reproducible run is recorded in checks/reproduction.json. It passed:
  - 21 regularized identities and 25 principal frames;
  - 130 coefficient-prime and 9,572 entry-prime checks;
  - 144 further exact regularized Cauchy identities;
  - five interval checks of the common-zero bounds;
  - 23 difference-moment identities, 36 rational determinant evaluations,
    1,100 residue checks and 74 rank/primitive-exponent checks;
  - 13 monic Rodrigues polynomials, 78 orthogonality equalities, 13 squared
    norms and nine direct Gram-product checks, plus the primitive example.

Interval calculations used Arb at 4,096-bit precision. Exact structural
records reproduced the archived values. The runner executed scratch copies
of archived scripts. All 120 verbatim evidence snapshots remain unchanged.
These finite calculations support identities and local arithmetic; the
asymptotic theorems are proved in the manuscript, not inferred from samples.

Sources and attribution
-----------------------
The six cited sources have versioned records and local PDF hashes in
sources/reading-record.json and sources/manuscript-reading.json. The OpenAI
determinant strategy is credited as motivation. The comparisons with
Zudilin, Prevost, Krattenthaler, Carreira and Brown are contextual and do not
import an unverified global irrationality claim. The particular formulas
used in the proofs are derived in the paper. Standard background inputs,
including the prime number theorem, are stated explicitly.

Reading scope is recorded rather than equated with verification. The
specific claims' priority remains provisional; the paper makes no claim
that a literature search establishes originality.
