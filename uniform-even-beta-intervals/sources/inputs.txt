Inputs for Uniform intervals for irrational even beta values

Reflection selects the parity of the zeta values in Ball and Rivoal,
Invent. Math. 146 (2001), §1. For the alternating sum over half-integer
poles, see Rivoal and Zudilin, Math. Ann. 326 (2003), Lemma 2.
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s002220100168
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-003-0420-2
Removing the lowest zeta value by differentiation is the step in
Rivoal, Acta Arith. 103 (2002), §2, Lemma 1. Its adaptation to beta(2)
and the use of triple zeros are explained by Krisman in §1.2. His
Lemmas 4.3 and 4.6 supply the local coefficient estimate and two
complementary sums for the rational term.
  https://doi.org/10.4064/aa103-2-5
  https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00285v2

For consecutive pole blocks, factorwise denominator estimates and
shifting a sum at integer zeros, see Zudilin, Abh. Math. Semin. Univ.
Hambg. 89 (2019), §3 and the proofs of Lemmas 1, 4 and 5. Lai and
Zhou vary the block parameters and normalization in §6, Theorem 6.1.
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s12188-019-00203-w
  https://doi.org/10.5486/PMD.2022.9252

Order-31 kernel, local pole estimates, arithmetic and phase certificates,
and original seed. Sections 3–5 and Appendix A of
  twinheterodox, At least one of beta(4), beta(6), ..., beta(32) is irrational.
  https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00346v1

Fixed powers and prime dilation. Sections 2–4 of
  twinheterodox, Irrational even beta values from powered hypergeometric kernels.
  https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00336v1
The order-31 specialization and local coefficient algorithms used here
are in Sections 2–6 and the checks of
  twinheterodox, Two shorter intervals of even Dirichlet beta values (2026).
  https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00601v1
  https://github.com/twinheterodox/beta4/tree/main/beta-intervals

Earlier all-starting-points result. Theorem 1.1 and Proposition B.1 of
  twinheterodox, Irrational even beta values in shifted intervals.
  https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00160v2

Auxiliary-prime nonvanishing criterion. Lai and Sprang, Lemma 2.3.
The kernel-specific dilation is in the powered-kernel papers above.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10393v2

Passage from algebraic generating functions to coefficient recurrences.
Stanley, Differentiably finite power series, Theorems 1.5 and 2.1.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/S0195-6698(80)80051-5

Explicit prime distribution. Theorem 5.2 on p. 4, row k=2, eta=0.2, of
  P. Dusart, Estimates of some functions over primes without R.H.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.0442v1

The analytic and density estimates and Dusart's theorem enter as
literature results. The five reference tables in checks/ come from the
17 November 2026 proof records and can be rebuilt from this package.
