\begin{abstract}
Let $\beta(s)=1-3^{-s}+5^{-s}-\cdots$ denote the Dirichlet beta function.
For all sufficiently large even integers $s$, we prove
\[
 \dim_{\Q}\Span_{\Q}\{\beta(2),\beta(4),\ldots,\beta(s)\}
 \geq 0.21\sqrt{\frac{s}{\log s}}.
\]
\end{abstract}
