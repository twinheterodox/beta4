Please prioritize these questions:

1. Is the half-integer tail decomposition, including its finite polynomial
   and the system at -1, correct? Do the differentiated sums and analytic
   continuations agree for all derivative orders used in the estimates?

2. Does the local denominator proof cover every entry of the normalized
   fundamental matrix, including the constant-coordinate column? In the
   iterated integrals, are the integration denominators indeed distinct
   cumulative degrees, with depth at most d and total series cost 4^k?

3. Are the coefficient and value bounds uniform in p and the full range
   2rn+2<=k<=kappa*n? Does the separation of the limits n and a avoid any
   hidden dependence problem?

4. Does the rank construction use a single polynomial row after the
   quadratic change of variable? Check the enlarged-system identities,
   residue descent, and independence of each selected family. In
   particular, is it legitimate to count the families at both i and -i,
   and to use separate branches at 1 and -1?

5. Does Fischler's Theorem 3 in the pinned 2026 author version apply to all
   six families, including the negative orders at infinity and the
   n-dependent choices of solutions? Check the common annihilating
   operator and the uniformity when b varies between 1 and a.

6. Does the reduction to even columns preserve the required weak rank
   condition? Check the application of Proposition 1, the loss of one
   dimension on removing 1, and the final constant and interpolation.

Please report any gap with its exact location and whether it invalidates
the theorem or can be repaired without changing the conclusion. Check
the main argument independently of the finite test results. Those tests
are not an asymptotic certificate.

Separately, relevant prior results that already imply this even-parity
dimension estimate would change the paper's positioning. The comparison
currently distinguishes a dimension bound from a count of irrational
values, and excludes arguments where the odd beta values alone can supply
the claimed dimension. Priority has not been asserted.
