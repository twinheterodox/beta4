1. Exact prime deletion gives a genuine arithmetic saving

Let chi=chi_4, theta=beta(4), F(t)=sum chi(j)t^j/j^4, and let S be a
finite set of odd primes. With T_p f(t)=f(t^p), define

  G_S=product_(p in S)(1-chi(p)T_p/p^4)F,
  alpha_S=product_(p in S)(1-chi(p)/p^4).

The coefficient of t^j in G_S is exactly chi(j)/j^4 if no selected prime
divides j, and zero otherwise. Also G_S(1)=alpha_S theta. These exact
identities apply to the ORIGINAL cyclotomic germ, not automatically to
the completed polylogarithm germ of round03.

For h_j=[t^j]G_S, b_i=h_i, and T_ij=h_(i+j)-h_(i+j-1), use the endpoint
pencil J_(n,S)(X)=X det T+b^T adj(T)b. Its actual target is
J_(n,S)(alpha_S theta). Whenever the pencil is nonzero, its positive
primitive multiplier kappa satisfies

  v_p(kappa)<=4 for every p in S.

This includes the endpoint scalar alpha_S and all common numerator
content. The prime can no longer occur to an exponent proportional to
determinant order. An exact n=8,p=11 comparison gives 24 before deletion
and 4 after deletion.

Deleting y<p<=2n gives the explicit upper bound

  kappa <= P^4 D^(n+1),
  P=product_(y<p<=2n, p odd) p,
  D=product_(p<=y, p odd) p^(4 floor(log_p(2n))).

Thus log^+(kappa)=o(n^2) if pi(y_n)log n=o(n). In particular y_n=sqrt(n)
works without any prime number theorem. This is an upper bound only.

The analytic cost is real: all primitive (4P)-th roots are genuine
singularities, by applying four Euler derivatives to the divisor
expansion. The fixed two-branch contour of F cannot simply be reused.
This singularity statement is not itself a capacity or actual-value
no-go theorem.

2. A sparse family explains why the saving alone is insufficient

Keep only the prime 3 in the coefficient prefix. Specifically, put
M=3^k, n=M-1, delete every odd prime other than 3 up to B>=2n, and set

  c=(-1)^k/M^4,
  r(t)=sum_(0<=a<k) (-1)^a t^(3^a)/3^(4a),
  r=r(1).

The relevant prefix is r(t)+ct^M. An explicit [n/n] Pade pair is

  q(t)=r(t)/t-ct^(M-1),   p(t)=r(t)^2/t,
  q(t)(r(t)+ct^M)-p(t)=-c^2 t^(2M-1).

More strongly, with epsilon=(-1)^(n(n-1)/2+1),

  J_(n,S)(X)=epsilon c^(M-2)[(r-c)X-r^2].

Primitive normalization removes the entire factor c^(M-2). The actual
source is therefore the explicit Euler-product approximation

  L_(k,B)(theta)=(r-c)alpha_S theta-r^2.

With s=81/82, r=s(1-c), it decomposes as

  L=(r-c)(alpha_S theta-s)+s(s-1)c-s^2c^2,
  |alpha_S theta-s|<=B^(-3).

For the natural B=2n the actual cancellation remains unresolved; an
upper bound O(M^-3) is not a lower bound. Exact primitive logarithms at
k=1,2,3 are approximately -2.37, 14.08, 121.29, respectively.

For the deliberately larger cutoff B=p, where p=3 mod4 is prime and
p>=M^4, there IS an actual obstruction. For k>=2,

  |L|>=1/(200M^4),
  kappa_L>=p^4/R_k^2,  R_k=(M^4-(-1)^k)/82,
  |kappa_L L|> (6561/200)M^4>1.

The real lower bound prevents the Euler tail from cancelling the
explicit discrepancy. The largest selected prime p divides no Euler
numerator q^4-chi(q), so it retains its fourth layer; negative multiplier
valuations at other primes are bounded by the fixed numerator R_k^2.
This is a fully primitive lower bound, not a product of upper estimates.
Other sparse indices also give identically zero pencils, explicitly
showing why normality cannot be assumed after depletion.

Proofs and checks: arithmetic/findings.txt and euler_depletion.py/json.

3. A differentiated kernel with proved nonzero real decay

The construction worker's main family is

  R_n(u)=n!(2u+n)(u-n)_n(u+n+1)_n/(u)_(n+1)^3,
  S_n=sum_(k=n)^infinity (-1)^k R_n''(k+1/2).

Reflection cancels the beta(3) and beta(5) terms, giving explicit rational
A_n,B_n with S_n=A_n theta+B_n. No lower beta value or pi period remains.
The coefficients are written as factorial/harmonic sums in the packet.

The actual real asymptotic is proved, not fitted:

  S_n ~ (-1)^(n+1) 3sqrt(pi)n^(-5/2)(log n)^2(27/256)^n.

In particular the forms are eventually nonzero. The proof uses the
kernel -pi sec(pi u), integration by parts twice, and a contour shift
across u=n-1/2. Its remaining vertical integral is bounded by
10|R_n'(n-1)|. Exact gamma products and Stirling's formula then give
the displayed equivalence and eventual sign. A finite n=256 check
also certifies nonvanishing directly from the residue/remainder bound.

For O_m=lcm of the positive odd integers <=m, the explicit multiplier

  M_n=2^max(0,2 floor(log_2(2n))-3) O_(4n-1)^5

makes both coefficients integers. Dyadic cost is only polynomial, so
clearing all powers of 2 still leaves shrinking nonzero forms. Odd
denominators remain. For every prime p=4n-1>=7 the actual primitive
multiplier has v_p(kappa_n)=5, with an explicit nonzero residue.

The PARTICULAR sequence M_n S_n diverges: its exponential logarithmic
rate is 20+log(27/256), approximately 17.75066. This follows from a
two-sided real estimate and the prime number theorem. It does not
settle the full primitive sequence. That would require control of
gcd(M_n A_n,M_n B_n); no adequate uniform content theorem is obtained.
All tested positive-index primitive values beyond the isolated n=1
pair 1458X-1441 exceed one.

The preceding half-integer-pole Catalan variant also has a proved real
asymptotic. Its exact target-coefficient recurrence forces an exponential
dyadic cost, and its dyadic-only normalized values diverge. This is a
local normalization obstruction; negative valuations at other primes
could affect a full primitive normalization. The integer-pole change
removes that specific local obstruction, not the entire arithmetic issue.

Proofs and checks: construction/findings.txt, derivative_tail.py/json,
residue_checks.py/json; source-recursive audit in arithmetic/construction_review.txt.

4. A denser Loewner pencil and quantitative nonvanishing

Put S_j(a)=sum_(k<a)(-1)^k/(2k+1)^j. On integer nodes define

  K_aa(X)=X-S_4(a),
  K_ab(X)=(S_3(a)-S_3(b))/(6(a-b)),  a!=b.

Its determinant is monic with degree equal to the number of nodes.
It is the divided-difference matrix of cos(pi z)g_3(z), normalized
by -1/6, where g_3(z)=sum_(k>=0)(-1)^k/(2k+1+2z)^3. The old pencil
is precisely the even-node restriction. Multiplying instead by cos^3
gives an ENTIRE function with the same values and first derivatives
at the integers, hence the same matrix. This analytic pole cancellation
does not remove the arithmetic denominators or the off-axis growth.

An elementary Loewner lemma gives a useful nonvanishing mechanism.
For N ordered nodes with nonzero alternating values, any symmetric
divided-difference matrix (with arbitrary diagonal) has rank at least
ceil((N-1)/2). A row dependence would otherwise produce a rational
function of degree too small to match the alternating signs, even
after allowing common zeros. Hence among the first N full-parity nodes
there is some nonzero principal minor of order at least (N-1)/2.
No favorable value bound or identified minor follows automatically.

Exact large-prime coefficient valuations show that the actual monic
primitive multiplier Q_N satisfies log Q_N>=N^2+o(N^2). The old even
grid gives (9/2)n^2+o(n^2) from the analogous prime band. Thus the new
grid reduces this arithmetic lower bound, but finite tests show a large
loss in real decay at the same time.

Binomial row projections U_(i,k)=binom(m,k-i) were also tested, always
normalizing det(XUU^T+UK(0)U^T) by det(UU^T) to make it monic. Exact
prime laws retain lower multiplier rates (1+rho)^2 for m/n->rho<=1,
and 4 for rho>=1. These are genuine primitive coefficient lower bounds,
not actual-value no-go theorems for all projected families. All 55
nontrivial tested full-parity and projected polynomials exceed one
after primitive normalization.

Proofs and checks: fresh/findings.txt, loewner_probe.py, filter_probe.py,
checks.py, and their JSON records. The rank lemma is proved in the note;
the binomial determinant product is explicitly attributed as classical.

5. A uniform actual obstruction to overshifting the dense grid

The coordinator additionally treats N=2r consecutive full-parity nodes
m,...,m+N-1. Integral estimates for g_3,g_4 give, after parity grouping,

  m^3(-1)^m K(theta)=C+E,
  C=[[0,-D/48],[-D^T/48,0]], D_ij=1/(2i-2j-1).

The Cauchy inverse formula gives ||C^-1||_infinity<=192r^2, while
||E||_infinity<=r^2/(4m). For m>=96r^4 this proves the actual bound

  |det K(theta)|>=m^(-3N)(384r^2)^(-N)>0.

If m+N<p<2m is prime, all diagonal prefixes contain p once and its
contribution cancels from off-diagonal differences. The actual monic
primitive multiplier has v_p(kappa)=4N, and monicity ensures kappa
is an integer. Consequently, for m>=768r^4,

  |kappa det K(theta)|> (m/(384r^2))^N>=2^N.

These hypotheses hold for a prescribed prime-indexed family: choose
p>768r^4+2r+1 and m=p-2r-1. No prime-distribution theorem is needed.
This rules out that overshifting repair. It does not cover m comparable
to N, arbitrary nodes, or projected pencils.

Proof and checks: coordinator/loewner_shift.txt, loewner_shift_check.py/json;
fresh/coordinator_review.txt and exact inverse cross-checks through order24.
