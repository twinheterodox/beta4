ROUND08: FRESH STRUCTURAL TESTS AND EXACT ARCHIVE GATES
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished.

Status: no irrationality proof and no credible complete construction
whose arithmetic and analytic estimates reach one. This round clarifies
several proposed mechanisms and retains an exact local tool. It does not
establish that the proof is closer. The persistent goal remains active.

Three fresh workers explored structural, geometric and discrete mechanisms,
with archive comparisons and cross-reviews. Root reviewed retained
arguments and independently reproduced selected identities. These are
model mathematical checks, not formal verification or a human audit.
No novelty claim or publication is made.

1. A local arithmetic tool retained

The positive csch functional for beta(4) is prior work of Carreira,
already recorded in sources/carreira/beta4_determinants.tex Section10.
For the Euler functional E(x^d)=E_d(0), put tau=-E o D^3/12. Its
analytic norm at every odd prime is at most1, because its monomial
values are dyadic. Extending tau_X to simple half-integer poles gives

 mu_X(R)=tau_X[x^4 R(-x^2)],
 tau_X(g)=p^(-3)sum_(a=0)^(p-1)(-1)^a tau_Y^ext(g(a+px)),
 Y=chi_(-4)(p)p^4 X+C_p,   C_p in p^4 Z_p.

The proof treats polynomial quotients and principal parts together.
C_p is p-adic; it is not identified with a real L-value. See
collaboration/round-08/geometry/euler_distribution.txt and the
adversarial independent review. Exact and finite p-adic checks passed.

This identity is not a favorable denominator/decay estimate. It leaves
actual primitive polynomials of existing designs unchanged. Carreira's
tested positive csch designs have large primitive values. His finite
tests do not exclude every design, but a sharper sufficient clearing
bound alone cannot change already computed values.

Direct Fauzan and Lai PDFs were added and read selectively. Describing
them initially as a fresh lead overlooked their explicit discussion
and audits in Carreira's archived source. This was corrected before a
new determinant scan. The source-reading log records what was read.

2. Structural routes tested without general impossibility claims

The beta4 tail moments admit an E-function formulation: rationality of
theta would choose a rational initial value that cancels an exponential
growth term. Explicit rational countermodels have the same cancellation,
positive compact support and an exterior density pole. These features
alone cannot supply the required rigidity theorem. The specific
logarithmic beta4 density is not excluded by a general result here.

The square-torus Epstein value is E(i,4)=4 zeta(4)theta. Four Maass
raising operators give a holomorphic CM Eisenstein period. However a
nonzero local function with the same Laplace eigenvalue and fixed-point
symmetry lies in their kernel. The differentiated CM value does not by
itself recover theta; an arithmetic reconstruction theorem is missing.

An independent monodromy check found central shears preserving the
local monodromy matrices while changing the highest polylogarithm
coordinate. They do not preserve the boundary normalization. Also,
theta rational does not make theta/(2pi i)^4 a rational coordinate.
The proposed monodromy-to-irrationality implication does not follow.

These conclusions concern the stated arguments. They do not rule out
E-functions, spectral methods, monodromy or period geometry in general.

3. Discrete routes and rational controls

The rational-tail recurrence generates rational states for every rational
initial value; denominators do not become bounded. Ordinary real
convergence does not identify a real Euler product with its p-adic limits.
A nested positive Euler fraction reduces exactly to ordinary beta4
partial sums, including after primitive normalization.

Rational-limit controls preserve positive integer partial coefficients,
the same degree pattern, square numerators, and coprimality through any
prescribed finite index window. Those features cannot establish descent.
The exact repeated fourth powers of the target remain outside the controls;
no theorem exploiting that distinction was found.

See round-08/discrete/findings.txt. Adversarial checked the controls;
root independently rebuilt nested fractions and five window constructions.

4. Ladder integral: a real bound but exact old-family identification

A proposed dimensional deformation has

 J_n=integral_[0,1]^4 A^n/Dcube^(n+1),
 A=t(1-t)r(1-r)x(1-x)y(1-y),
 Dcube=r x(1-x)+(1-r)y(1-y)+tr(1-r)(x^2+y^2).

An elementary bound proves0<J_n<=6theta*64^(-n), with endpoints
controlled by an integrable majorant. This appeared to be an untested
source-parameter ray. Checking the angular derivative indices exposed
the error: the source lambda equals2h-1, not h. The exact factor is

                 J_n=(n!)^4 4^(-n) L_(n+1,n+1),

where L is the previously tested balanced ladder family. Independent
quadrature checks the first two nontrivial cases. Rational scaling cannot
improve primitive arithmetic. The earlier balanced-family tests apply.

A separate residue calculation extends convergence of the old S_b series:

 S_b(m)~(-1)^(b-1)binom(6b-2,3b-1)m^(-(6b-1)).

The unweighted angular range extends to b<2h<6b. In particular h=2b-1
converges, but corresponds to a different dimension and is not J_n above.
Lower periods remain. The sole prescribed cofactor block b2..5 gives

 1360645139563699526527482 theta-1365344717261304522665065,

whose Arb-certified value is about-1.9742*10^22. This is one finite
test, not an asymptotic no-go. No larger scan was performed.
