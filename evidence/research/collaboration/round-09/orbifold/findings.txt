ROUND09: EXPLICIT ORBIFOLD GAMMA DATA AND THE ARITHMETIC GATE
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Assessment
----------
An explicit compact toric orbifold supplies quarter-Gamma connection data,
an integral K-lattice, a scalar differential operator, and integer regularized
period coefficients. The obvious prescribed rational extraction of beta4
nevertheless fails: its actual primitive integer errors grow exponentially.
This is a theorem about the one sequence below, not a theorem against
orbifold quantum cohomology, Gamma classes or geometric irrationality methods.

The additional geometric ingredient was the concrete orbifold and its
integral structure. The extraction itself reduces to the old finite-Gamma
arithmetic, including all nonlinear normalization costs. No novelty claim.
The separate deformation.txt tests a nonconstant toric deformation instead
of extending this failed sequence into a larger no-go project.

1. Space, twisted sectors, differential operator and lattice
----------------------------------------------------------
Use the weighted projective stack

 X=P(1,3,4,4,4,4,4),   dim_C X=6,   h=c1(O_X(1)).

Write w=(1,3,4,4,4,4,4), S=sum w=24. The inertia components with stabilizer
elements exp(2pi i/4) and exp(6pi i/4) are P(4,4,4,4,4), of dimension4.
Their rational ordinary cohomology has h^5=0. With the convention that the
stabilizer acts on O(w) by exp(2pi i wf), the Euler sequence and Iritani's
definition (0903.1463v3, formula23) give

 Gamma_X|_(f=1/4) = Gamma(3/4+h) Gamma(1/4+3h) Gamma(1+4h)^5,
 Gamma_X|_(f=3/4) = Gamma(1/4+h) Gamma(3/4+3h) Gamma(1+4h)^5.

The labels exchange if the inverse-sector convention is used; this is why
the later scalar Frobenius conventions are given explicitly. The constant
factor on each sector is Gamma(1/4)Gamma(3/4)=pi sqrt2, so this example avoids
an unknown individual Gamma(1/4) normalization. It does not avoid the Tate
and grading factors in Iritani's actual lattice framing.

The algebraic K-group has the concrete presentation

 K_0(X)=Z[L,L^(-1)]/((1-L)(1-L^3)(1-L^4)^5),

where replacing L=O(1) by O(-1) gives the same presentation up to a unit.
This follows from equivariant localization for the complement of the origin
in the weight representation of G_m. The relation has unit constant and
leading coefficients, so 1,L,...,L^23 give a free integral basis. Its Euler
pairing is unimodular: the line-bundle exceptional basis has upper-triangular
Euler matrix with diagonal1; the needed negative-degree vanishings are the
weighted projective cohomology vanishings for degrees between -23 and -1.
Iritani's Gamma framing maps this lattice to flat sections. Sector projection
alone is not an integral operation and is not being used to claim integral
Taylor coefficients.

At z=1 and q=Q exp(t), Coates--Corti--Lee--Tseng Corollary1.8 gives

 [ P(D)-q ] J=0,   D=q d/dq,
 P(D)=D (3D)(3D-1)(3D-2) [(4D)(4D-1)(4D-2)(4D-3)]^5.       (1)

This has order24. Its exponents at q=0 are 0 with multiplicity7, 1/3 and
2/3 once each, and 1/4,1/2,3/4 with multiplicity5 each. Iritani0903,
Theorems4.11 and4.14, identify the K-lattice with the toric mirror thimble
lattice under their hypotheses. The weighted-projective mirror theorem is
proved in the primary source above; no general Gamma conjecture is assumed.

2. Regularization and the dimension that is lost
-----------------------------------------------
The regularized holomorphic period has integer coefficients

 c_n=(24n)!/[n!(3n)!(4n)!^5].                              (2)

It is also CT(W_0^(24n)), where

 W_0=y+x1+...+x5+1/[y^3 (x1...x5)^4].                     (3)

This independently proves integrality by the multinomial theorem. The
nonzero constant terms of W_0^N occur only at N divisible by24.

Put M=24^24/(3^3 4^20), z=Mq. The hypergeometric numerator parameters are
 initially k/24, 1<=k<=24. The denominator parameters are k/w_i,
1<=k<=w_i, with multiplicities. Cancel the six common parameters
1/4,1/3,1/2,2/3,3/4,1. The irreducible scalar operator becomes

 L=D^6(D-1/4)^4(D-1/2)^4(D-3/4)^4
       -z product_(1<=k<=23, k notin {6,8,12,16,18})(D+k/24). (4)

It has order18 and only the singular points0,1,infinity. Its conifold
exponent at1 is2; possible resonance does not add a new finite singularity.
Its quarter exponents now have multiplicity4, not5. On the fourfold cover
z=u^4, the finite singularity lifts to all four fourth roots of unity.
Thus a single canceled dominant connection term does not by itself create
an exponentially larger disk of convergence for all the required germs.

The geometric reason for the missing degree is explicit. X is Gorenstein
(every weight divides24), and a general anticanonical hypersurface Y has
dimension5. Its quarter-sector fixed locus is a degree6 hypersurface in
P^4, of dimension3, so h^4 vanishes there. The fourth quarter-Frobenius
coefficient belongs to an extension, for example (D-rho)L, not the
homogeneous rank18 local system. Iritani1101 Theorem6.9 on the ambient
integral VHS does not automatically give the arithmetic of this extension.

The initially tested P(1,4^5) has rank21 and integer regularized coefficients
(21n)!/[n!(4n)!^5], but is not Gorenstein: every degree21 polynomial is
divisible by the weight1 coordinate. Its anticanonical hypersurface is
reducible. It is therefore not an instance of the stated Batyrev theorem.
The change to X above fixes this concrete geometric defect.

3. Exact connection extraction, including lower products
--------------------------------------------------------
Define the normalized coefficient function, for rational x near a quarter,

 b_n(x)=Gamma(1+24(n+x))/Gamma(1+24x)
        product_i Gamma(1+w_i x)/Gamma(1+w_i(n+x)).           (5)

For each fixed n it is a rational function of x with rational coefficients.
Multiplication formulas reduce the reciprocal hypergeometric Gamma quotient
in Bloch--Vlasenko Proposition26 to a nonzero constant times

 M^x F(x),    F(x)=Gamma(1+x)Gamma(1+3x)Gamma(1+4x)^5
                         /Gamma(1+24x).                    (6)

Equivalently, uniformly near rho,
 b_n(rho+s)/b_n(rho) -> M^s F(rho+s)/F(rho).
This also follows directly from Stirling and does not require the general
connection theorem. At rho=1/4 and3/4, F(rho) is a rational multiple of
pi sqrt2. The factor M^s contributes only to the first logarithmic
coefficient and hence disappears from the fourth cumulant below.

Set

 C(x)=1/4 [ sum_i w_i^4 zeta(4,1+w_i x)
                         -24^4 zeta(4,1+24x) ].             (7)

Then C(x)=[s^4]log F(x+s). By the Hurwitz shifts and the difference between
the two quarter values,

 C(1/4)-C(3/4)=C0-5120 theta,
 C0=1357014445571814521897312639/271692096890942768040000,
 theta=beta(4).                                             (8)

This is an exact identity. The coefficient -5120 is
(1-3^4)*256/4. All ordinary zeta4 terms cancel between the two sectors.
The rational shifts giving C0 are reproduced without numerical fitting.

Taking a logarithm is essential. If a normalized series is
1+a1 s+a2 s^2+a3 s^3+a4 s^4+..., its fourth logarithmic coefficient is
a4-a1 a3-a2^2/2+a1^2 a2-a1^4/4. Thus the G^2 and other decomposable
contributions have been removed explicitly, rather than omitted. This is
nonlinear extraction from connection data. An integral lattice for the
original linear differential system alone gives no denominator bound for it.

4. Prescribed rational sequence and actual primitive obstruction
----------------------------------------------------------------
Let S_m(a)=sum_(j=0)^(m-1)(a+j)^(-4), and set

 T_n(rho)=1/4 [sum_i w_i^4 S_(w_i n)(1+w_i rho)
                         -24^4 S_(24n)(1+24rho)],
 r_n=[C0-T_n(1/4)+T_n(3/4)]/5120.                           (9)

T_n is exactly [s^4]log(b_n(rho+s)/b_n(rho)). Thus r_n is defined a priori
and rational. Write r_n=P_n/Q_n in lowest terms, Q_n>0. All clearing and
nonlinear normalization costs are included in this actual primitive pair.

The signed error has the exact identity

 r_n-theta=[C(n+1/4)-C(n+3/4)]/5120
            ~ -3/(10240 n^5).                              (10)

It is strictly negative for every n>=0. To prove the sign, substitute the
Hurwitz integral into (7), scaling the integration variable separately in
each term:

 C(x)=1/24 int_0^infty u^3 exp(-xu)
       [sum_i 1/(exp(u/w_i)-1)-1/(exp(u/24)-1)] du.

For fixed u>0, h(a)/a=1/[a(exp(u/a)-1)] is strictly increasing in a>0.
Since every w_i<24 and sum_i w_i=24, the bracket is strictly negative.
Differentiation shows C'(x)>0, proving (10)'s sign. The usual Hurwitz
asymptotic gives C(x)=-3/(4x^4)+O(x^(-5)); differentiating the convergent
Laplace expansion gives the stated leading difference. This is a proof,
not merely the numerical error pattern.

There is also a short actual denominator proof. Let p>18 be prime and

                  6n+5 < p <= 12n+3.

Exclude p if it divides any of 24n+7,...,24n+18. Then v_p(Q_n)=4.
Indeed, in the w=3 part of (9), exactly one denominator 4j+7 or4j+13
equals p; it occurs with coefficient +/-81/80 after all normalizations.
No other odd multiple of p occurs, and p exceeds every w=1 denominator.
The w=4 difference telescopes to denominators4n+2,4n+3 and fixed terms,
all smaller than p. The w=24 difference telescopes to the twelve excluded
endpoints and fixed terms. C0's denominator has no primes above18. Thus
the unique p^(-4) contribution cannot cancel. Each endpoint has at most one
prime in this interval for n sufficiently large, so there are at most12
exceptions. The prime number theorem now gives

 log Q_n >= 4[sum_(6n+5<p<=12n+3)log p -12log(24n+18)]
          =24n+o(n).

Together with (10), this proves |Q_n theta-P_n| -> infinity. This is not
an expensive sufficient clearing bound: it detects primes in the actual
reduced denominator. It closes only the explicit extraction (9).

For scale, the small exact checks at n=1,2,4,8,16 give denominator bit
lengths151,208,383,584,1213. Their log primitive errors are approximately
93.7,131.0,249.2,385.7,818.1. No large scan was undertaken.

5. What remains missing
-----------------------
We have a geometric realization and exact beta4 extraction, but no useful
primitive approximants. The pure beta4 extraction uses an extended system
and a fourth cumulant; neither integral K-monodromy nor the homogeneous
Calabi-Yau lattice supplies its arithmetic. The tested coefficient limit
has only polynomial decay and an actual exponential denominator obstruction.

A different geometric mechanism would need a calibrated beta4 extension
and an integral sequence with favorable error after primitive reduction.
The deformation in deformation.txt genuinely changes the finite critical
values and has an exact integral coefficient formula; its beta4 calibration
is still a missing theorem, before any claim about its arithmetic margin.
