ROUND09: NONCONSTANT TORIC BOX DEFORMATION
29 October 2026. Local/unpublished. A construction gate, not a proof route.

This is a different geometric step from extracting the old Gamma filter:
change the mirror Laurent polynomial by an actual age1 box monomial.
It changes the critical-value configuration and gives integral coefficients.
It does not yet identify a pure beta4 connection coefficient.

For X=P(1,3,4^5), take the fan vertices e_y,e_1,...,e_5 and
v_0=(-3,-4,-4,-4,-4,-4). The quarter-sector box point is
(v_0+3e_y)/4=(0,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1), with age1. Its monomial is
U=(x1...x5)^(-1). Thus consider

 W_lambda=y+x1+...+x5+y^(-3)(x1...x5)^(-4)+lambda U.          (1)

This is not a constant shift of W. It is among the allowed monomials of
the reflexive fan polytope. The extra toric parameter therefore belongs to
the extended mirror construction discussed in Iritani0903 and1101.

1. Exact integer sequence

The only nonzero constant terms of W_lambda^N have N=6n. Writing a for
the exponent of the old inverse monomial and b for that of U, the exponent
conditions force b=n-4a, the y exponent3a, and each x_i exponent n. Hence

 c_n(lambda)=CT(W_lambda^(6n))
   =(6n)!/(n!)^5 sum_(a=0)^floor(n/4)
                    lambda^(n-4a)/[a!(3a)!(n-4a)!].         (2)

These are integers for integer lambda. An independent formula is

 c_n(lambda)=(6n)!/(n!)^6
       sum_a binom(n,4a)binom(4a,a)lambda^(n-4a).            (3)

The inner sum is a binomial transform of the algebraic sequence which is
binom(4a,a) at index4a and0 otherwise. Thus this deformation is a Hadamard
product of the central multinomial sequence with an explicit algebraic
sequence. It is not evidence of an unexamined generic Picard-Fuchs system.

2. Exact critical values

At a torus critical point all x_i=x. Write p=y x^5. The y derivative gives
p^4=3, and the x derivatives give x^6=lambda+4p/3. The critical value is
W_lambda=6x. Put kappa=4/3^(3/4). For the generating function
sum c_n(lambda)t^n, the four candidate finite singularities from torus
critical points have reciprocals

          6^6(lambda+kappa zeta),   zeta in {1,i,-1,-i}.     (4)

For nonnegative real lambda these are distinct except at degenerations.
At lambda=0 their absolute values coincide; the first nonconstant deformation
separates the positive critical value from the two complex ones. But its
dominant/next growth ratio is only

 (lambda+kappa)/sqrt(lambda^2+kappa^2) <= sqrt2,              (5)

with equality at lambda=kappa. At integer lambda=2 the ratio is about1.41.
The absolute next growth is 6^6 sqrt(lambda^2+kappa^2), much larger than1.
Consequently canceling just the largest coefficient-growth term is not a
small integer form by itself, even if the companion coefficients were
integral. This is an analytic warning, not a primitive-value impossibility
theorem: no suitable beta4 companion sequence has been supplied yet.

3. More geometric freedom and the precise missing gate

The remaining order4 box monomials give the larger family

 W=y+sum x_i+y^(-3)X^(-4)+lambda X^(-1)
                   +mu y^(-1)X^(-2)+nu y^(-2)X^(-3), X=prod x_i.

At critical points x_i=x and p=yx^5, its reduction is

 W=5x+x^(-5) f(p),
 f(p)=p+lambda+mu/p+nu/p^2+1/p^3,
 p^4-mu p^2-2nu p-3=0, x^6=f(p), W_crit=6x.                (6)

The global monomial change p=yX gives exactly
 W=sum_i x_i+f(p)/X,
not just its critical-point restriction. It preserves the torus constant
term and Haar form. The integral coefficient sequence is therefore
(6n)!/(n!)^6 CT_p f(p)^n. This follows by multinomial expansion, so all three
parameters can be treated without invoking a numerical differential-equation
fit. No parameter scan was launched: calibration must come first.

The central Gamma framing is topological, but fixing nonzero box parameters
and extracting a new one-variable period does not automatically preserve
the normalized quarter-Frobenius coefficient or isolate beta4. In particular,
the fourth cumulant from findings.txt already lives beyond the homogeneous
anticanonical period system. What is missing is an explicit extended period,
cycle and boundary normalization for (1) or(6) whose relevant connection
coefficient is rational*beta4 plus controlled rational terms. Only after
that identity is proved would denominator/nonvanishing estimates have a
defined object to estimate.

This supplies a concrete possible repair mechanism and a decisive gate;
it supplies neither that gate nor a credible integrality/convergence margin.
It should not be advertised as a proof route until the calibration is done.
The Kerr involution/strong-temperedness hypotheses were not established for
this family, and cannot be inferred from its reflexive polytope.
