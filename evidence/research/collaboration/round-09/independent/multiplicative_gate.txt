Round09 independent multiplicative gate
29 October 2026. Local/unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Outcome. Keeping fourth-power Dirichlet denominators under convolution is
real, but the basic gap construction has only exponential decay in its
polynomial degree. A divisibility-matrix refinement gives explicit rational
approximants with a rank1 determinant reduction. The tested families do not
improve the arithmetic balance. This packet is closed without a general
obstruction to multiplicative constructions, signed bases or determinants.

1. Polynomial degree is a genuine cost

Write chi=chi_4, theta=beta(4). For P in Z[X], deg(P)=D, expand P(beta(s))
as an absolutely convergent Dirichlet series for Re(s)>1. Its coefficients
are integers. At n=3^e the coefficient is the coefficient of z^e in
P(1/(1+z)). If all coefficients e=0,...,D vanish, P(1/(1+z)) has a zero of
order>D at0, and hence P=0. Equivalently, multiply by(1+z)^D and inspect
the degree-D numerator. Thus no nonzero P of degreeD kills all Dirichlet
coefficients through3^D. Killing e=0,...,D-1 forces P=c(X-1)^D.

The primitive choice is (X-1)^D. Its first possible integer is3^D and its
actual absolute value is(1-theta)^D. Under theta=u/v in lowest terms,
v^D |P(theta)|=|v-u|^D. A fixed exponential root smaller than1 does not
overcome arbitrary v. The convolution denominator exponent4 therefore
does not by itself prove irrationality. No restriction on arbitrary
coupled Dirichlet series or determinant constructions follows.

2. Exact divisibility-matrix construction

Let D be a finite set of distinct positive odd integers containing1,
g(n)=chi(n)/n^4 and e the coordinate vector at1. Put

  K_(d,f)=g(lcm(d,f)).

Absolute convergence and complete multiplicativity give

  theta K_(d,f)=sum_(n>=1) chi(n) 1_(d|n)1_(f|n)/n^4.

Thus theta K-ee^T is the same signed moment matrix with the n=1 atom
removed. No positivity assertion is justified. If K is nonsingular, set
c_D=e^T K^-1 e. The matrix determinant lemma gives exactly

  det(XK-ee^T)=X^(r-1) det(K)(X-c_D), r=|D|.                (1)

One must discard the rational scalar and account for the entire common
content. If c_D=b/a in lowest terms with a>0, the only nontrivial primitive
factor is aX-b. The powers X^(r-1) do not supply an independent shrinking
quantity; theta>0, and the intended irrationality test is the linear one.
No determinant sign or nonzero value is inferred from nonsingularity of K.

For factor-closed D, put F(n)=chi(n)n^4=1/g(n), and
  h(n)=sum_(d|n)mu(n/d)F(d).
If E_(d,k)=1_(k|d), then

  K=diag(g(d)) E diag(h(d)) E^T diag(g(d)).                (2)

Indeed F(gcd(d,f))=sum_(k|d,k|f)h(k), while
g(d)g(f)F(gcd(d,f))=g(lcm(d,f)). The inverse incidence matrix has entries
mu(d/k) when k|d. Since diag(g)^-1 e=e, (2) yields

  c_D=sum_(d in D) mu(d)^2/h(d).                           (3)

All h(d) are nonzero: for p^a,
h(p^a)=chi(p)^(a-1)p^(4a-4)(chi(p)p^4-1). For squarefree d,
h(d)=product_(p|d)(chi(p)p^4-1). On the complete divisor basis of a
squarefree M, (3) becomes

  c_D=product_(p|M)(1-chi(p)/p^4)^-1.                     (4)

So that case is exactly the old Euler approximation, after primitive
reduction. For the different basis D={odd d<=N}, (3) is the partial sum
of the absolutely convergent squarefree expansion

  theta=sum_(d odd squarefree) 1/product_(p|d)(chi(p)p^4-1). (5)

Equation(5) follows from its Euler product. Since
product_(p odd)(1-1/p^4)^-1<2, the absolute summand is<2/d^4;
the tail atN is therefore<2/(3N^3). This upper bound alone neither proves
nonzero errors nor shows that all errors are merely polynomial-sized.

For arbitrary nonsingular K with c_D!=0, define lambda=K^-1 e/c_D and
W(n)=sum_(d in D,d|n)lambda_d. Then lambda_1=1 and exactly

  theta-c_D=c_D sum_(n>=2) chi(n)W(n)^2/n^4.              (6)

This is a signed error identity, not a positive-measure criterion.

3. Finite decisive checks

divisibility_checks.py prescribes every basis before evaluating theta.
It verifies1920 exact incidence entries,24 Schur complements,57 numerical-
argument exact determinant identities,4 Boolean product identities, and
102 prime-power coefficient identities. Arb at768 bits certifies signs
for28 prescribed forms. No parameter is fitted to theta's digits.

The only records with primitive magnitude below1 are two descriptions of
the same form82theta-81. Representative cutoff results:

    maximum odd N     digits in primitive A     log10|A theta+B|
          5                     5                     1.07345
         25                    26                    19.05270
         99                   100                    90.86193
        399                   411                   400.18814

Five incomplete interval bases and six Boolean-rank truncations also give
primitive values greater than1. These are finite tests, not a growth
theorem. General signed nonvanishing and useful primitive estimates remain
unproved. Enlarging the same cutoff scan is not justified by these results.

Exact route and missing ingredient: prescribe D_N (or a materially new
coupled extension) for which the reduced denominators of c_D, together
with the signed formula(6), give nonzero a_N theta-b_N tending to0.
The complete Boolean implementation cannot be claimed as a new route
because(4) is exact overlap. No plausible family satisfying the needed
primitive estimate emerged from this gate.
