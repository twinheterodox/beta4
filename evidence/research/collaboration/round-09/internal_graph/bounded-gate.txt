BOUNDED NONUNIFORM-GRAPH PERIOD AND ARITHMETIC GATE
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

1. Scope

After the exact n1 reduction in first-period.txt, the coordinator
authorized only n2,n3,n4 and a first period elimination. This note
records that bounded gate. No index or parameter optimization was run.

All four periods have the same specified decomposition in
1,G=beta2,B=beta4,log2,zeta3. This does not assert those numbers are
linearly independent. The first cofactor projection to1,B has a very
large primitive value, so the immediate construction is unfavorable.
This is not an asymptotic obstruction to this entire graph family.

2. Exact harmonic formula used

For the ray b=2n+1,q=n put a=n+1,lambda=4n+1,delta=3n. The graph
dimension is2lambda+2 and the central edge is its Green kernel.
The radial Gegenbauer coefficient is

 d_k=(a)_k/(lambda)_k,
 p_j=(k+a)_j(-delta)_j/[(k+lambda+1)_j j!], 0<=j<=delta,

 f_k(r)=d_k sum_j p_j r^(k-2a+2j),       r<1,
       =d_k sum_j p_j r^(-k-4a-2j),      r>1.

It follows directly from the same Gegenbauer connection formula as
the n1 case. The terminating polynomial is nonnegative on[0,1] by
Euler's integral with positive parameters k+a anddelta+1.

Set
 L_j=2(k+lambda+1-a+j), M_l=2(k+2a+l-1),
 T_jl=2(k+lambda+2-2a+j+l),
 V_jl=2(k+4a-lambda-2+j+l).

The radial integral is
 I_k=2d_k^2 sum_(j,l) p_j p_l
          [1/(L_jT_jl)+1/(L_jM_l)+1/(M_lV_jl)].

Hence the exact cube period is

 J_n=(n!)^4(4n)! *4lambda^2/Gamma(lambda+1)^2
            sum_(k>=0) C_k^lambda(0) I_k/(k+lambda)^2.     (3)

The factor4lambda^2 multiplies I_k, which already includes2d_k^2.
At n0 the
resulting scalar summand is6/(k+1)^4 and J0=6B.

Only even k=2m survive, and
 C_(2m)^lambda(0)=(-1)^m(lambda)_m/m!.
The code builds the resulting rational R_n(k) exactly. For n1..4
its numerator/denominator degrees are(8,20),(16,36),(24,52),(32,68).
After removal of the polynomial C_(2m)^lambda(0)/(-1)^m, the radial
coefficient times C_k^lambda(1) isO(k^(-4n-3)). This is summable.
The positivity and absolute-interchange argument in first-period.txt
therefore applies to each of these four cases. No term at a logarithmic
radial exponent is omitted.

5. Equivalence and interpretation

The completed conformal graph has three distinct indices on the n1
case:2 on finite outer edges,3 on infinity edges,5 on the central edge.
The uniform DIS graph has at most two. Therefore ordinary conformal
relabeling or a graph automorphism cannot identify them directly. A
direct unique-vertex star-triangle move is unavailable on the finite
three-valent graph because its incident index sum is9, not12.
These observations do not classify more complicated graph identities.

The n1 test R(m-5)/S5(m) is nonpolynomial, ruling out one especially
simple old fixed-b source identification. General linear combinations,
cohomological reductions and broader integration-by-parts equivalences
are not excluded. They would need their actual rational coefficient cost
tracked; membership in a common five-period space alone is not a new
primitive mechanism.

The useful outcome is the finite harmonic reduction of this internal
deformation and its actual first arithmetic data. The result gives no
reason to run a larger cofactor scan automatically. A continuation would
need a specific source symmetry or arithmetic cancellation that changes
the cost of removing the three lower periods. No such saving is proved.
