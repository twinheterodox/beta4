Theorem. For every fixed integer n>=0, the prescribed positive cube
integral J_n has a specified rational decomposition in

          1, beta(2), beta(4), log(2), zeta(3).

The theorem does not assert independence of those five numbers,
estimate coefficient denominators, or give nonvanishing after a signed
combination removes three auxiliary periods.

Put lambda=4n+1, delta=3n and a=n+1. The spherical radial coefficient is
d_(n,k) P_(n,k)(r^2), where

 d_(n,k)=(a)_k/(lambda)_k,
 P_(n,k)(s)=2F1(k+a,-delta;k+lambda+1;s).

Let A_j(k) denote the coefficient of s^j in dP. Its exact cancellation
of Pochhammer factors is

 A_j(k)=[(4n)!/n!] (-1)^j binom(3n,j)
    /[prod_(q=n+j+1)^(4n)(k+q)
      prod_(q=4n+2)^(4n+j+1)(k+q)].

All its poles are simple, and the angular pole k=-lambda is absent.
Empty products have value1. Define

 L_j=2(k+3n+1+j), M_l=2(k+2n+1+l),
 T_jl=2(k+2n+1+j+l), V_jl=2(k+1+j+l),

 I_n(k)=2 sum_(j,l=0)^(3n) A_j(k) A_l(k)
          [1/(L_jT_jl)+1/(L_jM_l)+1/(M_lV_jl)],
 C_n(k)=prod_(r=1)^(4n)(k+2r)/[2^(4n)(4n)!],
 R_n(k)=4(n!)^4 C_n(k) I_n(k)/[(4n)!(k+lambda)^2].

The exact representation is

               J_n=sum_(ell>=0)(-1)^ell R_n(2ell).

Absolute interchange does not require a guessed degree cancellation.
Euler's integral gives P_(n,k)(s)=E[(1-sT)^(3n)] with
T~Beta(k+n+1,3n+1). For fixed n>=1 and k>=1,

 P_(n,k)(s)<=C(n)[(1-s)^(3n)+k^(-3n)].

After log radial coordinates r=exp(x),s=exp(y), the exponential factor
in the full radial integral is

 exp[n(x+y)-(k+n+1)(|x|+|y|)-(k+4n+1)|x-y|].

The endpoint bound and d_(n,k)=O_n(k^(-3n)) therefore give
I_n(k)=O_n(k^(-12n-2)). The absolute angular majorant is
C_k^lambda(1)=O_n(k^(8n+1)), with an additional(k+lambda)^(-2).
Thus the integrated absolute terms areO_n(k^(-4n-3)), a summable bound.
The n0 case is direct. All estimates are for fixed n; they are not
uniform estimates when the arithmetic construction lets n grow.

The poles of R_n occur only at integer k=-q with1<=q<=8n+1 and
have order at most4. Away from the angular pole, A_jA_l supplies at
most2 orders and the radial fractions at most2. At the angular pole
A_jA_l is regular, so the squared angular factor plus radial factors
again gives at most4. R_n is proper.

For k'=-2lambda-k and j'=3n-j, the coefficient formula gives
A_j(k')=A_j'(k). The radial factors obey L<->-M and T<->-V under
complementing j,l. Swapping the two summation indices then proves
I_n(k')=I_n(k). C_n and the squared angular denominator are invariant,
so

                    R_n(-2lambda-k)=R_n(k).

Consequently the partial-fraction coefficients satisfy
c_(2lambda-q,s)=(-1)^s c_(q,s). On odd q the beta-tail shift signs
agree across each pair, so odd beta orders cancel. On even q the eta
shift signs are opposite, so even eta orders cancel. The central pole
q=lambda is odd and has only even orders. With maximum order4, the
remaining terms are beta2,beta4,eta1,eta3 and rational finite tails.
Since eta1=log2 and eta3=3zeta3/4, the theorem follows.

There is no sign discrepancy between using A_j=d*p here and using
A_j/(k+lambda) in the coordinator's reciprocal-product computation.
The latter coefficient has a minus sign under reflection; the pair of
coefficients in each summand cancels it.
