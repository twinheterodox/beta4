binomial summand. Independent/audit.txt supplies the complete rational

Explicitly, the forward coefficient is

                    binom(15n,n+k)binom(15n,k),

               -(B^alpha)'(n,k) H_(4n+k)^(r-1)(a)/(r-1)], r=3,4.

The derivative is of the full Gamma interpolation. It is not a derivative

The dual rational kernel is

  [(s+alpha)_(11n+1)(s+n+alpha)_(11n+1)(s+2n+alpha)_(8n+2)].

3. Boundary transfer and formal coefficients

For the forward sum, the factorial continuation has the same terminating
endpoints k=0 and k=14n+43 as the source certificate. The shifted positive
Gamma factors are finite at those boundaries. Hence the forward scalar
certificate sums to the recurrence for Q_n.

Use the UNcentered rational kernel

 H_n^alpha(s)=(-1)^n (15n)!^2/(10n)!^3
   *prod_(j=12,13,14)(s-jn-alpha)_(10n)
   /[(s)_(14n+1)(s-n)_(16n+1)].

Its partial fractions are B/(s+k)^2-B'/(s+k). Its derivative tails
are sampled at s=m+alpha, m>4n. This shift is essential. For n>=2,
all four shifted tails n,...,n+3 can be started at m=12n: omitted first
and second derivatives lie inside the common triple-zero interval.
The transferred certificate has lower value O(t^3) at s=12n+alpha+t.
Its denominator there is proved nonzero by the exact polynomial test.
At infinity the certificate is constant+O(1/s), so neither its first
nor its second derivative contributes. Absolute locally uniform tail
convergence justifies differentiation. The two exceptional recurrence
indices n=0,1 are checked exactly for both numerators. The first three
values are checked against the matrix readouts.

For the dual, write its Gamma summand at the pole coordinate k=-s-alpha.
Gamma reflection gives a 1-periodic multiplier times R_n^alpha(s).
The three rebuilt row certificates therefore give rational identities

 (-s)^j R_(n+1)^alpha(s)-sum_i Q_alpha(n)_(ij)(-s)^i R_n^alpha(s)
                        =V_j(n,s+1)-V_j(n,s).

For n>=1, all finite poles of V_j lie in -alpha+Z. The extra positive
integer factors in its denominator cancel against the numerator zeros
of R_n^alpha(s-1). Translation preserves the total Laurent coefficients
of each pole order. Thus A and B transport SEPARATELY by Q_alpha.

For the derivative tails, n and n+1 can both start at integer m=16n.
The omitted terms lie inside the common double-zero interval. The lower
certificate derivative vanishes; its denominator is checked nonzero.
At infinity its derivative is O(s^-2). Subtracting the already established
A,B recurrences proves the D recurrence, without numerical period
independence. The exceptional n=0 step checks all nine row coordinates
exactly, retaining the initial simple pole.

This proves the separate coefficient transport and the forward readout
identification needed for (1), for every n>=0 at both quarters.
