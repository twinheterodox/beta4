BETA(4): A COMPLETE FORWARD/DUAL TRANSFER, WITH AN ARITHMETIC SHORTFALL
30 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Assessment

The missing exact pairing from round 12 has been repaired. There are now
two complete quarter-argument families whose forward sums and dual minors
give IDENTICAL rational coefficients for every index. Their coefficient
recurrences, initial data, boundary terms and changing normalization have
been checked explicitly. The direct divisibility transfer produces actual
primewise improvements, but it does not supply a viable beta4 proof.

The obstruction found is quantitative and scoped to the stated arithmetic
estimates. Isolating beta4 multiplies the two quarter denominators. Even
after ignoring every numerator-related cost, the transferred estimates
can certify at most 88.20753... in odd-prime content density, whereas the
established analytic estimate would need 142.86203.... The shortfall is
54.65450... per index. These are NOT bounds on the actual primitive gcd,
and do not establish an impossibility theorem for this family or method.

1. What changed from the failed transfer

The original source is Leibtag et al., New Directions in Irrationality,
arXiv 2610.27799v1, Section 6.2. Its forward and dual presentations expose
different divisibility of the same coefficients. Round 12 changed only
the harmonic sampling and produced unequal numerators.

Here the pole grid, both parameter trajectories, initial states, rational
readouts and factorial normalization are deformed together. Integer zero
blocks in the dual remain fixed. In the forward kernel, its zero blocks
and its sampling grid move together. Thus the telescoping boundary terms
continue to vanish rather than becoming an omitted correction.

For alpha=-3/4,-1/4 and a=1+alpha, the construction gives simultaneous
approximants P3_n/Q_n and P4_n/Q_n to zeta(3,a),zeta(4,a). Its period
support is exactly the displayed span of 1,zeta(3,a),zeta(4,a); no
independence assumption is used to identify the coefficients.

The forward denominator is the positive rational binomial sum

 Q_n=sum_(k=0)^(14n)
     binom(k+12n+alpha,10n)binom(k+13n+alpha,10n)
     binom(k+14n+alpha,10n)binom(15n,n+k)binom(15n,k).

The numerator formulas include the complete Gamma-interpolation
derivatives, including both simple-zero tails. The dual is a 3x3 minor
of explicit Laurent coefficient rows. The complete identities and their
new Pochhammer normalization are in coordinator/complete-deformation.txt.

2. What is proved about the identities

An Euler-adjoint concomitant gives a rational pairing K(n,alpha), with
the exact endpoint and initial cross products. A separate model check
rederived these identities for symbolic n,alpha.

For BOTH quarter specializations, exact rational-function calculations
verify the matrix intertwining, companions, regularity and nondegenerate
initial data. Ten rebuilt polynomial telescoping certificates prove
the all-index forward readouts and separate dual coefficient-row
recurrences. The largest certificate has bidegree(553,114). These are
exact symbolic substitutions; the size is not a measure of mathematical
novelty. The generic-alpha algebra and the two certified all-index
specializations are distinguished throughout.

3. Analytic scale and projection

Both quarter systems have exactly the source's limiting matrix. The
shifted explicit state obeys the same exponential growth exclusion that
identifies the recessive mode. The endpoint projection remains nonzero.
Consequently the established relative approximation rate is

 g=112.80753924745468...,
 log Q_n/n -> sigma=65.45153656586513... .

Let superscripts a,b denote the arguments 1/4,3/4. The exact beta4 form is

 W_n=Q_n^a Q_n^b beta4
       -(P4_n^a Q_n^b-P4_n^b Q_n^a)/256.

It follows from zeta(4,1/4)-zeta(4,3/4)=256beta4, with every auxiliary
period eliminated. The rational error has upper exponential rate
2sigma-g=18.09553... before integer normalization. This is an upper
bound, not a proved optimal rate or signed asymptotic for W_n.

The separate quarter approximations do not share a denominator up to
an established inexpensive factor. Treating them as if they did would
remove an actual elimination cost.

4. Arithmetic advantage, and why it is insufficient

Both presentations now legitimately supply bounds on the SAME rational
coefficients. They are combined by taking the stronger bound for each
prime and each coefficient, retaining Lambda and the final projection.
All 442 finite quarter/prime diagnostics passed. Four exhibit a genuine
dual improvement over the forward bound; for example at n=2,a=3/4,p=19,
the q bound improves 0->1 and the P4 bound improves -4->-3.
All four gains survive the final beta4 projection: its common coefficient
valuation bound improves -4->-3 at the corresponding primes.

The all-index large-prime estimates and a sufficient global integer
normalization are proved in coordinator/arithmetic-gate.txt. A separate
optimistic test discards all P4 costs, uses only selected q summands and
dual poles, and allows separate best-per-prime choices. Thus it gives
the transferred certificate more saving than it can actually attain.

The dyadic certificate cost is 180log2 per index for the product. An exact
854-cell floor calculation bounds its possible odd-prime q saving by
88.2075294973..., including the limiting tail. The independent checker
used a smaller 720-cell grid and obtained the same exact rational bound.
Fixed-neighborhood and uniform tail bounds justify the PNT passage. The saving required by
the established decay estimate is 142.8620263851.... This large shortfall
does not warrant a full numerator-density optimization or a ray scan.

This ceiling is for the SPECIFIED source-style termwise estimates.
It does not bound cancellation inside q sums or determinants, actual
common numerator content, or gains from another identity. Nor does it
exclude an additional exponential cancellation in the projected error.
Any of those would be an extra ingredient not supplied by this transfer.

6. Source and check status

The original joint source mechanism was audited before transfer. All
three relevant source notebooks were read and rerun in a private copy,
including their five telescoping certificates, 181 prime diagnostics,
980 offset chambers, 19,600 integration cells and spectral checks.
Missing source modules were acquired at the already pinned revision.
Sources, versions, hashes and exact reading/verification scope are in
coordinator/source-audit.txt and coordinator/sources.json. No Lean build or claim
about every theorem in the source paper is made.

One independent model checker reviewed the initial periods, generic
adjoint pairing, shifted boundaries and finite identities using a separate
implementation. Its first report is independent/audit.txt. Its completed
second report, independent/final-audit.txt, independently reconstructs
all ten certificate equations, exceptional initial steps, the shifted
valuation phases, exact floor integrals and spectral constants. It also
reviews the written boundary, dyadic and PNT/tail arguments. Both reports
found no correction needed in their stated scopes.

The checker did not rerun the 442 prime diagnostics or the five primitive
Arb calculations; those are main-agent computational checks. These are
independent model cross-checks, not human review or formal verification.

7. Recommendation

Retain the complete paired construction as a repair of a genuine gap in
our earlier attempt. Do not continue this ray by routine parameter scans
or fine tuning of the existing valuation bounds. A credible reopening
would require a cheaper character projection, a new uniform content
theorem, or demonstrably faster projected decay. None is presently
established. Return this bounded phase for review rather than launching
another campaign. No priority claim is made for the identities.
