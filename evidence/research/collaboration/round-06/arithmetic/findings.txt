Prescribed Hermite sources and the arithmetic cost of their coefficient height
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished.

There is no irrationality proof. The new prescribed construction is a
two-endpoint rational Hermite family, with no coefficient selected by
evaluating theta=beta(4). Its rational endpoint contribution is dyadic for
one denominator choice and integral for another. Its exact primitive
arithmetic is given by two augmented determinants. Uniform control of
their common content and of the source height is still missing.

The first independent construction, a binomial polynomial with fourfold
Mellin zeros, does not force the desired fourth-prime cancellation. An
infinite prime subsequence retains the full fourth power after primitive
normalization. This is a theorem about its arithmetic, not a theorem that
its actual values cannot shrink.

The Hermite pivot has been specified and checked exactly at eleven small,
prescribed indices. Ten have a nonzero theta coefficient; all ten primitive
values exceed one. The remaining circular n=1 form has A=0 and primitive
value one. These finite facts do not prove asymptotic failure. In particular,
neither a lower bound on the source coefficient A nor a large available
absolute upper bound is an actual-value no-go theorem.


1. Independent choices and the archive distinction

00_initial_choices.txt was recorded before reading any round06 work or
exchanging proposals. It lists four concrete mechanisms: a confluent
multiple-orthogonal polynomial, a prescribed Hermite divisibility module,
a differentiated double-pole kernel, and cumulative fourth-pole residues.
The first was selected. Its failure to cancel the intended prime powers
led to the recorded Hermite pivot, rather than a larger parameter scan.

Archive searches found no earlier copy of the particular confluent
polynomial below. They did find the free polynomial E^3F subspace in
round03/arithmetic and the rational E^4 identity in round03/fresh.
Consequently those identities and the local fourth-power divisibility
lemma are explicitly reused here; they are not claimed as new findings.

The difference in the present rational construction is the prescription
of zeros at BOTH 0 and 1. The old round03/fresh family prescribed only
Taylor osculation at 0, and reduced its particular repeated-pole choices
to positive scalar polynomial forms. That exact reduction does not
automatically apply to the present two-endpoint interpolation conditions.
No assertion that the broader rational architecture is new is intended.


2. A binomial construction with fourfold moment cancellation

For n>=1 put

 P_n(x)=sum_(j=0)^(4n) (-1)^j binom(4n,j)binom(n+j,n)^4 x^j.

Its coefficients are integers. Its exact Mellin transform is

 integral_0^1 P_n(x)x^(t-1) dx
   = (4n)!/(n!)^4 * product_(k=1)^n(t-k)^4
                         / product_(j=0)^(4n)(t+j).             (2.1)

This holds for Re(t)>0 and then as a rational identity. To prove it,
take the residues of the right side at t=-j. They are exactly the
coefficients of P_n. Both sides tend to zero as t tends to infinity,
so the partial fractions agree. Differentiation at t=1,...,n gives

 integral_0^1 P_n(x)x^k(log x)^r dx=0,
                  0<=k<n, 0<=r<4.                            (2.2)

Thus the fourth powers in the binomial numerator have a real cancellation
purpose as well as an arithmetic one.

Write P_n(i)=a_n+i b_n and N_n=a_n^2+b_n^2. Then

 Q_n(x)=(a_n-b_n x)P_n(x)=N_n+(1+x^2)V_n(x),
                         V_n in Z[x].                         (2.3)

The prescribed theta-only form is

 I_n=(1/6)integral_0^1 Q_n(x)(-log x)^3/(1+x^2) dx
    =N_n theta+sum_j [x^j]V_n/(j+1)^4.                         (2.4)

No decimal approximation to theta occurs in these definitions. Although
N_n is a sum of squares, no uniform statement about N_n!=0 is needed for
the prime subsequence below.


3. The quotient loses the intended fourth-power cancellation

Theorem. Let p be any prime congruent to 3 modulo 4, and n=(p+1)/4.
For the formal rational pair in (2.4), the actual primitive multiplier
kappa has v_p(kappa)=4.

Here, for any nonzero rational pair (A,B), kappa is the positive rational
number such that (kappa A,kappa B) is its primitive integer pair.

Proof. Since 4n=p+1, the elementary Lucas binomial congruence gives

 P_n(x) == (1-x^p)(1-r x) mod p,
                         r=(5/4)^4 in F_p.                    (3.1)

Indeed only the four indices j=0,1,p,p+1 survive modulo p. Their second
binomial factors reduce to 1,n+1,1,n+1 respectively. The fourth power
then gives r, and the signs are as displayed.

Work in F_p[i], where i^2=-1. As p=3 mod4, this is a field and i^p=-i.
Equation (3.1) gives

 a_n+i b_n=(1+i)(1-r i),
             N_n=2(1+r^2) != 0 mod p.                         (3.2)

The last inequality uses that -1 is not a square modulo p.
For any Q with Q(i)=N, the coefficient of x^(p-1) in (Q-N)/(1+x^2)
is the alternating sum of the coefficients of Q above that index.
In (3.1) the upper part of Q is -x^p(a_n-b_n x)(1-rx). Its contribution
to this alternating sum is minus the imaginary part of
(a_n-b_n i)(1-r i). Since

 (1-r i)=(a_n+i b_n)/(1+i),

that imaginary part is -N_n/2. Therefore

                  [x^(p-1)]V_n == N_n/2 != 0 mod p.           (3.3)

Now deg V_n<=4n-1=p, so the rational sum in (2.4) has exactly one term
whose denominator is divisible by p: the term with j=p-1. Its valuation
is exactly -4. N_n is a p-unit. Consequently primitive normalization
must supply exactly p^4. QED.

There are infinitely many primes 3 mod4, so this is an infinite failure
of the proposed forced-cancellation mechanism. It neither estimates
I_n(theta) from below nor rules out every use of this moment family.


4. Why merely imposing an integral E^3F polynomial does not suffice

Let E=x*d/dx and F(0)=0. For polynomial F,

 Q=A+(1+x^2)E^3F/x

has the exact form

 (1/6)integral Q(-log x)^3/(1+x^2) dx
          =A theta+integral_0^1 F(x)dx/x.                     (4.1)

If F is integral, the last expression needs only one coefficient-LCM
layer. But every rational polynomial V is E^3F/x for a unique rational
F with F(0)=0: its coefficient f_(j+1) is v_j/(j+1)^3. Thus this condition
does not change the rational source space.

In particular, if additional homogeneous orthogonality conditions have
already selected a one-dimensional rational source space, requiring F
to be integral can only rescale that same ray. A global rational scalar
does not change the primitive endpoint pair. This is a scoped obstruction
to regarding a fully prescribed polynomial orthogonality ray as an
arithmetic improvement merely because it is rewritten using E^3F.


5. The prescribed two-endpoint rational Hermite construction

Fix one of the two integer polynomials

 D_c(x)=1+x^2,             D_u(x)=1+x-x^2=1+x(1-x).             (5.1)

Both are positive on [0,1] and have D(0)=1. The second also has D(1)=1.
For each n, seek A and R with

 deg R<=2n, R(0)=0, G=R/D^n,
 f=A/(1+x^2)+E^4G/x,                                      (5.2)

such that f has zeros of order at least n at both 0 and 1. These are
linear homogeneous conditions involving only rational functions with
integer coefficients. They do not involve theta or a fitted target.

Here is the complete deterministic prescription. Set

 C_0=D^(n+4),
 C_j=(1+x^2)D^(n+4)E^4(x^j/D^n)/x,       1<=j<=2n.          (5.3)

These are integer polynomials. Their integrality follows recursively:
if the current rational function is U/D^k, applying E replaces its
numerator by x(D U'-k D' U) and increments k. After four steps the
numerator is divisible by x.

Let M_n be the 2n by (2n+1) integer matrix whose first n rows are the
coefficients [x^k]C_j, 0<=k<n, and whose next n rows are the coefficients
[z^k]C_j(1+z), 0<=k<n. At a full-row-rank index take the signed maximal
minors of M_n, divide by their gcd, and choose the sign with A positive
(or first nonzero coordinate positive if A=0). The resulting vector is

                         (A,R_1,...,R_(2n)).                 (5.4)

For completeness the family can be prescribed at a rank-defective
index too: compute the canonical rational RREF nullspace basis in
increasing free-column order, take the first basis vector with nonzero
A if one exists, otherwise the first basis vector, clear denominators
and divide its coordinate gcd, and use the same sign convention.
This fallback was not used in any of the archived finite checks.

Dimension ensures a nonzero source vector always exists. The rational
source map itself is injective. Indeed E^4G/x=(E^3G)' has zero residues
at every finite pole, whereas A/(1+x^2) has nonzero residues at +/-i
if A!=0. Thus an identically zero f forces A=0. Then E^4G=0 forces a
rational G to be constant; G(0)=0 forces G=0. In particular a nonzero
selected vector gives a nonzero rational source f.

This does NOT prove that its endpoint form is nonzero, or that A is
nonzero. Those are separate questions. At circular n=1 the construction
gives A=0, G=x^2/(1+x^2), and endpoint form 1/2; its primitive form is 1.

Writing S=sum_j v_j C_j, the endpoint conditions give

 S=x^n(1-x)^n P_n(x),
 f=x^n(1-x)^n P_n(x)/[(1+x^2)D(x)^(n+4)],  deg P_n<=8.        (5.5)

To see the degree bound, G is bounded at infinity and E^4G=O(1/x),
so E^4G/x=O(1/x^2). Multiplication by (1+x^2)D^(n+4) gives degree at
most 2n+8, as does the A term. In the circular case every C_j has a
factor 1+x^2. Cancelling it gives the sharper expression

 f=x^n(1-x)^n P_n(x)/(1+x^2)^(n+4),        deg P_n<=6.         (5.6)

Thus the increasing number of endpoint conditions leaves a numerator
of fixed degree, rather than an unspecified polynomial chosen afterward.


6. Exact period cancellation and actual primitive arithmetic

Four integrations by parts, using G(0)=0 and regularity on [0,1], give

 (1/6)integral_0^1 f(x)(-log x)^3 dx
                    =A theta+G(1)=A theta+R(1)/D(1)^n.       (6.1)

The identity is already proved in round03/fresh, Section 2. One direct
check substitutes x=exp(-z): the E^4 term becomes the fourth derivative
of G(exp(-z)); integration against z^3 leaves its endpoint value.

The pair in (6.1) has no auxiliary period. For the circular source its
rational coefficient is dyadic. For D_u its pair (A,R(1)) is already
integral. This is exact, but is not an invariant improvement by itself:
the integer source vector can be very large. Its selection cost must
be kept when comparing primitive values.

There is a simple exact determinantal content formula. At a full-rank
index let c_j be the signed maximal minors and Delta=gcd(c_j). Put

 u=(1,0,...,0), w=(0,1,...,1), d=D(1)^n,
 a=det([M_n;u]), b=det([M_n;w]).

Expansion in the last row gives a=c_0 and b=sum_(j>0)c_j. Consequently

 g = gcd(d A,R(1)) = gcd(d a,b)/Delta,
 primitive pair = (d A/g, R(1)/g),
                       kappa=d/g.                           (6.2)

These are actual primitive formulas, not sufficient entrywise clearing.
For D_u, d=1 and kappa=1/g<=1. For D_c, d=2^n and kappa=2^n/g.
The formula retains both the common content Delta of the source minors
and the additional content of their two endpoint combinations.

If both endpoint coefficients vanish at some untested index, its formal
pair is zero and has no useful primitive normalization; no claim excludes
that possibility. The script rejects such a case rather than calling it
a small nonzero form.


7. The remaining source-height cost and a precise real target

The local fourth-power integrality lemma from round03/fresh still applies.
Since D(0)=1 and R is integral, the Taylor coefficients of G are integral.
The zero of order n at 0 in (5.2) gives, for odd 1<=j<=n,

                 j^4 [x^j]G=-A*(-1)^((j-1)/2).

Therefore

             lcm(1,3,...,largest odd<=n)^4 divides A.          (7.1)

This proves that deleting odd endpoint denominators can move substantial
arithmetic size into the target coefficient. It is a source-level height
statement, not a bound on actual primitive values: g in (6.2) can remove
common factors, and real cancellations can be stronger than an absolute
estimate. No conclusion of asymptotic failure follows from (7.1).

There is an explicit, uniform sufficient real budget. Let H_n be the sum
of the absolute coefficients of the fixed-degree P_n in (5.5), or its
cancelled version (5.6). On [0,1],

 max x(1-x)/(1+x^2)=(sqrt(2)-1)/2=:rho_c,
 max x(1-x)/(1+x-x^2)=1/5=:rho_u.

The remaining denominator factors are at least one. Since
(1/6)integral_0^1(-log x)^3 dx=1, (6.1) gives

        |primitive form at theta| <= kappa H_n rho_D^n.      (7.2)

Thus a fully usable theorem for this particular construction would be
uniform nonvanishing together with kappa H_n rho_D^n -> 0, or a stronger
signed estimate that replaces (7.2). At full rank kappa is given exactly
by (6.2). Neither the required content/height bound nor the required
nonvanishing theorem has been proved. A large right side in (7.2) is not
a lower bound for the actual form.

The surviving new question is sharply defined: can the two-endpoint
Hermite constraints give controlled primitive source height H_n/g, or a
signed real estimate strong enough to compensate for its growth? The
eleven small cases are not evidence for an affirmative answer. They do
not settle it in the negative either.


8. Exact finite checks and reproduction

From beta4/ run

 .venv/bin/python collaboration/round-06/arithmetic/hermite_sources.py

The script writes only hermite_sources.json in this directory. It uses
integer and rational FLINT/Python arithmetic for all source construction,
cofactors, common content, polynomial division, Mellin identities, and
prime valuations. Arb at 1200 bits evaluates already-prescribed pairs;
it never chooses their coefficients.

The binomial checks use n=1,2,3,5,6,8,11, with p=3,7,11,19,23,31,43.
They verify all 4n Mellin zeros, three rational instances of (2.1), the
full Lucas polynomial congruence (3.1), and actual v_p(kappa)=4.

The Hermite checks are:

 denominator       n       kappa        log |primitive form(theta)|
 1+x^2             1       2            0 (A=0; primitive form 1)
 1+x^2             2       4            5.5163297977...
 1+x^2             3       4/3          8.7255841101...
 1+x^2             4       2           18.5936361072...
 1+x^2             6       2           33.4596324887...
 1+x^2             8       2           48.7281801134...
 1+x-x^2           1       1            6.7045606269...
 1+x-x^2           2       1           14.6615156378...
 1+x-x^2           3       1/3         24.4307198889...
 1+x-x^2           4       1           33.2337728169...
 1+x-x^2           6       1           55.2588518183...

All eleven matrices have full row rank. The exact saved data contain the
primitive source vector, both augmented determinants, their content,
the primitive endpoint pair, P_n, and the absolute bound (7.2). Source
Bernstein coefficients are also saved when they give a finite sign
certificate. Their finite signs are not extrapolated.

For example the circular n=2 primitive endpoint pair is (568,-313),
whereas the endpoint-unit n=1 pair is (1346,-515). Every tested pair
with nonzero theta coefficient has absolute value above one. No larger
scan was used to seek a reversal.


9. Scope and reading record

All new files are in this arithmetic directory. No older packet was
edited. The initial-choice record precedes collaboration on round06.
Relevant archive reading consisted of the round03 rational-Euler note,
the recorded free Rodrigues construction, initial ideas from round04,
and targeted searches of new-avenues.txt and rounds01--05. No external
literature was consulted for the elementary arguments in this note.

A brief exchange with the fresh worker supplied an abstract triangular
filter observation for their separate Gram measure: a leading-residue
sequence with a first nonzero term cannot be annihilated throughout a
prefix by a low-degree p-primitive multiplier, provided all moments used
remain p^3-integral. Their degree shift can reach a fourth layer, so that
hypothesis must not be dropped. This observation is not part of the
Hermite theorem and is not used for any claim here.

The positive result of the pivot is an explicit target-independent
two-endpoint source family, fixed-degree residual numerators, and exact
primitive formulas with endpoint denominators supported only at 2 or
at no primes at all. The unresolved cost is the size and common content
of the coefficients required to obtain those sources. This packet does
not establish an arithmetic gain sufficient for irrationality.
