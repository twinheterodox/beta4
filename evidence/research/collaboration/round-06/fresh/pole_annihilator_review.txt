1. Orbit classification and the rational trace invariant

The reviewed input that t is a degree-one coordinate on the Fricke
quotient implies the full orbit dichotomy, not merely one direction:
t(tau)=t(p^2 tau) iff p^2 tau=gamma tau or gamma W32 tau for some
gamma in Gamma0(32). The index-two extension is a group because W32
normalizes Gamma0(32).

For a real Mobius matrix M with positive determinant Delta and an
interior fixed point tau, its derivative rho satisfies

  rho+rho^(-1)=(tr M)^2/Delta-2.                            (1.1)

This follows algebraically from the fixed-point quadratic, and does not
require a choice of an angle or a complex conjugation heuristic.
Our separate symbolic script proves the generic identity before any
specialization to the coordinator's two orbit cases.

For the ordinary case, M=gamma^(-1) diag(p^2,1) has determinant p^2
and trace a+dp^2. For the Fricke case it may be represented by

  M=[[-cp^2,a/32],[-dp^2,b]],

with determinant p^2/32 and trace b-p^2c. Differentiating t invariance
at the orbit equality gives rho=delta_2/delta_1 in both cases. Therefore

  R+R^(-1)=h^2/p^2-2,   h=a+dp^2,
  R+R^(-1)=32r^2/p^2-2, r=p^2c-b,

exactly as stated. The interior fixed-point condition gives
|h|<2p and |r|<p/sqrt(8). Because a,d are odd, h is even. The h=0
case would require (pd)^2=-1 mod 32 and is impossible.

At an unbranched zero, these identities give

  B_c=delta_1^2[(1+R)^2-(c/p^2)R]=0

for c=h^2 or c=32r^2. The Fricke expression is independent of d;
therefore the factor cancels the entire shifted family, not only the
representative with d=1. The two positive label sets are disjoint.

2. The refined ordinary trace set is necessary AND sufficient

Write h=2j. From ad=1 mod 32, odd d, and odd p, one has

  a= d^(-1)=d mod 8,       p^2=1 mod 8,
  a-dp^2=0 mod 8.

Hence

  h^2-4p^2=(a-dp^2)^2+4p^2(ad-1)=0 mod 64,

which is equivalent to j^2=p^2 mod 16. Thus impossible labels can be
removed exactly; this is not a heuristic density reduction.

For completeness, every j with 1<=j<p and this congruence is realized.
The number j^2-p^2 is either 0 or 16 modulo 32. Choose k with
k^2=j^2-p^2 mod 32 (take k=0 or 4), and choose the odd residue

  d=p^(-2)(j+k) mod 32.

Then (p^2d-j)^2=j^2-p^2 mod 32, so
(p^2d^2-2jd+1)=0 mod 32. Set

  a=2j-dp^2,   c=1,   b=(ad-1)/32.

This is an integral Gamma0(32) matrix with h=2j. Its fixed-point
quadratic has discriminant 4(j^2-p^2)<0, so it has an upper-half-plane
solution. Therefore the refined set loses no ordinary family. Its
ordinary factor count is p/4+O(1), and the total count is
(1/4+1/sqrt(8))p+O(1).

The independent checker compares congruence eligibility with direct
realizability over all odd d modulo 32, for every odd p from 3 to 101
and every 1<=j<p: 2,550 exact checks. This finite check is supplementary;
the argument above proves the assertion in full.

3. Integrality and local multiplicities

The coefficient identity delta_2=p^2 v with v integral is exact. Thus

  B_c=(delta_1+p^2v)^2-c delta_1 v

has integer q-coefficients and leading term q^2. The product W_p has
leading coefficient one and exact order 2b_p. It is not a common scalar
rescaling, and the initial vanishing coefficients supply no witnesses.

The branch-point argument is valid. Gamma0(32) has no elliptic elements:
trace zero would give a^2=-1 mod 32; trace +/-1 would give
 a^2+/-a+1=0 mod 2. Both are impossible. A finite stabilizer in the
index-two Fricke extension therefore has order at most two. Since t
is a coordinate on the quotient, its ramification order e is precisely
1 or 2 at an interior point.

At a denominator zero choose h in the Fricke extension with
h(tau0)=p^2 tau0, and set g=h^(-1) composed with the dilation. Invariance
makes the denominator exactly t(tau)-t(g(tau)) locally. If g'(tau0)=1,
g is the identity (an interior fixed-point automorphism is conjugate to
a rotation), putting the dilation in the group. This contradicts the
q-leading terms t(q)=q+... and t(q^(p^2))=q^(p^2)+.... If e=2 and
g'(tau0)=-1, uniqueness of the real Mobius half-turn makes g the old
order-two stabilizer; the same contradiction follows. Thus

  ord_(tau0)(t(tau)-t(p^2 tau))=e<=2.

If e=2, both derivatives vanish because p^2 tau0 is in the same orbit,
and every B_c vanishes to order at least two. If e=1 the relevant factor
already vanishes and the denominator is simple. These observations cover
the cases excluded from the derivative ratio calculation.

4. Remaining fixed points and the character cancellation

For r=0 the matrix gamma_new=(a c;N c d), N=32p^2, has determinant one
and fixes tau after W_N. Conversely, if an element gamma_new W_N has an
interior fixed point, its normalized trace is sqrt(N)(b-c). The bound
on an elliptic trace forces b=c, giving r=0. Thus the description as the
new Fricke fixed-point orbit is exact.

At an unbranched r=0 point, R=-1, and
B_c=(c/p^2)delta_1^2 is nonzero for every retained positive label. These
points are in fact always unbranched for the old quotient. Writing

  tau=c/d+i/(sqrt(32)p d),    ad=1+32p^2c^2, d>0,

an old Fricke fixed-point representation would force d_old=pd and
c_old=pc. Its determinant condition fails modulo p. This independently
proves the simple-denominator claim without numerical derivatives.

The weight-one character is essential and is correctly included. Both
Theta_1=theta3(2tau)^2 and Theta_8=theta3(16tau)^2 transform by chi_4(d)
on Gamma0(32). Applying that law to gamma W32 gives

  J(p^2 tau)=-i sqrt(32) chi_4(d)(d tau-c)J(tau)
           =chi_4(d)J(tau)/p.

Thus J_p=(1+chi_4(d))J, and d=3 mod 4 really cancels the pole through
J_p^3. This is an exact cancellation, not a small numerical value.
For d=1 mod 4, nonvanishing of the residual period is an additional
condition and cannot be inferred from the pure Fricke endpoint identity.

All six stated examples have d=1 mod 4. Our independently implemented
Lambert sums, with 5,000 terms and explicit absolute tails, enclose all
six H_p-C_p(theta) residuals away from zero. Independently evaluated
theta sums at p^2 tau prove J_p!=0. The eta quotient t is zero-free in
the upper half-plane; together with the branch argument this gives
T=2t!=0 and D'!=0. No numerical fit to a residue is being assumed.

5. Weight and its removal by a zero-free integral unit

Before normalization, W_p has weight 4b_p, and the differentiated
Fricke invariance of t gives

  delta_1(W_N tau)=N tau^2 delta_2(tau),
  delta_2(W_N tau)=N tau^2 delta_1(tau).

Therefore W_p(W_N tau)=N^(2b_p)tau^(4b_p)W_p(tau). The warning that an
old weight-zero residue phase formula cannot be reused was necessary.

The new normalization fully repairs that weight issue. The Jacobi
product proves that Theta_a=theta3(2a tau)^2 has no zeros in H. It is
an integer unit series. The theta transformation gives

  Theta_a(W_N tau)=-i N tau/(2a) Theta_(N/(4a))(tau).

For a=1 and a=8p^2, the partners are exchanged, and the two factors
multiply to -N tau^2. Thus

  F_p=Theta_1 Theta_(8p^2),
  F_p(W_N tau)=-N tau^2 F_p(tau).

Both theta factors have character chi_4; their product has trivial
character on Gamma0(N). So F_p is weight two on that group, and

  U_p=W_p/F_p^(2b_p)

has weight zero and is Fricke invariant. The exponent 2b_p correctly
kills both the weight and the negative Fricke sign. The reciprocal of
an integer series with constant term one is integral at every order,
while zero-freeness ensures holomorphy of the reciprocal in H. Hence
U_p is integral, introduces no interior pole, and has exactly the same
interior zero divisor as W_p.

The normalized remainder retains all canceled and surviving pole claims.
This normalization is a convolution of coefficient pairs, so its height
and primitive content still need a separate estimate. Integrality by
itself establishes neither favorable height nor small primitive values.

6. Audit of the interval tails and independent code

The coordinator's reused Lambert routine has a safe tail: after the
last included odd d, bound d^-4 by one and sum the remaining r^d over
odd d; use |1+q^d|>=1-r^d and |1+q^(2d)|>=1-r^(2d). Its derivative
bounds have sufficient factors of two. The eta routine bounds omitted
logarithms and logarithmic derivatives by geometric tails and then
converts the logarithm error through exp(error)-1. The theta tail uses
the first omitted square and the minimum subsequent square gap. These
are valid bounds for the input radii, including the examples near the
unit circle.

The coordinator's direct wide theta interval at q can serve only as a
consistency check. Its actual nonvanishing test uses the small transformed
q and the exact character identity; that is valid and is what the proof
states. Equality of two interval enclosures containing zero is not itself
an identity proof; the orbit and character arguments supply that proof.

7. Scope of the accepted conclusion

A genuine interior pole gives a Taylor-series convergence radius below
one, hence a raw coefficient limsup obstruction. It does not prove growth
at every index, nor control primitive pair rescaling, nor give a uniform
obstruction when p grows with the index. The coordinator's final note
keeps those distinctions. The integral orbit-wise multiplier and the
weight-zero unit normalization are positive constructions, while the
remaining fixed-point periods are a concrete unresolved analytic problem.
