INDEPENDENT COORDINATOR REVIEW OF THE ROUND06 DIFFERENCE GRAM
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished; model cross-check, not formal verification.

Reviewed fresh/findings.txt and gram_checks.py after their creation.
The claimed positive full-rank construction and the scoped primitive
growth obstruction are mathematically consistent.

1. Period coefficient. The coefficient of u^m v^m in (u-v)^(2m) is
(-1)^m binom(2m,m). Multiplying by the diagonal J coefficient (-1)^m
gives the positive central binomial coefficient. Thus its Hankel pencil
really has full degree n, with leading determinant 2^(n-1), rather than
rank one. The monic Chebyshev basis used to evaluate that determinant
is integral and unit triangular.

2. Positive measure. The two halves of the square, and the Jacobian
dy=2s ds, give exactly the factor 1/(96s) in the density. Restricting
v to [(1-s)/4,(1-s)/2] gives rho >=(1-s)/(3072s), which indeed implies
rho >=(1-s^2)/6144 since 2>=s(1+s). This verifies the slightly weakened
rational density bound. The shifted Jacobi norm product gives the
displayed lower bound, including the factor 2^(2n^2+n). Both real
estimates therefore have the same n^2 rate, -log(4).

3. Prime onset. For p>4n all T_4 terms and all j-m denominators are
p-integral. Exactly the T_3 terms with j>=(p+1)/2 can contain p, only
to its third power. At m0=floor(p/4)+1 the one- or two-term first
residue is nonzero. Entries outside the final r-by-r block, with
r=max(0,2n-1-m0), are actually p-integral. The final block is reverse
triangular with nonzero anti-diagonal. It has determinant a p-unit
after multiplication entrywise by p^3.

4. Primitive exponent. Selecting that whole block leaves the upper-left
theta pencil. The coefficient of X^(n-r) in the reduced maximal-pole
part is the product of two p-units. It follows that some actual
coefficient has valuation -3r; the bound is not merely an estimate
on a chosen clearing denominator. The leading coefficient 2^(n-1)
also bounds all possible negative valuations of kappa to the single
prime2, with magnitude at most n-1. Therefore the prime range
(4n,8n) supplies the asserted global lower rate 6n^2.

5. Combining this true primitive lower bound with the true positive
value lower bound is legitimate: log Z_n(theta)>=(6-log4)n^2+o(n^2).
This closes this principal Gram sequence, not all determinants or
alternative frames. The text makes that limitation clear.

6. The optional polynomial-filter observation includes the necessary
degree restriction d<=m0-2. It ensures m0+d<p/2 so fourth-power
denominators in shifted T_4 moments cannot undermine the triangular
third-layer reduction. Its stronger statement with p-unit actual
leading coefficient uses only the first equation, whose largest moment
index is m0, and is sound in the stated range.

No substantive correction was needed. The exact script was rerun by
the coordinator; the separate research-worker implementation and this
mathematical review are model checks, not a human audit or proof-assistant
verification. The new construction is useful for distinguishing a true
rank improvement from sufficient primitive arithmetic; no novelty or
irrationality claim follows.
