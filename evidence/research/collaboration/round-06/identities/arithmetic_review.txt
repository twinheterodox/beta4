1. The preliminary moment family and surviving prime layer

Taking residues at t=-j of the proposed Mellin rational function yields
exactly (-1)^j binom(4n,j)binom(n+j,n)^4. Both sides are proper rational
functions, so the Mellin identity and the fourfold moment zeros follow.
The choice Q=(a-bx)P indeed has Q(i)=a^2+b^2 and gives an integer quotient
by1+x^2 and a theta-only endpoint form.

For p=4n-1=3 modulo4, Lucas leaves exactly j=0,1,p,p+1, giving
P=(1-x^p)(1-rx) mod p with r=(5/4)^4. The displayed computation of
a+ib=(1+i)(1-ri) gives a=1+r,b=1-r, N=2(1+r^2). Direct polynomial
division gives the particularly simple check

 [x^(p-1)]V=[x^(p+1)]Q=ar+b=1+r^2=N/2 mod p.

There is exactly one p-divisible denominator in the rational endpoint
sum, while N is a p-unit. Thus the exact primitive exponent is4. The
note correctly interprets this as failure of forced arithmetic deletion,
not as a lower bound for the real evaluations.

2. Prescription and source injectivity

For either denominator D, the common denominator of
f=A/(1+x^2)+E^4(R/D^n)/x is(1+x^2)D^(n+4). The stated columns are its
integer numerators. Their low-order coefficients at0 and1 impose the
intended zeros, since the denominator is nonzero at both endpoints.
Dimension always supplies a nonzero vector. At full row rank the signed
maximal minors give the unique primitive source ray; the RREF fallback
is a deterministic definition at other indices and was not silently
used to choose successful finite values.

The injectivity argument is correct: E^4G/x=(E^3G)' is a rational
derivative and has zero residues, whereas A/(1+x^2) has nonzero residues
at +/-i if A is nonzero. Once A=0, E^4G=0 and regularity at0 with G(0)=0
force G=0. A nonzero source is therefore guaranteed. The note does not
incorrectly promote this to a nonzero endpoint value or a nonzero A.

Because G is bounded at infinity, E^4G/x=O(x^-2), so the cleared
numerator has degree at most2n+8. Dividing by the endpoint factors
leaves degree at most8. In the circular case the common1+x^2 cancels,
leaving degree at most6. Both degree bounds retain the endpoint
conditions and include the constant-G cancellation at infinity.

3. Exact endpoint and normalization

With x=exp(-z), the derivative contribution is integral_0^infinity
g''''(z) z^3/6 dz, where g(z)=G(exp(-z)). Four integrations by parts
give G(1): all intermediate terms at z=0 vanish by their powers of z,
and g and its derivatives decay exponentially at infinity. Thus the
endpoint A theta+R(1)/D(1)^n has no auxiliary period.

The primitive pair is exactly (dA,R(1))/g with d=D(1)^n and
g=gcd(dA,R(1)). At full rank, source content Delta divides the two
augmented determinants, and their gcd divided by Delta is exactly g.
The source sign convention changes neither gcd. These are actual
content formulas, including the possibility kappa=d/g<1; they are
not merely common-denominator upper estimates.

The local coefficient identity j^4[x^j]G=-A(-1)^((j-1)/2) for odd
j<=n follows directly from the zero at0. Since G has integral Taylor
coefficients, the fourth power of the odd LCM divides A. This controls
source height, not the primitive value after common content and real
cancellation. That distinction is correctly preserved.
