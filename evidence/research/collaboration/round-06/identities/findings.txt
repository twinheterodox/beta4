ROUND 06 — FUNCTIONAL RELATIONS, POWER ORBITS, AND AN ALGEBRAIC PIVOT
29 October 2026. Local, unpublished proof and computation packet.

Outcome and scope
=================

Exact completed-polylogarithm distribution does extend to the old
non-torsion ladder. It does NOT preserve its theta-only endpoint. I prove
two obstructions to repairing that loss with a fixed module:

* A fixed finite list of rational-function D4 germs cannot absorb all
  prime-power defects modulo constants. New primitive10p branch points
  force the total degree of additional arguments to grow at least
  linearly in p. A precise divisor-symbol contraction also detects a
  surviving p denominator in any symbol-certified rewrite of the defect.
* The natural anharmonic reflection at q^p, q=(1-i)/2, introduces the
  norms N_p=Norm((1+i)^p-1). For distinct odd primes these integers are
  pairwise coprime. Their logarithms add one independent rational-log
  direction per prime, with an explicit weighted height cost.

These are scoped module/presentation obstructions, NOT independence
theorems for tetralogarithm VALUES and NOT primitive-form no-go theorems.

The substantial pivot is an exact specialization of an actual weight-four
functional relation of Gangl. It isolates12 beta(4), with21 remaining
arguments in a quartic field. All21 can be moved inside the unit disk;
their coefficient L1 norm is39 and their largest modulus is0.9810017... .
Their least common integer scale to algebraic integers is216. Ordinary
relative traces work before choosing inversions; the completion logarithms
and the incompatible embeddings obstruct the tempting free rational trace.
A prescribed termwise trace after inversion actually has radius less than
1/6. No rational primitive approximation family is claimed for this pivot.

Independent initial choices were saved in00_initial_choices.txt before
discussion with the other workers. No old files were changed. This is an
application and exact specialization of classical functional relations,
not a claim that the general relation or the specialization is new in
the literature. No priority search or external publication was performed.

1. Exact function and primary-source reading
==========================================

Use theta=beta(4) and the standard single-valued classical tetralogarithm

 D4(z)=Im[Li4(z)-log|z| Li3(z)+(log^2|z|/3)Li2(z)].      (1.1)

Initially this formula is read on |z|<1, and then continued as the
single-valued function. The convention is the IMAGINARY part at even
weight. It satisfies D4(conj z)=-D4(z), D4(1/z)=-D4(z), and vanishes on
the real projective line. The inversion and distribution conventions were
checked in Zagier's primary author appendix, section1, PDF pages3-5:
 https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/zagier/files/tex/LewinPolylogarithms/fulltext.pdf

Actual weight-four input for the pivot: Herbert Gangl, Functional equations
of higher logarithms, arXiv:math/0207222v1, Theorem4.1 and its proof in
section6 (PDF pages7 and10-13). The source and extracted text are adjacent:
 gangl_functional_equations.pdf / gangl_functional_equations.txt
 https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0207222

Gangl's22-term relation in section3 is a TRILOGARITHM relation. It is not
used as a weight-four theorem. The selected Theorem4.1 is explicitly for
L4. Its proof checks the fourth-weight symbol, including the final terms.

There is a literal normalization discrepancy in section2.1: BOTH the
arXiv PDF and published author PDF print the Bernoulli sum through m,
rather than m-1. The page was inspected visually. With the standard
Li0(z)=z/(1-z), this adds -log^4|z| Im Li0(z)/45 at weight4 and does not
give(1.1); at weight2 it also contradicts the stated Bloch--Wigner
identification. We do not silently apply that literal displayed function.
Instead, the independent exact symbol certificate in section5 below,
combined with Zagier's correctly stated m-1 completion, PROVES the
required identity. The published copy and definition-page image are
retained locally. The literal extra term is also numerically certified
to change our specialized relation to-0.1568968453..., not zero.

Also read Rudenko, On the functional equations for polylogarithms in one
variable, arXiv:1511.09110v1, Theorem1.4 and section3 (PDF pages2-3,6-8):
 https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.09110
 rudenko_one_variable.pdf / rudenko_one_variable.txt
The printed definition repeats Re at even weight; (1.1), independently

2. Distribution is exact, but the endpoint changes
=================================================

For any positive integer d, direct summation of the convergent series gives

 D4(z^d)=d^3 sum_(zeta^d=1) D4(zeta z).                 (2.1)

Indeed the Li_s root-of-unity sum is d^(1-s)Li_s(z^d), while
log|z^d|=d log|z|. All three completion terms therefore have the same
factor d^3. Continuation gives the identity globally; no additive
constant is lost, as the series identity tends to zero at z=0.

The exact exclusion of Taylor summands whose index is divisible by p is

 D4(z)-p^-4 D4(z^p)
  =sum_(n>=1,p not dividing n) Im(z^n)
        [n^-4-log|z| n^-3+(log^2|z|/3)n^-2].            (2.2)

Thus completion does not destroy physical index deletion when its log
scales correctly. Formula(2.1) rewrites (2.2) as

 D4(z)-(1/p)sum_(zeta^p=1)D4(zeta z).                  (2.3)

For a squarefree product P of selected primes, all rotations can be merged:

 product_(p|P)(1-p^-4 V_p)D4(z)
     =sum_(zeta^P=1) a_(ord zeta) D4(zeta z),
 a_r=mu(r)/r product_(p|P/r)(1-1/p), r|P.              (2.4)

Here V_p means z->z^p on the completed function. There are P rotations,
their norms equal |z|, their coefficients have denominator dividing P,

 P a_r=mu(r) product_(p|P/r)(p-1),
 sum_(zeta^P=1)|a_(ord zeta)|=2^omega(P) phi(P)/P.      (2.5)

The rotation field Q(zeta_P) has degree phi(P). At the Gaussian ladder
point the argument field Q(i,zeta_P) has degree2phi(P), or phi(P) over
Q(i), for odd P. The argument count and coefficient height have not
disappeared. This is a presentation
identity, not a reduction of the primitive multiplier of an old form.

For comparison, the inherited completed Broadhurst ladder is

 R(z)=(225/136)D4(-z^2)-(25/34)D4(-z^3)+(9/68)D4(-z^5),
 q=(1-i)/2, R(q)=theta.                               (2.6)

For primes greater than5, raising rotations to powers2,3,5 permutes each
rotation orbit, so(2.4) applies to R as a P-function orbit as well.
Its depleted endpoint is EXACTLY

 sum_(d|P) mu(d)d^-4 R(q^d),                           (2.7)

not an asserted rational multiple of theta. No relation removing the
additional values in(2.7) has been proved. This is where torsion endpoints
and the non-torsion completed ladder differ: i^p=chi_4(p)i, whereas q^p
does not return to q by a root of unity alone.

3. A fixed rational-function module cannot absorb the power defects
===================================================================

First an explicit local singularity calculation avoids assumptions about
positivity, monodromy cancellation, or the interpretation of a symbol.
Near z=1 write z=exp(w), w=a+ib with a,b real and small. Integrating
Li1(exp(w))=-log(1-exp(w)) gives, for s=2,3,4, a holomorphic remainder
plus the sole nonanalytic term -w^(s-1)log(-w)/(s-1)! . In(1.1) its
coefficient is

 -w^3/6+a w^2/2-a^2 w/3 = i b(a^2+b^2)/6.

Therefore

 D4(exp(a+ib)) = [b(a^2+b^2)/6]log|a+ib|
                         + a real-analytic function.  (3.1)

In particular z=1 is a genuine non-real-analytic point. Single-valuedness
does not mean analyticity there. An unramified holomorphic pullback retains
this singularity. The function is real-analytic away from0,1,infinity.

THEOREM3.1. Fix any finite list of nonconstant rational functions f_j(t)
over C. For all sufficiently large primes p, R(t^p) cannot equal a
rational linear combination of D4(f_j(t)) plus a constant, as functions
of a complex parameter t. This remains true if coefficients may depend
on p and if R(t) is included in the fixed list.

Proof. At every primitive10p-th root zeta, -t^(5p) takes the value1 with
nonzero derivative. Neither -t^(2p) nor -t^(3p) equals0,1,infinity there.
The coefficient9/68 is nonzero, so(3.1) shows that R(t^p) has a genuine
singularity at each of the4(p-1) such points. The possible singularities
of the fixed list lie in the finite union of the preimages of0,1,infinity
under its rational functions. Eventually no primitive10p root is in this
finite set. A sum of real-analytic functions cannot equal the singular
left side. QED.

This is stronger than the elementary fact that the literal powers of a
non-torsion number have distinct moduli. It permits arbitrary clean
functional equations among the listed functions and arbitrary constants.
It is a PARAMETRIC statement; an identity at the single point t=q is
not excluded.

A quantitative extension is immediate. If additional rational arguments
g_(p,j) are used and their total degree is D_p, then, outside finitely many
p depending on the fixed list,

                         3D_p >=4(p-1).               (3.2)

Each nonconstant rational map of degree d has at most3d distinct points
over0,1,infinity. This is a degree/argument-complexity lower bound, not a
coefficient-height lower bound: t^p itself has small coefficients and
degree p. It allows a growing module or different approximation methods.

For an explicit symbol diagnostic use

 J4[f]=(f)^2 tensor ((f) wedge(1-f)),

where parentheses mean the divisor, and evaluate the first two factors
at0 and the wedge at(0,zeta). At a primitive10p root this gives

 J4[-t^(5p)] ->(5p)^3;
 J4[-t^(2p)], J4[-t^(3p)], J4[-t^m] (m=2,3,5) ->0.

Consequently the p-deleted R has contraction

          J4[R(t)-p^-4 R(t^p)] -> -1125/(68p).         (3.3)

For p>17 its p-adic valuation is-1. Every rational function has integer
divisor orders. Thus any rational-coefficient rewrite whose divisor
symbols AGREE with this defect must have at least one coefficient with
negative p-adic valuation. It cannot be rewritten with only p-integral
coefficients in this symbol calculus. This includes symbol-certified
functional reductions and is not a statement about arbitrary numerical
relations at q, nor about common content of endpoint integer forms.

4. The anharmonic orbit introduces genuinely new logarithmic directions
=======================================================================

Put a=1+i, q=a^-1. For an odd prime p define

 A_p=a^p-1 in Z[i],
 N_p=A_p conjugate(A_p)=2^p+1-2 Re(a^p).               (4.1)

THEOREM4.1. N_p>1 is odd. The integers N_p for DISTINCT odd primes p
are pairwise coprime. Every rational prime ell dividing N_p satisfies
ell=1 modulo4p.

Proof. A_p is prime to1+i. For distinct primes p,r, the Euclidean
identity for a^m-1 shows gcd(A_p,A_r) divides a-1=i, hence is a unit.
If a Gaussian prime divides A_p and conjugate(A_r), then a^p=1 and
conjugate(a)^r=1 in its residue field. Since conjugate(a)=-ia, raising
to the other exponent gives(-i)^(pr)=1. Because pr is odd, this forces
i=1 or-1, possible only at a prime over2, already excluded. These two
orientations cover a rational prime common to N_p and N_r.

An inert odd rational prime cannot divide N_p: its Gaussian ideal is
conjugation-stable, so the same argument with p=r would force i=+/-1.
Thus ell splits and its residue field is F_ell. The residue of a has
order exactly p, since a-1=i is a unit. Hence p divides ell-1, and
splitting gives4 divides ell-1. Finally
N_p=|a^p-1|^2>1 for p>=3. QED.

For example N_3=13,N_5=41,N_7=113. No primitive-divisor theorem or
unproved prime-distribution statement is needed.

Taking absolute values gives the exact logarithmic relation

 log|1-q^p|=(log N_p-p log2)/2.                        (4.2)

The real logarithms log N_p, together with log2, are linearly independent
over Q: clear rational coefficients, exponentiate, and use unique prime
factorization and pairwise coprimality. Relative to any fixed finite set
of rational logarithms, all but finitely many selected primes therefore
require a new independent log direction. Also

 log N_p=p log2+O(2^(-p/2)),                           (4.3)

with explicit bounds2log(2^(p/2)-1)<=log N_p<=2log(2^(p/2)+1).
If log N_p is represented as sum_j c_j log r_j with c_j rational and
r_j positive rational, valuations give the useful weighted height bound

 log N_p <=sum_j |c_j| log(|numerator(r_j)denominator(r_j)|)
          <=2sum_j |c_j| h(r_j),                      (4.4)

where h is the usual logarithmic rational height. Coefficient size and
argument height can trade off; neither can simply be omitted.

This applies directly to the proposed anharmonic repair. Although

 q^p/(q^p+conj(q)^p) equals q or conj(q)

according as p=1 or3 modulo4, the reflection1-q^p and the fractional
linear argument q^p/(q^p-1) contain A_p in their divisor data. Returning
the quotient of conjugate powers to the old point does not close the
whole anharmonic/logarithmic orbit. The theorem rules out a FIXED
rational S-unit/log module for that mechanism. It does not prove
independence of D4(q^p), prohibit cancellations in a larger clean
functional relation, or give a lower bound for primitive approximation
errors. The completion in(1.1) itself uses log|q^p|, so(4.2) is specifically
the extra cost of invoking reflection/anharmonic transformations.

5. A concrete new presentation from a genuine weight-four relation
=================================================================

Set w=(1+i)/2 and let b,c be the roots of

                   2x^2-(1-i)x-1=0,

choosing b=(conj(w)+sqrt(2-i/2))/2 with the principal square root and
c=conj(w)-b. Define

 X={w,b,c}, Y={2/3,2/3,-1/3},
 t=-w/2, u=-4/27, U_x=1-1/x, V_y=1-1/y.

Exactly x^2(x-1)=t for every x in X, and y^2(y-1)=u for every y in Y.
The n=3 coefficient pattern of Gangl's Theorem4.1 gives the following
zero relation for the STANDARD D4, proved independently just below:

 0=3D4(t/u)
   -4 sum_(x in X,y in Y)D4(U_x/V_y)
   +9 sum_(x in X,y in Y)D4((1-x)/(1-y))
   -36sum_(x in X,y in Y)D4(x/y)
   +12sum_(x in X)D4(U_x)-12sum_(y in Y)D4(V_y).       (5.1)

For an independent symbol proof, parametrize the split triples by

 X(r)={r(r+1),r+1,-r}/(r^2+r+1), Y(s)=X(s).

The34 terms of the displayed pattern belong to Q(r,s). The adjacent
gangl_symbol_check.py factors each argument and its complement into
12 multiplicative atoms and computes all1018 occupied coordinates of
beta4[f]=f^2 tensor(f wedge(1-f)) EXACTLY. Every coordinate is zero;
the JSON retains the complete factor vectors, not floating residuals.
Zagier's criterion for(1.1) therefore makes the value constant. Swapping
r,s negates it by inversion, so the constant is0. The specialization
r=w/b,s=1 gives exactly the X,Y above; this is checked in the exact
quadratic field by gangl_specialization.py. This proof uses the source's
coefficient pattern but not its ambiguous printed Bernoulli upper limit.

Multiplicity two at y=2/3 is retained. It is justified by continuity
from nearby distinct roots; none of the displayed nonreal arguments
becomes undefined. The symbol proof yields a constant, and swapping X,Y
negates the value by D4 inversion, while the diagonal X=Y has value0.
Thus the additive constant is0. No unaccounted lower-weight products
or assumed independence of periods enters(5.1).

All V_y are real, so their D4 terms vanish. Exactly U_w=i contributes
12theta. Every other term is a specified D4 value. Applying only the
proved inversion and conjugation laws places its argument inside|z|<1.
The result can be written

                  theta=sum_(j=1)^21 d_j D4(z_j).     (5.2)

The complete EXACT list, in the basis z_j=A_j+B_j b with A_j,B_j in Q(i),
is gangl_specialization.json; the adjacent self-contained script constructs
it directly from(5.1), merges duplicates, and supplies an integral
polynomial for every argument. This is also an unambiguous compact
definition of(5.2), without decimal arguments or a PSLQ fit.

Its measured quantities are certified with Arb512-bit intervals:

 sum |d_j|=39; common coefficient denominator12;
 max |z_j|=0.9810017628608227634... <1;
 least positive integer D with every Dz_j integral is D=216.

For integrality, beta=2b satisfies

 beta^2-(1-i)beta-2=0,
 beta^4-2beta^3-2beta^2+4beta+4=0.                     (5.3)

The identities bc=-1/2 and(1-b)(1-c)=i/2 show that inversions of b,c,
1-b,1-c introduce only powers of2. All remaining denominators in the
explicit table divide2^3*3^3=216 in Z[i,beta]. Conversely the table
contains8(1+i)/27 and3(1+i)/8, forcing27 and8 respectively for ANY
integer integral scale, so216 is exact, not merely a convenient bound.
The largest displayed primitive integral-polynomial height is629856;
for Gaussian arguments the recorded degree-four polynomial can be a

The quartic in(5.3) is irreducible with discriminant2^8*17. Hence this
presentation has introduced a degree-four field and ramification at17,
as well as the explicit denominator prime3. It has not replaced the
old rational three-block presentation at negligible arithmetic cost.

All completion logarithms lie in the span of

                      log2, log3, log|b|.             (5.4)

Indeed |c|=1/(2|b|), |1-b|=2^(-3/2)|b|^-2,
|1-c|=2^(1/2)|b|^2, and |w|=2^-1/2. Together with the rational factors

products exactly, but does NOT say that they separately vanish in an
ordinary Li4-only expansion.

6. What the trace does and does not do
====================================

There is a valid relative trace already present. Let sigma fix i and
exchange b,c. For every rational function F over Q(i),

 F(b)^n+F(c)^n in Q(i).

These power sums obey the exact order-two recurrence with coefficients
F(b)+F(c) and F(b)F(c). In particular the uncompleted Li_s sums over
the entire X-set in(5.1), with a COMMON formal variable multiplying
their arguments, have coefficients in Q(i); taking imaginary parts
gives rational coefficients. This useful statement requires no CM field.

There are two distinct obstacles to promoting it to the intended rational
completed approximation.

(a) The original relative-trace orbit includes arguments of modulus>1.
After individually inverting them to obtain(5.2), the chosen inside-disk
representatives are no longer closed under sigma. A naive termwise trace
of the resulting power series has arguments as large as

            max_j |sigma(z_j)|=6.004046715799924595... .

The maximum occurs uniquely at the relative conjugate of-2(1-1/b), whose
coefficient in(5.2) is2/3. No other term has that modulus. In the ordinary
Li4 part, n^4 times the traced coefficient is a finite exponential sum.
Its largest nonreal conjugate pair cannot cancel, so the root limsup of
the absolute coefficients equals that maximum. Equivalently its rational
generating function after four Euler derivatives has an uncanceled pole
there. The traced Taylor series has radius at most1/6.004... and cannot
be evaluated term by term at1. This is a proved failure of that specified
free-trace prescription, not of all algebraic simultaneous approximants.

(b) The completion needs log|b|, not merely logarithms of elements in
the relative field K=Q(i,b). In fact |b|^2 is NOT in K. If it were,
conj(b)=|b|^2/b would belong to K, making K conjugation-stable. But
K=Q(i,sqrt(8-2i)); conjugation-stability would force sqrt17 into K,
since sqrt(8-2i)sqrt(8+2i)=2sqrt17. The Gaussian square-class test rules
this out: neither17 nor(8-2i)/17 is a square in Q(i); the latter's norm
4/17 is not a rational square. Thus K is not CM in the chosen embedding.

For reference the positive real number r=|b|^2 is

 r=[1+sqrt17+sqrt(2+2sqrt17)]/8,
 16r^4-8r^3-8r^2-2r+1=0.                             (6.1)

A log germ formed from this norm naturally belongs to K times its
conjugate field, not to the two-term relative trace alone. Independent
permutations of holomorphic and antiholomorphic branches require explicit
log/monodromy bookkeeping. No such paired trace identity is assumed here.
This identifies precisely what is already rational and what is missing.

7. Actual real error available, and the missing integer family
=============================================================

With every logarithm kept as its EXACT constant, truncate each completed
series in(5.2) after N. Its error is bounded by

 sum_j |d_j| r_j^(N+1)/(1-r_j)
   [1/(N+1)^4+|log r_j|/(N+1)^3+log^2(r_j)/(3(N+1)^2)].
                                                                  (7.1)

The proved bounds at N=20,100,500 are respectively less than
0.000855,5.075e-7,3.480e-12. This is a quantitative REAL evaluation
scheme, not an integer linear form: the truncations retain algebraic
coefficients and the three logarithms(5.4).

For this fixed completed-source truncation the errors are nonzero
infinitely often. If they were eventually zero then every sufficiently
late coefficient would vanish. Multiplication by n^4 gives a sum of
distinct exponentials z_j^n,conj(z_j)^n with polynomial coefficients
of degree at most2. At the unique largest-modulus argument its polynomial
coefficient is nonzero. Such an exponential polynomial cannot vanish
eventually, as seen by its rational generating function's poles.
This does not provide the needed rational/primitive normalization.

The immediate construction gate is explicit: supply a coupled algebraic
and logarithmic numerator family that preserves(5.2), controls the other
embeddings, and yields rational coefficients after the correct period
cancellations. Simply tracing the existing Taylor terms fails by6(a);
simply treating the logs as rational constants fails by6(b). No claim
that every possible signed or simultaneous family fails is made.
