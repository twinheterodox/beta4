UNSQUARED DENSE-QUARTER POLES: EXACT PERIOD GATE, FAILED BOUNDED TEST
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Outcome

Lifting the round10 positive Bernoulli functional from y^2 to the imaginary
line turns its unwanted zeta(4) coefficient into a polynomial of degree four
in the pole. Ordinary Lagrange interpolation then removes that coefficient
without the high-degree positive numerator of round10. The beta(4)
coefficient matrix is positive definite. The matrix evaluated at beta(4)
is not asserted positive or uniformly nonsingular.

All seven prescribed primitive determinant values are large. Adding
fourth-order zeros at positive integers makes these four comparisons worse,
despite smaller raw values. This bounded gate is closed without further
index scans. There is no asymptotic primitive-growth theorem, universal
obstruction, or credible complete proof mechanism here.

An exact dilation places this functional inside ordinary-plus-alternating
half-pole derivative sums. The ordinary sum is rational but generally
nonzero; retaining it is essential. The source has zeros at EVEN integers
after dilation, which distinguishes it from the previously specified
all-integer-zero ray. This is a concrete change of source geometry and
rational correction, not discovery of a wholly independent functional.

1. The functional and its polynomial arithmetic

Use the archived Bernoulli functional

  nu(R)=integral_0^infinity R(y^2) w(y)dy,
  w(y)=-(y^4/3) (d/dy)^3 [1/(exp(2pi y)-1)] >0.

The already established formulas are

  nu(t^e)=(2e+4)! |B_(2e+2)|/[6(2e+2)!],
  nu(1/(t+a^2))=a^3 zeta(4,a)-1/(2a)-1/3,    a>0.

Define

  Lambda(R)=integral_0^infinity [R(i y)+R(-i y)]w(y)dy/2.

It is defined here on real rational functions having only negative real
poles, and on polynomials. Their integrals converge because w decays
exponentially at infinity and is bounded near zero. In particular,

  Lambda(x^(2e))=(-1)^e nu(t^e),
  Lambda(x^(2e+1))=0,
  Lambda(1/(x+a))=a^4 zeta(4,a)-1/2-a/3.                 (1)

The last equality is simply
  (1/(a+iy)+1/(a-iy))/2 = a/(a^2+y^2).

For every m>=0 the polynomial moments also equal

  Lambda(x^m)=(m+4)(m+3) B_(m+2)/6.                    (2)

Thus the polynomial functional is B((x^4 P)'')/6, where B(x^m)=B_m
and B_1=-1/2. This observation was independently supplied by the
coordinator. No arithmetic identification with a p-adic L-value is made.

2. The exact period cancellation

Let a_j=(2j+1)/4, j=0,...,K-1, and
  D_K(x)=product_j(x+a_j),
  T_j=sum_(k=0)^(floor(j/2)-1) 4^4/(4k+1+2(j mod2))^4.
The empty sum is zero. Then

  zeta(4,a_j)=120 zeta(4)+128(-1)^j theta-T_j,
  theta=beta(4).

Consequently the three coefficients of the pole value (1) are
  zeta4: 120 a_j^4,
  theta: 128(-1)^j a_j^4,
  rational: -a_j^4 T_j-1/2-a_j/3.                       (3)

For any polynomial P of degree <=K-6, P/D_K is proper and

  Lambda(P/D_K)
   =sum_j P(-a_j)/D_K'(-a_j) Lambda(1/(x+a_j)).

Its zeta4 coefficient vanishes, since
  sum_j a_j^4 P(-a_j)/D_K'(-a_j)=0.                    (4)
This is the Lagrange identity for the polynomial x^4P(x), of degree
at most K-2. No numerical identity or period-independence assumption
is used. The rational part of (3), including both elementary corrections,
is retained in every calculation.

For h>=1 and K>=2h+4 define H_ik(X) by replacing theta by X in
Lambda(x^(i+k)/D_K), 0<=i,k<h. Then H(X)=B+XA with rational matrices.
Because
  D_K'(-a_j)=(-1)^j j!(K-1-j)!/2^(K-1),

  A=128 sum_j [(-1)^j a_j^4/D_K'(-a_j)] v_j v_j^t,
  v_j=(1,-a_j,...,(-a_j)^(h-1))^t,                    (5)

is positive definite. All weights are positive, and a polynomial of
degree<h cannot vanish at the K>=h distinct nodes. Hence det H(X)
has exact degree h and positive leading coefficient. This is polynomial
nonvanishing, not yet nonvanishing of det H(theta).

3. Fourth-order integer zeros and derivative sums

Prescribe M>=0 and
  V_M(x)=product_(n=1)^M (x-n)^4,
  H_ik=Lambda(V_M(x)x^(i+k)/D_K(x)),
  K>=4M+2h+4.                                        (6)

Equation (4) still holds, and (5) gains the positive weight V_M(-a_j).
No polynomial quotient occurs in these fixtures: deg(V_M x^(i+k))<=K-6.
The general functional includes polynomial quotients through (2); they
are not discarded where present.

Put F(x)=x^4 P(x)/D_K(x), where deg P<=K-6. Then F=O(x^-2), and

  Lambda(P/D_K)=-(1/6) sum_(n>=0) F'''(n).             (7)

Proof: decompose F=sum_j c_j/(x+a_j), which is proper and has
sum_j c_j=0. Here c_j=a_j^4 P(-a_j)/D'_K(-a_j). Differentiating three
times and summing gives sum_j c_j zeta(4,a_j). The elementary rational
terms in (1) vanish after weighting by P(-a_j)/D'(-a_j), again by
Lagrange interpolation (degrees deg P and deg P+1 are <K-1).
This proves (7) directly without assuming a summation formula for
arbitrary Bernoulli-series functions. Absolute convergence is immediate
from F'''(n)=O(n^-5).

For P=V_M x^m, F has fourth-order zeros at 0,1,...,M. Thus the first
M+1 summands of (7) vanish. This was the specific proposed arithmetic
repair. It is not a claim that the remaining derivative summands have
one sign, or that a matrix of them is positive.

4. Exact relationship to the old Euler functional

Let d=m+4+4M and put

  U(u)=u^(m+4) product_(n=1)^M (u-2n)^4
                      /product_(j=0)^(K-1)(u+j+1/2).

Then F(u/2)=2^(K-d) U(u). Define

  O(U)=-(1/6)sum_(n>=0) U'''(n),
  A(U)=-(1/6)sum_(n>=0)(-1)^n U'''(n).

Splitting the even lattice gives exactly

  Lambda(V_M x^m/D_K)=2^(K-d+2)[O(U)+A(U)].           (8)

If U=sum_j c_j/(u+j+1/2), then sum_j c_j=0. Write
  b_j=j+1/2,
  H_j=sum_(k<j) b_k^(-4),
  T'_j=sum_(k<j) (-1)^k b_k^(-4).
The two contributions are

  O(U)=-sum_j c_j H_j,
  A(U)=16 theta sum_j(-1)^j c_j
                      -sum_j(-1)^j c_j T'_j.         (9)

Indeed zeta(4,1/2)=15 zeta4, so the ordinary zeta4 term is multiplied
by sum c_j=0. The ordinary contribution remains a rational number;
setting it to zero would alter the primitive polynomials. Equations
(8)-(9) independently reproduce every matrix entry in all seven tests.

6. Missing theorem and decision

A sufficient target remains an unbounded prescribed family with degree
O(K), nonzero Q_K(theta), and -log|Q_K(theta)|/K tending to infinity.
Under theta=u/v, the value v^deg Q_K Q_K(theta) would then be a nonzero
integer tending to zero. There is currently no primitive-content estimate
or actual signed-error estimate meeting that condition here.

The finite results do not establish a family impossibility theorem.
They do establish that removing S_K and adding these integer zeros do
not by themselves supply a convincing numerical margin in the prescribed
fixtures. Further scans require a specific arithmetic or analytic change.
