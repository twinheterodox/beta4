RATIONALITY-DEPENDENT NIVEN GATE
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Outcome

A fixed hypothetical theta=u/v does create exact endpoint zeros in a
nonlinear source. It does not supply the finite differentiation and
endpoint integrality needed for a Niven-type contradiction. The exact
derivative obstruction below is stronger than a numerical failure, but
its scope is only the proposed blanket endpoint-integrality mechanism.
It does not exclude every finite Niven truncation or a construction using
the full polylogarithmic jet. No credible repaired mechanism emerged.

1. The assumption-dependent positive source

Let chi4(m) be 0 on even m and (-1)^((m-1)/2) on odd m, and put

 F(x)=sum_(m>=1) chi4(m)x^m/m^4, E=x*d/dx,
 theta=F(1)=beta(4), E^4F=x/(1+x^2).                  (1)

The series, or its integral

 F(x)=x/6 integral_0^1 (-log t)^3/(1+x^2 t^2) dt,

shows F'(x)>0 on [0,1]: the differentiated integrand contains
(1-x^2 t^2)/(1+x^2 t^2)^2, positive almost everywhere. Thus
0<F(x)<theta for 0<x<1.

Assume theta=u/v, u,v positive integers. Define

 A_n(x)=[vF(x)(u-vF(x))]^n/n!,
 I_n=integral_0^1 A_n(x)/(1+x^2) dx.                 (2)

Then I_n>0, and

  I_n <= (u^2/4)^n/n! ->0.                           (3)

Consequently an integrality theorem I_n in Z for an unbounded sequence
would prove irrationality. This is a precise sufficient contradiction;
no such theorem follows merely from the rationality assumption.

The factor A_n has a zero of order n at each endpoint. At zero its
first nonzero ordinary derivative is A_n^(n)(0)=(uv)^n. At x=1,
write t=log x. The first surviving derivative is

  E^n A_n(1)=(-uv)^n (EF(1))^n
            =(-uv)^n (pi^3/32)^n.                  (4)

The rational endpoint value does not rationalize this derivative or the
remaining jet, which also contains beta(2) and beta(1). The critical
beta(3) evaluation used in (4) is the same archived endpoint identity;
the derivative formula itself only needs EF(1).

2. An exact surviving prime layer in the origin derivatives

Fix n>=1 and nonzero integers u,v. For every ODD prime p>n with p not
dividing uv,

  v_p(A_n^(p+n-1)(0))=-3.                           (5)

The odd-prime restriction matters: p=2,n=1 would be an exception because
chi4(2)=0.

Proof. Expand

 A_n=(1/n!) sum_(k=0)^n (-1)^k binom(n,k)
                    u^(n-k) v^(n+k) F^(n+k).

Set N=p+n-1<2p. In a coefficient of degree N, a p-denominator can
only come from the coefficient chi4(p)/p^4 of one F factor. For
F^(n+k), including that coefficient requires degree at least
p+n+k-1. Thus only k=0 can contribute such a denominator, and its
only possibilities choose that coefficient once and the linear
coefficient of F in each other factor. Their multiplicity is n.
The resulting leading coefficient is

  u^n v^n chi4(p)/[(n-1)! p^4],

whose p-valuation is -4. Every other term is p-integral. Finally
v_p(N!)=1, so ordinary differentiation gives exactly (5). QED.

Thus no fixed numerator and denominator u,v make all origin derivatives
integral. This blocks a blanket endpoint-integrality claim, not every
finite truncation: a separate construction could use only selected
derivatives and pay selected denominators. No bound making such a
repair useful was obtained, and no old denominator scan is reopened.

3. Why changing differentiation coordinates does not complete the proof

In the coordinate z=F(x), A_n is a polynomial and differentiation in z
terminates. But the corresponding natural integral is exactly

 J_n=integral_0^1 A_n(x) F'(x) dx
    =u^(2n+1)n!/[v(2n+1)!].                         (6)

Substitute w=vF(x) and integrate w^n(u-w)^n by elementary repeated
integration by parts. Formula (6) is a positive rational beta integral,
not an integer. At n=(p-1)/2 for an odd prime p not dividing uv,
v_p(J_n)=-1. Its positive primitive constant polynomial is always1;
denominator clearing removes the apparent shrinking-integer gain.

The Euler derivative E retains the rational forcing in (1), but it does
not even preserve the rational function field C(x,F). Here is a short
functional proof, independent of any numerical period independence.
E^3F=arctan x has monodromy pi around x=i. The lower primitives have
finite limits there, so the induced change in F is

  Delta F=(pi/6)log^3(x/i),
  Delta(EF)=(pi/2)log^2(x/i).                        (7)

These follow by three integrations with E; each lower change vanishes
at x=i. Suppose EF=R(x,F), R in C(x,z). At a generic nearby x, repeating
the loop replaces F by F+m Delta F for every integer m, and EF by
EF+m Delta(EF). A rational function of z agreeing with an affine
function on that infinite set must itself be affine. Its z coefficient
would therefore be Delta(EF)/Delta F=3/log(x/i), which is not a rational
function of x. This contradicts R in C(x,z).

Thus the finite polynomial derivative sum central to the proposed
Niven transfer is unavailable in this field. The larger field involving
EF,E^2F,E^3F does close differentially under (1); the argument above
does not exclude it. It would require a new endpoint elimination and
arithmetic theorem, rather than an identification of real and p-adic
periods or the rationality of F(1) alone.

4. A rational-endpoint control retaining positive forcing

Define, using the convergent rational Taylor series of Li4,

 H(x)=x+[Li4(x/2)-Li4(x^2/2)]/32.                   (8)

Then H(0)=0, H(1)=1, and

 (E^4H)/x=1+[1/(2-x)-16x/(2-x^2)]/32 >=33/64
                                                   (0<=x<=1). (9)

For the bound, 1/(2-x)>=1/2 and 16x/(2-x^2)<=16. The forcing is
positive and rational, with all its poles outside the interval.
Also H'>=251/256. Indeed, for 0<=z<1,

 z <= Li3(z) <= z+z^2/[8(1-z)],

directly from its series. Hence, for 0<x<=1,

 Li3(x/2)/x >=1/2,
 2Li3(x^2/2)/x <= x+x^3/[16(1-x^2/2)] <=9/8.

Insert these in H'=1+[Li3(x/2)-2Li3(x^2/2)]/(32x).
The bounds extend to x=0 by continuity. Therefore 0<H(x)<1 inside
the interval, and [H(1-H)]^n/n! has the same positive endpoint-zero
features as (2). Its integral against (E^4H)dx/x is positive and at
most (33/32)4^(-n)/n!, since (E^4H)/x<=33/32.

The comparison is not a rational function in disguise. Li4(x/2)
has genuine logarithmic-cube monodromy around x=2, while the second
term is locally regular there on a continued branch. Thus rational
Taylor coefficients, a rational positive forcing, nontrivial logarithmic
monodromy, and positive endpoint factors coexist with a rational endpoint.
The target has a more restrictive pole geometry; no theorem turning
that distinction into the missing endpoint integrality was found.

This is a control for the broad proposed Niven transfer only, not an
obstruction to all assumption-dependent constructions.

5. Reproduction and scope

python3 checks.py uses exact rational arithmetic to compare (1), (8)
and (9) through degree32; reproduce (5) for prescribed small n, odd
primes and three fixed integer pairs; check the first endpoint-jet
formula on rational formal jets; and check the retained prime in (6).
No numerical theta enters construction or testing. The all-index
claims are the elementary proofs above; the finite checks supplement
them and are not formal verification.

The bounded run passed 96 origin derivative-layer checks, 18 endpoint
jet checks, 18 coordinate-integral prime checks and 60 dense-grid bound
checks, with both differential-equation series checked through degree32.

Root independently checked the derivative-layer argument and required
the explicit odd-prime qualification, incorporated above. This packet
does not assert a new irrationality result, a universal Niven no-go,
a p-adic identification, or an asymptotic primitive obstruction.
The packet is frozen after this bounded gate; manifest.json records
its file hashes. No additional source scan or research branch is active.
