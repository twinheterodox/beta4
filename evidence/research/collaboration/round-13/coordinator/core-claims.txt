1. Differential operators over the correct field

Let C=2t^2-2t+1, P=4t^4-16t^3+12t^2-8t+1, and
 Rplus=(4t^4-24t^3+44t^2-28t+9+4sqrt(2))/C.
The inverse parameter tau(t) has rational Schwarzian

 S={tau,t}=N/(2t^2 C^2 P^2),
 N=64t^12-512t^11+1984t^10-3584t^9+3952t^8-3200t^7
    +2208t^6-1600t^5+988t^4-448t^3+124t^2-16t+1.

Indeed S=-(D^3t)/(Dt)^3+(3/2)(D^2t)^2/(Dt)^4. It is invariant under
Gamma0(32)^+. Its finite poles have order at most two: at the four roots
of P (elliptic order2), the cusps 0 and (1+/-i)/2; infinity is also a cusp
and S=1/(2t^2)+O(t^-3). Thus the stated denominator and numerator-degree
bound follow. Matching the Fourier series to a degree exceeding the total
pole degree proves the identity. recover_modular_system.py checks through60.

Set Q=S/2, y=(dt/dtau)^(1/2), ignoring a nonzero constant factor. Then
y''+Qy=0 has solutions y and tau y. Its symmetric-cube operator is

 Lsym = d^4 +10Q d^2 +10Q' d +9Q^2+3Q''.

The identity J_+^2/(Dt)=Rplus/(t sqrt(P)) gives A_+=g y^3 with

 g=t^(-3/2) Rplus^(5/2) P^(-5/4),
 h=g'/g=-3/(2t)+(5/2)Rplus'/Rplus-(5/4)P'/P in K(t).

Consequently Lplus=g Lsym g^-1 lies in K(t)[d] and annihilates the four
functions A_+ tau^j, 0<=j<=3. It can be specified without fractional powers
by replacing d with d-h in Lsym, preserving operator order. Conjugate to
obtain Lminus.

For f_+=D^4H_+, Bol's identity gives

 Lplus(B_+)=Lplus(R_+)=r_+=t J_+^5 f_+/(Dt)^5 in K(t).

The source is invariant under Gamma0(32)^+: the Fricke signs of J_+^5 and
f_+ are opposite. It has pole order at most3 at t=0, at most5 at each
nonzero finite cusp, at most2 at the roots of P, and vanishes to order at
least4 at infinity. The last statements follow from holomorphy of the
weight-five forms at cusps and from local Fricke parity at the elliptic
points. Hence r_+=numerator/(t^3 C^5 P^2), numerator degree<=17.
Both rational components of that numerator are saved in
modular-system-coefficients.json and checked through degree60.

2. Homogeneous rank and rational descent

The monodromy of each Lplus/minus is a finite character twist of Sym^3
of the modular rank-two representation. Gamma0(32) contains noncommuting
opposite unipotents, so is Zariski dense in SL2; its symmetric cube is
irreducible. The two representations restrict identically to Gamma0(32)
but have opposite signs on the Fricke coset. They are not isomorphic on
Gamma0(32)^+: an intertwiner on the dense subgroup is scalar by Schur,
and a scalar cannot intertwine opposite Fricke signs.

Their eight homogeneous solutions therefore form a direct sum. The
least common left multiple L8 of Lplus,Lminus has order8 and lies in Q(t)[d]
by conjugation. Both A0 and A1 have nonzero projections to both summands.
Thus A0 has minimal order8, and A0,A0',...,A0^(7) are independent over Q(t).
The same holds for C_epsilon=16 epsilon A0+4 A1, epsilon=+1,-1: its two
coefficients are 8 epsilon+sqrt(2) and 8 epsilon-sqrt(2), both nonzero.
No nonzero rational function belongs to this homogeneous differential module.

L8 R0 is rational, since Lplus Rplus and Lminus Rminus are rational over K.
This proves an upper bound9 for the differential rank of R0. The prime
argument below proves that rank9 is attained under the rationality assumption.

3. A prime-prefix congruence and independence

In the coefficientwise p-integral local ring, through degrees below2p,

 p^4 R0(t) == t^p [16 chi(p) A0(t)+4 A1(t)] mod p, t^(2p),

assuming beta(4) rational and p outside its fixed denominator. Before
substitution, only the outer divisor d=p contributes to p^4H in this
range: p^4U ==16chi(p)q^p and p^4V==2q^p. The prefactors are integral.
The inverse q(t) is integral and q(t)^p==t^p mod p,t^(2p). The rational
theta*A term disappears modulo p after multiplying by p^4.

Let O be a fixed nonzero rational differential operator of order<=7.
Clear its finitely many bad coefficient primes; poles at t=0 only shift
the truncation by a fixed amount. Characteristic-p differentiation kills
t^p. Thus the corresponding congruence is t^p O C_chi(p).
Neither O C_+ nor O C_- is zero, by the preceding rank8 result. Their
first nonzero rational Laurent coefficients remain nonzero modulo all
but finitely many primes. A coefficient of O R0 at index p+k_epsilon
therefore has valuation exactly -4 for almost every prime in that class.

Consequences to check:
 (i) Under rationality of beta4, 1,R0,R0',...,R0^(7) are independent.
     A relation O R0+r=0 contradicts the p^-4 coefficient above. Thus the
     differential rank is exactly9, not the unfiltered rank4.
 (ii) Adding the eight functions A0,A0',...,A0^(7) gives seventeen
      independent functions. Rational-function combinations of the latter
      are p-integral outside a fixed prime set and cannot cancel (i).
 (iii) Every element outside the cheap span of 1 and those eight A-functions
       has fourth-power common-prefix denominator growth, provided it is
       formed in this seventeen-dimensional Q(t)-space.

More precise denominator-template claim (needs particular scrutiny):
O C_epsilon is not a polynomial, since the homogeneous differential module
has no rational functions. It has infinitely many nonzero Taylor/Laurent
coefficients. Consequently a fixed extra polynomial in the coefficient
index cannot explain away this fourth layer: choose a nonzero coefficient
at k outside its finite root set. In any template consisting of fixed LCM
factors [1,...,b_h n+c_h] and a fixed polynomial in n, four LCM factors
with b_h>=1 are necessary. This concerns fixed functions and fixed
templates; it is not a theorem about arbitrary n-dependent bases.

For the explicit basis the sufficient types are: one constant, eight
R-derivatives of type d_(n+7)^4, and eight integral A-derivatives. Fixed
initial denominators and the rationality denominator can be cleared once.

4. Two mandatory logarithmic cusp singularities

Approach tau=1/4 by q=i exp(-s), s->0+. Dominated convergence gives

 s H_+(tau) -> beta(5)(5+i sqrt(2)),
 s H_-(tau) -> beta(5)(5-i sqrt(2)).

Only F(2tau) and G(tau) have the relevant pole. The theta Gaussian sums give

 J_+ ~ pi(2i+sqrt(2))/(4s),  J_- ~ pi(2i-sqrt(2))/(4s).

Let a be the finite cusp value of t here; a is one of (1+/-i)/2. The cusp
has width2 on Gamma0(32), and t-a has a simple zero in its local parameter.
Thus t-a ~ const exp(-pi^2/(8s)), and
 Y ~ -(pi^2/8)(t-a)/(a s^2).
The leading terms of R_j are nonzero multiples of (t-a)^-1 log^4(t-a).
The algebraic factors giving nonzero leading coefficients are

 [(2i+sqrt(2))^5(5+i sqrt(2))
  +(2i-sqrt(2))^5(5-i sqrt(2))]/2 = -432i,

 and the difference divided by2sqrt(2) is108.
Using beta(5)=5pi^5/1536 gives coefficient45 i a for R0 and -45a/4
for R1 on this local branch. Only nonzero coefficients are needed.
Conjugation supplies the other finite nonzero cusp.

Continuation changes R0 by a homogeneous solution of L8. At a cusp these
homogeneous solutions have logarithm degree at most3. Thus continuation
cannot cancel the leading logarithm degree4. Both cusps are logarithmic
singularities on EVERY branch of R0, independently of rationality.

It follows that any HOLomorphic map phi:D->C, phi(0)=0, for which
R0(phi(z)) is meromorphic throughout D, must omit both (1+/-i)/2. A finite
ramification does not remove a logarithm. No assertion about meromorphic
maps hitting infinity or arbitrary changes of base coordinate is included.

5. Universal derivative bound for those holomorphic maps

Normalize the two omitted points to0,1. The basepoint becomes
lambda0=(1+i)/2 (or its conjugate). The elliptic modular lambda covering
and Schwarz--Pick give

 |phi'(0)| <= Rstar = (4/pi) Re(K(lambda0)^2)
                       =3.52992550073551475255...,
 log Rstar =1.26127676611499713406... .

Here K(m)=integral_0^(pi/2)(1-m sin^2u)^(-1/2)du. Equivalently
tau0=iK(1-lambda0)/K(lambda0), and the covering derivative is
2Im(tau0)|(4i/pi)lambda0(1-lambda0)K(lambda0)^2|.
An exact power-series tail bound can certify Rstar<18/5 and log Rstar<13/10.
The latter is already enough for the original and homogeneous-extension
gates. Numerical values are currently diagnostics until that certificate
and an independent implementation are saved.

6. Immediate comparison; exact scope

For the standard CDT LCM profiles, the dimension9 family has tau=320/81,
and the dimension17 homogeneous extension has tau=832/289. Both exceed
log Rstar, so no holomorphic uniformizing map for these full families can
meet the necessary positivity condition of those holonomy bounds with
these profiles. The stronger template claim in Section3 would show that
a fixed rational change of basis inside these spaces does not lower the
flat LCM cost below that profile.

This does not exclude a new arithmetic normalization outside the stated
templates, n-dependent constructions, other base coordinates, meromorphic
map theorems, or new extensions. A first-primitive variant is being assessed
separately and must use CDT's actual integration cost, not a whole extra
LCM layer per primitive.
