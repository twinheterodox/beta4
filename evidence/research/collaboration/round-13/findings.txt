BETA(4): BOUNDED MODULAR-HOLONOMY FEASIBILITY ASSESSMENT
30 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Conclusion
----------
The tested level32 modular construction does not warrant another round
of adding derivatives, homogeneous companions, or first primitives in
the same coefficient-type framework. The obstruction is quantitative
and structural, not merely a failed choice of uniformizing map.

Two cusp logarithms force every admissible holomorphic map in the present
t coordinate to have log|phi'(0)|<1.3. Comparing any proposed augmented
holonomy bound with the unconditional bound for its companion functions
forces log|phi'(0)|>4. The comparison also covers the weighted-metric
refinement in CDT7.6.4/8.0.5, with the precise hypotheses below.

This closes the original construction and the two variants authorized
for this phase. It does not rule out modular methods, arithmetic holonomy,
different coordinates or maps, or new arithmetic beyond these coefficient
types. The appropriate next bounded investigation is a complete-family
identity with genuinely complementary arithmetic. That phase has not
been started.

1. The construction and what rationality supplies
------------------------------------------------
Let theta=beta(4), kappa=7theta/2, and use the level32 parameter and
Eichler-integral filter of modular-detour.txt. In brief,

 t=eta(tau)^2 eta(4tau) eta(8tau) eta(32tau)^2
       /[eta(2tau)^3 eta(16tau)^3],
 Y=(q d/dq)log t,  J_+=theta3(2tau)^2+2sqrt(2)theta3(16tau)^2,
 A_+=J_+^5/Y=A0+sqrt(2)A1,
 B_+=A_+[16F(tau)-10F(2tau)+F(4tau)
               +(sqrt(2)/2)(4G(tau)-5G(2tau)+G(4tau))]
     =B0+sqrt(2)B1,
 Rj=Bj-kappa Aj.

Here F=sum_(d odd) chi(d)q^d/[d^4(1+q^d)] and
G=sum_(d odd)q^d/[d^4(1+q^(2d))]. Conjugation acts on both factors in B.
The Aj have integral Taylor coefficients in t. A fixed multiple of the
Bj coefficients through degree n is cleared by d_n^4, d_n=lcm(1,...,n).

If theta is rational, the Rj have rational Taylor coefficients and the
same sufficient denominator bound after one fixed scalar multiplication.
Their principal singularity at t0=0.153332807... cancels, and the chosen
germs are holomorphic on |t|<1/sqrt(2). This analytic cancellation holds
without rationality; rationality supplies the arithmetic needed to apply
the dimension theorem. No independence of unknown numerical periods is
assumed.

2. Rational descent and the independently counted functions
----------------------------------------------------------
The explicit system is given in coordinator/rational-system.txt, with
integer source polynomials in modular-system-coefficients.json. It is
obtained from two conjugate order4 operators over Q(sqrt(2)), not from
assigning the unfiltered rank4 equation to the filtered construction.

Their homogeneous modules are nonisomorphic finite-character twists of
the irreducible symmetric cube of modular monodromy. They form a direct
sum of rank8. The least common left multiple descends to Q(t), and
A0,A0',...,A0^(7) are independent. This semisimple derivative module W
contains no nonzero rational function.

Under rationality of theta, the following families are independent over
Q(t), and their spans are closed under differentiation:

 Original: 1,R0,R0',...,R0^(7).                              rank9
 Variant1: Original plus A0,A0',...,A0^(7).                   rank17
 Variant2: Variant1 plus I(t)=integral_0^t A0(u)du.           rank18

The explicit nine-component rational block system also uses R1 through
its third derivative; its closed span equals the rank9 family above.

The independence proof uses the exact congruence, for all sufficiently
large odd primes outside the hypothetical rationality denominator,

 p^4 R0(t)=t^p(16 chi(p)A0(t)+4A1(t))  (mod p,t^(2p)).

For every nonzero rational differential operator O of order<=7,
O(16 epsilon A0+4A1) is nonzero and nonrational for epsilon=+1,-1.
A fixed nonzero coefficient then supplies a surviving valuation -4 in
O R0 at an index p+O(1). It cannot be canceled by the homogeneous
companions, which are p-integral outside a fixed finite prime set, or
by a first primitive, whose valuation in that band is at least -1.

The explicit primitive I is independent of 1 and W because it has a
nonzero fourth logarithm at a cusp, whereas all members of 1+W have
logarithm degree at most3. This proves rank18; it is not a conjectural
count of cohomology classes.

3. Arithmetic profiles
-----------------------
The R derivatives through order7 are cleared by a fixed multiple of
d_(n+7)^4. The A derivatives are integral. The coefficients of I are
integer/n. Fixed shifts can be handled by endpoints (1+epsilon)n and a
limit epsilon->0 in the relevant bounds.

The congruence proves a lower bound for every fixed nonzero remainder
direction even after adding cheap companions: a denominator template
consisting of a fixed polynomial in n and finitely many LCM factors
d_floor(b_h n+c_h) must contain at least four factors with b_h>=1.
Choose a nonzero coefficient of O(16 epsilon A0+4A1) outside the finitely
many roots of the index polynomial to prove this assertion.

For explicit remainder derivatives the common-prefix log denominator
is 4n+o(n). For arbitrary rational-function combinations it is at least
4n+o(n) in the liminf sense, not necessarily exactly that rate: rational
factors can add exponential denominators at fixed primes. Nor is this a
claim about the reduced denominator of every individual coefficient.

CDT's flat/integration costs for the displayed families are:

                         dimension   integral    integer/n    four-LCM     tau
 Original                    9           1           0           8       320/81
 Homogeneous variant        17           9           0           8       832/289
 First-primitive variant    18           9           1           8       931/324

All three tau values exceed1.3. They fail the entry condition for the
full-family bounds. Smaller selected families require a separate argument:
for example, nine integral companions plus one remainder have tau=19/25,
which passes that entry condition but does not produce a contradiction.

4. Mandatory singularities and an all-map derivative bound
----------------------------------------------------------
The quotient parameter has four elliptic branch values (the roots of
4t^4-16t^3+12t^2-8t+1) and cusp values 0,infinity,(1+i)/2,(1-i)/2.
Adjoining homogeneous companions restores the obstruction at the first
elliptic value. The estimate below optimistically ignores that additional
restriction, making its failure stronger.

At each of the two nonzero finite cusps a, the R components have a
nonzero log^4(t-a)/(t-a) term. On the branch computed at tau=1/4 the
coefficients are 45ia and -45a/4 for R0,R1. Changing branch adds a
homogeneous solution with logarithm degree at most3. Thus both cusp
singularities persist on every branch; cancellation in the principal
disk does not remove them globally.

The result also survives mixed choices O R0+u, where u is in the rational
span of 1,W and fixed first primitives of W. If Delta=M-1 denotes cusp
monodromy, Delta^4u is rational, while O Delta^4R0 is nonzero and
nonrational. Moreover (M^e-1)^4R0=e^4 Delta^4R0, so finite ramification
cannot remove this obstruction.

Every holomorphic phi:(D,0)->(C,0) making such a noncheap function
meromorphic must therefore omit both cusps. The universal cover of the
twice-punctured plane and Schwarz--Pick give

 |phi'(0)|<=Rstar=(4/pi) Re(K((1+i)/2)^2)
                   =3.52992550073551475...,
 log Rstar=1.26127676611499713... .

An independent exact rational tail certificate proves Rstar<18/5 and
log Rstar<13/10. The decimals are diagnostic values; the strict rational
certificate is what the obstruction uses. Maps on an open disk can be
restricted to smaller disks when applying a theorem requiring extension
past the boundary, then handled by a limit.

5. Why smaller families and more companions do not rescue this bound
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Suppose c independent unconditional companions and r>=1 remainder
directions share the same admissible map; m=c+r. Suppose k companions
have certified integer/n type and the others are integral. Write
a=log|phi'(0)| and H_k=sum_(j=1)^k 1/j. For k<m the exact integration cost is

 s(m,k)=(2m-1)H_k/m^2.

The all-primitive endpoint has
s(c,c)=((2c-1)H_(c-1)+1)/c^2. In every case
m s(m,k)>=c s(c,k). The full favorable profile is

 tau_F=4(1-c^2/m^2)+s(m,k),       tau_C=s(c,k).

The unconditional companion bound uses the same geometric numerator B
as the proposed augmented bound. A contradiction would require

 c(a-tau_C)<=B<m(a-tau_F),

and therefore

 r a>m tau_F-c tau_C>=4r(1+c/m)>4r.

This is incompatible with a<1.3. When c=0 the entry condition already
requires a>4. The analogous calculation covers certified integral or
single-LCM companion bases with tau_C<=1.

The comparison extends to CDT's fixed-radius and weighted-metric
refinements. For fixed radius weights s, their proof gives the numerator

 B_d(s)=-s^T Gs+(2/d)integral_0^d min_k[(Gs)_k-x log r_k] dx.

Because r_k<=1, the integrand is nondecreasing. Hence B_c(s)<=B_m(s).
Use the same weights for the unconditional companions, even when they
were selected for the full family. This proves the same necessary a>4
condition for CDT7.6.4/8.0.5; no assumption that their stationary weights
are global minimizers is made. The precise derivation and independent
check are in the two optimized-comparison notes.

The original comparison also handles the stated multiple-map bound in
CDT6.0.2 with a common normalized partition. Every map in that application
must obey the same cusp omission. No numerical conformal-map optimization
or large coefficient scan can fix this particular comparison.

6. Verification and remaining limits
------------------------------------
The main agent developed the constructions and comparison. One worker
performed an independent model check, with no delegation. Its conclusions
and qualifications are in independent/audit-report.txt and
independent/optimized-comparison-audit.txt. This is mathematical checking
with exact computations, not formal verification or an external human audit.

Reproducible checks include the source/Schwarzian identities through
degree145 from an independent eta-series implementation; the full prime
prefix for20 odd primes; symbolic symmetric-cube covariance and cusp
constants; an exact certificate for Rstar<18/5;495 exact piecewise
minimizations of the integration cost; and15,120 finite checks of the
relative arithmetic inequalities. The infinite claims additionally use
the modularity, divisor-degree, representation, and analytic arguments
written in the notes. A finite Fourier match by itself is not a proof.

No lemma needed for the scoped comparison is presently left conjectural.
The published CDT holonomy theorem and its foundational arithmetic
Hilbert--Samuel and multiplicity results remain imported mathematical
inputs, not independently formalized theorems. Reading scopes, including
unread or inaccessible dependencies, are recorded in coordinator/sources.txt.

Specific limits of the obstruction:
 * The fixed level32 family and the current t coordinate are essential.
 * The maps here are holomorphic into C; arbitrary meromorphic maps are
   not covered by the two-omitted-point bound.
 * Arithmetic profiles must be justified for the chosen basis. Rational
   combinations of first primitives need not remain of integer/n type.
 * Fixed-template lower bounds do not cover n-dependent constructions,
   arbitrary auxiliary-polynomial arithmetic, or a new integral lattice.
 * Higher extensions and different modular families are unassessed.

There is no assertion that these remaining possibilities cannot work.
There is also no specific candidate among them with a favorable margin.

7. Recommendation
------------------
Stop this bounded modular investigation. The rationality-sensitive
cancellation is real, but the resulting functional family has too much
arithmetic cost relative to the continuation it can retain. Increasing
its counted dimension through the tested companions cannot reverse this.

The best next bounded target is an identity of complete parameterized
families with complementary primewise divisibility, as discussed in
literature/strategy-2026-10-30.txt. Round12's two unequal numerators do
not supply one. A new representation of an unchanged primitive pair
cannot improve that pair's actual arithmetic; the task must establish
previously unproved uniform savings or generate a genuinely new family.
This is a research recommendation, not evidence that irrationality is
close or that the proposed identity exists.
