BETA(4) LANDSCAPE REVIEW: WHAT THE ARCHIVE DOES AND DOES NOT RULE OUT
Local and unpublished. Review phase; no new construction campaign.

Evidence basis
Read the current handover, archive index, all fifteen coordinated-round
reports, method-map.txt, next-proof-avenues.txt and arcsine-contact.txt.
The integrated reports are evidence of the project's recorded conclusions,
not a fresh independent audit of every underlying proof or computation.
Decisive underlying proofs for a proposed next step are read separately.
Earlier navigation entries are chronological; round 15 supersedes the
round 12 assertion that the quarter pairing is still unconstructed.

Classification

1. Actual primitive-value obstructions, with narrow hypotheses

 - Round 1: the positive cubic-tail kernel on shifted consecutive
   PRINCIPAL nodes has primitive values growing at least
   exp((2-2 log 2-o(1))n^2). Arbitrary different row and column sets are
   explicitly excluded from that conclusion. The analytic comparison
   with a Cauchy determinant DOES cover those other ordered sets.
 - Round 3: the specified positive repeated-quadratic-pole sources,
   with even index and subexponential rational pole height, fail after
   primitive reduction. This is not a general rational-kernel theorem.
 - Round 5: the specified Euler--Boole approximants fail uniformly in
   endpoint/truncation schedules; products of those factors do not fix
   the problem. General sums or nonfactoring determinants are not covered.
 - Round 6: the full principal difference-Gram family has lower rate
   6-log 4. Other subspaces and frames are not covered.
 - Round 7: fixed-endpoint, EVEN-depth third-derivative continued
   fractions fail. Growing endpoint and odd depth are not included.
 - Round 9: the particular quarter-Gamma cumulant sequence has only
   polynomial error and an exponential actual denominator lower bound.
 - Round 11: the specific sine-normalized cyclotomic norm family fails
   after the complete conjugate product and primitive content are paid.

2. Genuine structural obstructions to particular attempted implications

 - Same-variable algebraic repairs do not remove the slow filter's
   nonreal logarithmic branch points (round 1).
 - Certain higher-level modular coordinates and prefactors have actual
   uncancelled poles (rounds 5-6). Raw pole growth is generally not a
   primitive-value impossibility theorem.
 - Ordinary rational gauges of the two-state beta4 tail do not give
   the proposed polynomial unimodular transfer (round 12).
 - The direct even-power integer-signature construction lies in the
   wrong cyclotomic component (round 10); its rational trace vanishes.
 - An operator removing the endpoint logarithms from the specified
   regularized primitive also removes its additive constant (round 12).
   This does not exclude all global or nonlinear rationality arguments.
 - Full-rank changes of a complete Gram lattice change the determinant
   only by a rational scalar. They cannot improve its primitive
   polynomial (round 10 and earlier explicit comparisons).

3. Limitations of estimates or normalizations, not family impossibilities

 - Round 2: the fixed-cut absolute-contour estimate for the k=3,5 slow
   filters cannot beat the supplied arithmetic cost. It is not a lower
   bound for actual determinant values.
 - Round 13: the specified level-32 functions, coordinate and holonomy
   coefficient templates have incompatible bounds (<1.3 versus >4).
   This does not cover different modules, coordinates or meromorphic maps.
 - Round 14: the two displayed integer multipliers are nested and give
   no complementary saving. Integer clearing of that fixed cofactor
   fails even without odd-prime cost. Extra primitive numerator content
   remains uncontrolled, so arbitrary rational rescaling is not excluded.
 - Round 15: the specified source-style term/row estimates can certify
   at most 88.207529... odd-prime saving against 142.862026... required
   by the established decay. Neither actual primitive content nor a
   faster projected-error exponent is bounded by this comparison.

4. Finite failures or missing theorems

 - The circular Hermite family has proved signed nonvanishing and a
   complete endpoint system; useful primitive height remains unproved.
   The large values through n=80 are evidence, not a family theorem.
 - Common-base ladder determinants shrink after dyadic clearing. Odd
   content is unresolved; no justified uniform odd-prime saving exists.
 - Prime-depleted determinants have a proved arithmetic advantage, but
   uniform smallness/nonvanishing when the depletion set grows is missing.
   The one-surviving-prime oversieved family is a separate proved failure.
 - The internal-edge graph has exact period reduction and small positive
   integrals. Four-index elimination is a finite failure; higher-index
   primitive asymptotics were not proved.
 - Dense-quarter, unsquared-pole, Whipple-jet and arcsine constructions
   have exact identities and finite unfavorable tests. Do not convert
   those into general determinant or hypergeometric exclusions.
 - The unsquared-pole family has conditional nonvanishing at infinitely
   many prime scales; that resolves neither its size nor its content.

Recurring assumptions worth changing

 a. Construct accurate real approximations first and pay arithmetic later.
 b. Eliminate auxiliary periods by scalar cofactors, thereby multiplying
    growing coefficients, before checking a common integral structure.
 c. Use identical row and column nodes in a positive determinant.
 d. Bound valuations term by term rather than through relations between
    rows, columns and successive prime layers.
 e. Treat a basis change, rational gauge, geometric interpretation or
    exact identity as a primitive arithmetic improvement by itself.
 f. Use a rationality hypothesis only to replace an initial constant,
    without identifying any new global arithmetic or analytic constraint.
 g. Infer all-index behavior from a finite pattern, or ignore the
    rationality denominator raised to a growing polynomial degree.

An explicit repetition avoided

The parameter-dependent arcsine proposal in next-proof-avenues.txt was
subsequently developed in arcsine-contact.txt. That record includes the
full boundary recurrences, the odd-period identity, two prime layers,
full-parity rows and 202 primitive tests. Recommending those same tasks
again would be rediscovery. The remaining mixed-frame/local-rank issue
would need a specific different construction, not another row-basis change.

Promising gap for further source review

Round 1 already proves positivity and an essentially exact real estimate
for nonprincipal minors of a beta4-only kernel. Its arithmetic obstruction
was proved for principal frames. Litsey's later rectangular-border work
suggests a concrete different node geometry and a local-kernel pairing
mechanism. The geometry and its valuation theorem must be transferred
separately; the trigamma theorem itself is not a beta4 theorem.
