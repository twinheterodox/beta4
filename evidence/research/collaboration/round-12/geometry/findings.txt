REGULARIZED PRIMITIVE: A SCOPED ARITHMETIC-HOLONOMY GATE
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Outcome

Canceling the lower endpoint periods really does give a full rational
G-function germ if theta=beta(4) is rational. It does not improve its
meromorphic continuation. More strongly, every rational linear
differential operator that removes its logarithmic endpoint branching
also kills the constant carrying theta. This closes this particular
regularization shortcut, not arithmetic holonomy or geometric methods.

1. The exact arithmetic germ

Put E=x*d/dx and F_j=E^j F, where
 F(x)=sum_(m>=1) chi4(m)x^m/m^4,
 E^4 F=x/(1+x^2), F(1)=theta.
On the positive real axis define

 C(x)=F_0-log(x)F_1+log(x)^2 F_2/2-log(x)^3 F_3/6.       (1)

Differentiating telescopes, and the limit at0 is zero, so

 C'(x)=-log(x)^3/[6(1+x^2)],
 C(x)=integral_0^x -log(t)^3/[6(1+t^2)]dt,
 C(1)=theta.                                           (2)

Thus C_q(x):=C(x)+q-theta has rational Taylor coefficients at x=1
for every rational q. Its entire local jet, not merely its value, is
arithmetic. This is a valid repair of the inverse-germ objection in
round11, but (2) is the old log-cube beta4 integral.

The derivative in (2) also proves C(1/x)=C(x): both sides have the same
derivative and agree at x=1. Set

 x=(1-z)/(1+z), y=z^2,
 A(y)=atanh(sqrt(y))/sqrt(y)=sum_(n>=0)y^n/(2n+1).

The inversion symmetry makes C even in z. Exactly,

 C(x)=theta+J(y), J(0)=0,
 J'(y)=-(2/3)*y*A(y)^3/(1+y).                           (3)

Here J is an unconditional rational-coefficient G-function. Under the
hypothesis theta=q in Q, the actual period primitive is simply q+J.
The rationality hypothesis only changes its constant coefficient.

2. An exact rank-five module and why the basic holonomy test is blind

Let B=yA. Then

 B'=B/(2y)+1/[2(1-y)],
 (B^k)'=k B^k/(2y)+k B^(k-1)/[2(1-y)] (k=1,2,3),
 J'=-2B^3/[3y^2(1+y)].                                (4)

Consequently the rational differential span of 1,J is

 V=span_(Q(y)){1,B,B^2,B^3,J}.                          (5)

Its dimension is exactly5. B is transcendental over C(y), since its
continuation around y=1 has infinitely many distinct values. Hence
1,B,B^2,B^3 are independent. On the branch from0 to y=-1, A(-1)=pi/4,
and the residue of J' there is pi^3/96. Thus J has a genuine logarithmic
singularity at -1, whereas rational combinations of the first four
functions are meromorphic there. J is independent of them.

For EVERY rational q,

 span_(Q(y)){1,B,B^2,B^3,q+J}=V.                        (6)

Adding the constant q also leaves all meromorphic pullback domains
unchanged. It changes common-denominator bounds by at most a fixed
integer. Thus the basic CDT holonomy theorem cannot detect theta=q
from this module: all of those hypotheses already hold for q=0.
This is an exact normalization observation, not a numerical capacity
obstruction and not a theorem against adding genuinely new functions.

For a concrete diagnostic, let L_n=lcm(1,...,n). For every n>=1,

 L_(2n)^k [y^n]B^k is integral (k=1,2,3),
 3 L_(2n)^3 L_n [y^n]J is integral.                     (7)

These elementary sufficient bounds follow from the power series in
(3): each A denominator used in [y^n]J is at most2n, and integration
adds the divisor n. They are not optimal denominator claims.
Using the basis 1,B,B^2,B^3,3J gives CDT row costs0,2,4,6,7 and
tau=(3*2+5*4+7*6+9*7)/25=131/25. The standard two-slit domain
C minus ((-infinity,-1] union [1,infinity)) has conformal radius2,
via phi(w)=2w/(1+w^2). Hence this prescribed sufficient-bound test
has log2-tau<0 and does not even enter the theorem's useful range.
No conclusion about optimized arithmetic or other uniformizations is
drawn from that negative numerical margin. Equation(6) is the more
fundamental limitation of this particular proposal.

3. Linear differential regularization erases the assumed rational value

PROPOSITION. Let L be a linear differential operator with rational
functions of x as coefficients (even C(x) is allowed). If L(C_q) is
meromorphic near x=0, then L(1)=0. Consequently L(C_q)=L(C), independent
of q and of the additive connection constant theta.

Proof. Start on the branch with log(x) real for x>0. Let M_0 denote
positive continuation around0 and write c=2pi i. The F_j in (1) are
holomorphic at0, so Delta=(M_0-1)C_q is independent of q and theta.
Its derivative is

 Delta'(x)=-[(log(x)+c)^3-log(x)^3]/[6(1+x^2)].          (8)

Meromorphicity of L(C_q) at0 implies L(Delta)=0. Continue Delta
from the positive axis to a neighborhood of x=i with log(i)=pi i/2.
Its derivative has the nonzero residue

 Res_(x=i) Delta'
   =-[(5pi i/2)^3-(pi i/2)^3]/(12i)
   =31pi^3/24.                                        (9)

A small positive circuit about i, not winding around0, therefore adds
the nonzero constant 2pi i*(31pi^3/24)=31i pi^4/12 to Delta.
Rational coefficients of L are single-valued. Continuing L(Delta)=0
along that circuit and subtracting gives (31i pi^4/12)L(1)=0.
Therefore L(1)=0, proving the proposition. Possible poles of L cause
no difficulty: continuation paths avoid their finite set. QED.

The proposition also applies to rational differential operators in y:
y=((1-x)/(1+x))^2 is rational, and dy/dx is nonzero at x=0.
It therefore rules out retaining q while differentially removing the
y=1 logarithmic branch of q+J. Multiplication by rational functions
can be absorbed into L, so it does not evade the conclusion.

One can see the surviving branch directly. With s=1-y tending to0+
and x=s/4+O(s^2),

 C(x)=x[-log^3x+3log^2x-6log x+6]/6
                                      +O(x^3(1+|log x|^3)).        (10)

It contains -s log^3(s/4)/24 with nonzero coefficient. Choosing
q=theta changes the limiting value at y=1 to zero, but cannot remove
this branch. The proposition explains why a linear differential
cleanup cannot retain that favorable normalization.

4. What would suffice, and what is missing

A concrete sufficient arithmetic-holonomy construction would provide,
under theta in Q, rational holonomic germs f_1,...,f_m that are proved
Q(t)-independent, have a denominator array b satisfying CDT's column
condition, and share a meromorphic pullback phi for which

 |phi'(0)|>exp(tau(b)),
 integral_(T^2)log|phi(z)-phi(w)| dmu(z)dmu(w)
                    < m*(log|phi'(0)|-tau(b)).          (11)

Those inequalities contradict the cited theorem and prove irrationality.
They are a concrete sufficient theorem application, not a result proved
here. The missing new mechanism is a rationality-dependent gain in
actual continuation or in an independently justified arithmetic module.
The displayed C_q provides neither: (6) blocks extra rank, the genuine
logarithms block continuation across the endpoint, and the proposition
blocks a linear differential repair retaining its connection constant.

This does NOT address nonlinear rational expressions, extra integrations,
new geometric variations, nonsolvable local systems, or a construction
with different denominator structure. It makes no motivic inference from
a numerical rationality hypothesis and identifies no real and p-adic
periods. No credible full irrationality route emerged from this gate.
