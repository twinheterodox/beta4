ROUND 12 FRESH LANE: A QUARTER-GRID CMF TRANSFER GATE
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

OUTCOME

There is an exact quarter-grid construction in Q+Q beta(3)+Q beta(4)
with a rational projective coordinate for unnormalized beta(4). Its
beta(4) denominator is the same positive integer binomial sum as in
Leibtag et al., arXiv:2610.27799v1, Section 6.2. However, the two natural
extensions of that paper's numerator identity give DIFFERENT rational
numbers. Equality already fails exactly at n=1. The two prescribed n=1
primitive beta(4) forms are negative and have logarithmic absolute
values 166.95969... and 224.07073..., respectively, certified by Arb.

This does not prove an asymptotic obstruction, and no such claim is made.
The missing ingredient remains a second exact arithmetic presentation of
the SAME beta(4) approximants. Replacing harmonic numbers by quarter-grid
harmonic sums does not supply it. No direction or parameter scan was run.

2. The quarter-grid period identity, for every n>=0

Use rising factorials, and define the source rational function

 R_n(s)=(-1)^n (1+2n-s)_(14n) (1+n-s)_(16n)
              /[(s)_(11n+1)(s+n)_(11n+1)(s+2n)_(8n+2)].             (1)

For k=0,1,2 write its exact partial fractions as

 (-s)^k R_n(s)=sum_(j,i) c_(n,j,i)^(k)/(s+j)^i, 1<=i<=3.           (2)

Define row vectors, with coordinates k=0,1,2,

 a_(n,k)=3 sum_j c_(n,j,3)^(k),
 b_(n,k)=2 sum_j c_(n,j,2)^(k),
 d_(n,k)(alpha)=-sum_(j,i) i c_(n,j,i)^(k)
                                  H_(2n+j)^(i+1)(alpha),
 H_m^(r)(alpha)=sum_(ell=0)^(m-1) (ell+alpha)^(-r).                 (3)

Let L_n(alpha) be the row vector with coordinate

 L_(n,k)(alpha)=-sum_(m>=0) [d/ds ((-s)^k R_n(s))]
                                          evaluated at s=m+2n+alpha.

For alpha>0 all these sums converge absolutely. Since (2) is O(s^-2)
or smaller at infinity, sum_j c_(n,j,1)^(k)=0. Summing the differentiated
partial fractions therefore gives exactly

 L_n(alpha)=a_n zeta(4,alpha)+b_n zeta(3,alpha)+d_n(alpha).           (4)

No numerical period independence is used. Subtract alpha=3/4 from 1/4:

 V_n:=L_n(1/4)-L_n(3/4)
     =256 a_n beta(4)+64 b_n beta(3)+d_n^chi,
 d_n^chi=d_n(1/4)-d_n(3/4).                                      (5)

The identity zeta(r,1/4)-zeta(r,3/4)=4^r beta(r) follows directly by
splitting the absolutely convergent defining series into residues 1,3 mod4.

At n=0, R_0(s)=1/[s^3(s+1)], so the exact state is

 V_0=(768 beta(4)-128 beta(3)+128/9,
      -128 beta(3)+128/9,
      128/9).                                                    (6)

In particular (V_(0,0)-V_(0,1))/V_(0,2)=54 beta(4). This is a calibrated
rational projective coordinate, not a numerical identification of a limit.

By contrast, directly putting alternating signs on consecutive half-grid
samples of R_0 gives

 (48 beta(4)-16 beta(3)+8 beta(2)-4,
  -16 beta(3)+8 beta(2)-4,
  8 beta(2)-4).

Thus the source's formal rational last coordinate is lost in that naive
alternating transfer. The quarter difference repairs exactly this problem.
Equivalently, one can dilate the pole grid by two before alternating.

3. The denominator identity survives, but the numerator identity does not

Put u_n=(1,10n+1,(10n+1)^2) and

 Lambda_n=(8n)!(9n)!^2(11n)!^3(12n)!^3(13n)!^2
           /[6(16n)!^2(17n)!^2(18n)!^2(19n)!].                    (7)

The source identity is

 Q_n=Lambda_n det[b_n;a_n;u_n]=sum_(k=0)^(14n) B_(n,k),             (8)

where the B_(n,k) are positive integers and

 B_n(x)= (15n)!^2/(10n)!^3
         * Gamma(12n+x+1)Gamma(13n+x+1)Gamma(14n+x+1)
         /[Gamma(x+1)Gamma(n+x+1)Gamma(2n+x+1)
           Gamma(3n+x+1)Gamma(4n+x+1)
           Gamma(14n-x+1)Gamma(15n-x+1)].                         (9)

Use the holomorphic continuation at each integer -n<=k<=15n to define
B_(n,k)=B_n(k) and B'_(n,k)=B_n'(k). The endpoint extensions outside
0<=k<=14n have B_(n,k)=0, with potentially nonzero derivatives.

Because the rows a_n,b_n in (5) are unchanged, (8) is inherited exactly.
It supplies Q_n>0 for every n. Define the rational quarter dual numerator

 P_n^D=(Lambda_n/256) det[d_n^chi;b_n;u_n].                          (10)

Taking the determinant of (5) with b_n,u_n proves the actual error identity

 Q_n beta(4)-P_n^D=-(Lambda_n/256) det[V_n;b_n;u_n].                 (11)

This is a pure beta(4) form. It does not prove that the actual signed error
is eventually nonzero or sufficiently small after primitive reduction.

The tempting forward replacement is

 F_n(alpha)=sum_(k=-n)^(15n)
       [B_(n,k) H_(4n+k)^(4)(alpha)
        -(1/3)B'_(n,k) H_(4n+k)^(3)(alpha)],
 P_n^F=[F_n(1/4)-F_n(3/4)]/256.                                  (12)

The source numerator at alpha=1 equals Lambda_n det[d_n(1);b_n;u_n].
For quarter differences, P_n^D=P_n^F is FALSE: it fails exactly at n=1.
The reduced nonzero rational defect P_1^D-P_1^F is stored in
cmf_quarter_gate.json. Its approximate value is 0.0000016797244123165156.
Thus the source's two arithmetic presentations cannot simply be carried
over by replacing all harmonic numbers with quarter harmonic numbers.

4. The forward replacement is a separate exact scalar construction

The failure in Section 3 is not a failure of (12) to define an approximation
identity. In fact put

 T_n(z)=(-1)^n (15n)!^2/(10n)!^3
        * (z-8n)_(10n)(z-9n)_(10n)(z-10n)_(10n)
        /[(z+4n)_(14n+1)(z+3n)_(16n+1)].                          (13)

Then the full exact partial fraction decomposition is

 T_n(z)=sum_(k=-n)^(15n)
       [B_(n,k)/(z+4n+k)^2-B'_(n,k)/(z+4n+k)].                   (14)

Proof. Substitute x=-z-4n in (9). Twice applying Gamma reflection yields
B_n(x)=(sin(pi x)/pi)^2 T_n(-x-4n). At each integer k, the quadratic
zero of sine shows that the double-pole coefficient is B_n(k) and the
simple-pole coefficient is -B_n'(k) after changing coordinates to z.
There are no other poles, and both sides of (14) tend to zero at infinity.
This proves the rational identity. Alternatively, cancellation of the
displayed finite factorial products proves the same local coefficients.
Since T_n(z)=O(z^-2), sum_k B'_(n,k)=0. QED.

Differentiating (14) twice, summing, and using this zero residue sum gives

 (1/6)sum_(m>=0) T_n''(m+alpha)=Q_n zeta(4,alpha)-F_n(alpha).

Consequently

 Q_n beta(4)-P_n^F
   =(1/1536)sum_(m>=0)[T_n''(m+1/4)-T_n''(m+3/4)].                (15)

Thus P^F and P^D define two different exact beta(4) form sequences. The
forward source is a scalar double-pole construction, overlapping the
archived general mechanism. A second paired arithmetic presentation of
this precise sequence would be a new ingredient; (10) is not that pairing.

5. Actual primitive arithmetic and signed values at the prescribed gate

At n=1, Q_1=17922919673131438881235320. Rational P^D and P^F are computed
exactly from (10),(12). For each, clear denominators and divide by the
full gcd, obtaining A beta(4)-B with A>0 and gcd(A,B)=1.

The exact integers A,B are in cmf_quarter_gate.json. At 768-bit precision,
Arb certifies:

  construction     sign     log|A beta(4)-B|
  quarter dual      -       166.95969655625934022985...
  quarter forward   -       224.07073352222812688706...

Both absolute values exceed one. Before primitive integer normalization,
the corresponding errors Q_1 beta(4)-P_1 are approximately
  -1.6797244134381753e-6  (dual),
  -1.1216596554425937e-15 (forward).
These small rational forms do not become small integer forms.

Neither example is a theorem against its whole sequence. In particular,
the loss of exact numerator zeros on quarter samples is not by itself
proof that the actual signed sums fail to decay. No tail was dropped,
no later starting index was substituted, and all rational corrections
in (3), (12) were retained. The n=0 form is beta(4) itself; its size below
one has no irrationality consequence.

If beta(4)=u/v were rational, every nonzero primitive form would have
absolute value at least 1/v. A proof still needs infinitely many nonzero
forms tending to zero. This gate establishes neither requirement for
the growing sequences. There are no growing-degree polynomial claims.

6. Reproducibility, review, and closure

cmf_quarter_gate.py uses exact Fraction arithmetic for n=0,1 only. It
reproduces the source's q,p3,p4 identities at alpha=1 before evaluating
the quarter transfer. The log-derivative residue implementation is checked
by independent_checks.py using expanded SymPy rational identities for
all six R rows and both T kernels. verify_arb.py checks the full gcd and
rigorously encloses both signed primitive values.

The bounded gate is closed. Retain the calibrated period identity and
the exact distinction between the two sequences. Do not describe the
failed numerator transfer, or the two finite large primitive values, as
a general CMF or quarter-grid impossibility theorem. No further scan is
proposed without a new exact paired identity.
