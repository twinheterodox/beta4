ROUND10 FRESH GATE: TOPOLOGICAL INTEGER TRACES AND THE EVEN-BETA COMPONENT
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Outcome
-------
The integer-signature mechanism was worth an exact test: it starts with
integers rather than a rational integral with a denominator estimate.
Its direct even-power extension fails BEFORE an analytic/arithmetic
balance: every nonzero value lies in a cyclotomic eigenspace disjoint
from Q, and its rational trace is zero. Rational linear combinations
across arbitrary odd levels do not repair this. The surviving limit is
also beta4/pi^4, not beta4. This is a scoped obstruction to the explicit
cotangent construction, not to TQFT, signatures or geometric methods.
No novelty claim is made; character-parity obstructions are classical.

1. The potentially useful arithmetic principle
----------------------------------------------
Suppose an explicitly integral sequence N_m had a proved expansion

 N_m = A(m)*theta + B(m) + E_m,
 A,B in Q[m],  E_m -> 0,  E_m != 0 eventually.

Then theta is irrational: under theta=u/v, a FIXED integer D clears all
coefficients of A*theta+B. Hence D*E_m is a nonzero integer tending to0.
This requires no exponential height estimate. More generally the same
argument works on an arithmetic progression if the coefficient functions
are rational quasipolynomials. A limit N_m/m^d by itself is far weaker:
floor(theta*m^d) supplies an integer sequence for every real theta.

The hoped-for new input was a topological index/signature with a complete,
calibrated beta4 expansion of this kind. No such input was found.

2. Primary source and the explicit candidate
-------------------------------------------
Marché--Masbaum, Signatures in TQFT: asymptotics and modularity,
manuscript dated21 February2026, Corollary3.2, proves for odd coprime
0<q<p that the SU2 genus-two signature is

 Sigma_3(p,q)=(2/p) sum_(1<=n<p,n odd)
                 cot(pi*n/(2p))^3 / sin(pi*n*q/p).             (1)

Thus (1) is an actual integer. Their asymptotic limit and modularity
investigation concern this cubic expression; they do not assert the
even-beta construction tested here. Sections5 and6 discuss extensions.

Source: https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~julien.marche/papiers/Sign_Mod.pdf
Reading scope is in 00_archive_comparison.txt. The related papers
arXiv:2511.13129 and2602.16159 were screened by abstract/bibliography only;
no theorem from them is used below.

Define Sigma_s by replacing power3 in (1) with integer s>=1.
The first arithmetic test should precede any numerical optimization.

3. Exact Galois parity gate, including mixed levels
--------------------------------------------------
Let p>1 be odd and gcd(p,q)=1. Set
 w=exp(2*pi*i/(4p)), z=w^2=exp(pi*i/p), K=Q(z)=Q(zeta_p).
For each summand, with t=z^n, the elementary identities are

 cot(pi*n/(2p))=i*(t+1)/(t-1),
 1/sin(pi*n*q/p)=2*i/(t^q-t^(-q)).

Therefore

 Sigma_s(p,q)=(4/p)*i^(s+1)
              sum_(n odd,0<n<p) ((z^n+1)/(z^n-1))^s
                                  /(z^(nq)-z^(-nq))
              belongs to i^(s+1)K.                           (2)

All denominators are nonzero. The automorphism sigma of Q(w) given by
 w -> w^(2p+1) fixes z and sends i=w^p to -i. It exists since2p+1 is
coprime to4p. Thus

              sigma(Sigma_s)=(-1)^(s+1) Sigma_s.             (3)

When s is even, (3) proves that Sigma_s is rational only if it is zero.
It also proves its relative trace to K, and hence its absolute rational
trace, is zero. This is an exact all-level statement, not a sample.

For a rational linear combination of even-power sums at finitely many
odd levels p_j, put M=lcm(p_j). All terms lie in i*Q(zeta_M).
The involution fixing Q(zeta_M) and negating i negates the combination.
Consequently a rational linear combination cannot be a nonzero rational
number. This covers different even powers and different q_j as well.
It does NOT cover nonlinear combinations or other topological observables.

4. The beta4 calibration really occurs, but in the wrong component
-----------------------------------------------------------------
For each odd p>=3 choose the prescribed odd integer
 q=(p+1)/2 if p=1 mod4, and q=(p-1)/2 if p=3 mod4.
Then gcd(p,q)=1 and for odd n,

 sin(pi*n*q/p)=chi_(-4)(n)*cos(pi*n/(2p)).

Thus, putting h=pi/(2p),

 Sigma_s(p,q)=(2/p)sum_(n odd,0<n<p)
                          chi_(-4)(n)*cos(nh)^(s-1)/sin(nh)^s. (4)

For s>=2 the positive factor cos(x)^(s-1)/sin(x)^s is strictly
decreasing on(0,pi/2). Pairing consecutive alternating terms proves
Sigma_s>0, including when the number of terms is odd. Hence Sigma_4 is
nonzero and IRRATIONAL for every p>=3. This is irrationality of the finite
algebraic approximants, not of their limit.

Since sin(x)>=2x/pi on[0,pi/2], dominated convergence gives

       p^(1-s) Sigma_s(p,q) -> (2^(s+1)/pi^s)*beta(s).        (5)

In particular Sigma_4/p^3 ->32*theta/pi^4. For comparison the cubic
case has critical limit16*beta(3)/pi^3=1/2 and actual integer signatures.

There is also a rigorously justified two-term refinement:

 Sigma_4(p,q)=(32/pi^4)*theta*p^3
                     -(20/(3*pi^2))*beta(2)*p + O(p^(-1)).  (6)

Indeed cos(x)^3/sin(x)^4=x^(-4)-(5/6)x^(-2)+R(x), where R extends
smoothly to[0,pi/2]. Its alternating samples have uniformly bounded
sum by bounded total variation. The alternating infinite-series tails
of n^(-4) and n^(-2) contribute O(p^(-1)) after their respective scalings.
Formula(6) therefore follows without interchanging a divergent series.
The small diagnostics suggest the next term43/(360p); it is not needed
and is not claimed as a proved asymptotic here.

5. Why the desired contradiction does not follow
------------------------------------------------
First, Sigma_4 is never an integer, and every rational linear trace
projection described in Section3 destroys it. Second, rationality of
theta would NOT make theta/pi^4 rational. The normalized coefficient in
(5)-(6) cannot be substituted into the elementary principle of Section1.
Third, (6) includes beta(2); it must be eliminated rather than ignored.
The prescribed difference Sigma_4(3p)-3 Sigma_4(p) removes its p term,
but it remains in the same anti-invariant component and retains pi^(-4).

An algebraic approximant can instead normalize pi by sin(pi/(2p)), but
then an algebraic norm argument needs control of ALL conjugates and the
factor v^degree under theta=u/v. No favorable norm estimate is supplied
by the signature construction. This is a missing ingredient, not a
general impossibility theorem for algebraic approximants.

A successful repair would need a different, explicitly integral global
observable, a calibration to UNNORMALIZED theta, and a full small
nonzero remainder. Merely proving more terms of (6), increasing genus,
or changing the cotangent index does not supply these three inputs.
