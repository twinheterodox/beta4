1. The norm theorem

The finite Newton expansion gives ||P||_Int=max|Delta^j P(0)|_p:
finite differences cannot enlarge the sup norm, and every binomial
polynomial has sup norm1 on Z_p. Thus the functional norm is exactly
the maximum of |tau(binomial(x,j))|_p through degree n. This argument
does not identify the integer-valued norm with the monomial Gauss norm.

The generating function -log(1+z)^3/[12(1+z/2)] follows with the
displayed derivative normalization. If a,b,c are the valuations of
three positive log indices, their total degree is at least
p^a+p^b+p^c. When a>=b+2, replacing(a,b) by(a-1,b+1) decreases this
cost by (p-1)(p^(a-1)-p^b)>0. The minimum for total valuation h is
therefore exactly m_p(h). It is strictly increasing with h.

At the threshold m_p(h), a term of valuation -h must have the three
indices equal to the minimizing powers themselves; it cannot have a
nonconstant geometric-series index. Every other term has valuation at
least-h+1. The number of leading terms is1 or3. The threshold degree
is odd, so the log-cube leading sign is positive. Consequently

 p^h a_(m_p(h)) == -c_h/12 mod p,
 c_h=1 when h=0 mod3, and3 otherwise.

For p>=5 this is a unit, proving sharpness and excluding cancellation.
The claim includes h0: a3=-1/12 and m_p(0)=3. Primes2,3 are properly
excluded. The stated globally integer-valued denominator consequence
follows because the threshold binomial polynomials are also globally
integer-valued, so each individual prime exponent is attained.

Independent exact reproduction used unsigned Stirling numbers:
 log(1+z)^3=6 sum_(n>=3)(-1)^(n-3)c(n,3)z^n/n!.
This differs from both the Euler-moment and direct log-convolution
implementations in gate.py. It verifies282 norm instances through
degree96 for p5,7,11 and13 sharp residue assertions. The all-degree
claim rests on the argument above, not these finite checks.

2. Full rank versus proper subspaces

For a basis change T, the polynomial Gram determinant multiplies by
(det T)^2. Primitive reduction removes that rational scalar exactly.
Finite homogeneous congruences retain a full rational span since they
contain an integer multiple of the original lattice. Therefore they
cannot change the complete determinant's primitive polynomial.
This does not apply to an intentionally lower-dimensional source,
different poles, or a changed multiplier. The worker keeps these cases
separate and does not infer a general lattice obstruction.

3. Proper-source gate, checked independently

The identity
 F_p(-x^2)=-x^2(x^(p-1)-1)^2
is exact. It proves p^2 divisibility for all x in Z_p by separating
units and nonunits. The source F_p*span{1,t} differs from the control
D_p*span{1,t}; the source normalization is a common scalar only.

All three entries of the2x2 Gram matrix have numerator degree at most
2p+2, below K=2p+3, so their polynomial quotient is zero. This allows
a particularly short independent computation using

 D_K'(-(j+1/2)^2)=(-1)^j (K-1-j)!(K+j)!/(2j+1).

Write z_j=-(j+1/2)^2, o_j=2j+1, T_j=sum_(r<j)(-1)^r/(2r+1)^4.
For source P divided by N, the entry with power t^ell is exactly

 sum_(j=0)^(K-1) P(z_j)^2 z_j^ell
       /[N^2 (K-1-j)!(K+j)!]
       *[2 o_j^4(X-T_j)-(-1)^j].

Taking ell0,1,2 and the2x2 determinant reproduces every rational
coefficient, every primitive integer quadratic, every primitive
multiplier, and min coefficient valuation-16, for both p5,7 and both
Fermat and central sources. No polynomial-division implementation or
Euler recurrence from the producer was imported.

At theta, the archived weight is strictly positive on y>0, D_K(y^2)
is positive, and a nonzero member of either source space has finitely
many real zeros. The Gram matrices are consequently positive definite.
All integrals are finite: the weight is bounded near0 and decays
exponentially at infinity. Thus nonvanishing is analytic, independent
of any period independence assumption. The saved1600-bit Arb intervals
also certify the four actual primitive values as positive and >1.

The matched source normalizations have the same p-valuation since
v_p((2p)!)=2. The shared raw valuation-16 is therefore a legitimate
local comparison. It gives no new target-prime saving in these tests.
It does not imply an asymptotic growth theorem for other source choices.

The added leading-coefficient comparison is also correct: the top Newton
coefficient of a degree2p integer-valued polynomial is (2p)! times its
leading monomial coefficient. Both sources attain the permitted p^2
denominator. For the old source, its integer-valued property follows
directly from D_p(-x^2)/(2p)!=(-1)^p binomial(x+p-1/2,2p), since
x+p-1/2 is in Z_p for odd p. Thus changed root placement, rather than a
stronger leading-coefficient denominator bound, is the tested difference.
