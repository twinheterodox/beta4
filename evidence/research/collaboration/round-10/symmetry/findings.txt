ROUND10: A THREE-BRANCH WHIPPLE SYMMETRY GATE
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Outcome and scope

One prescribed nonsymmetric fixture supplies genuinely different rational
pole-splitting jets. It does not supply a new arithmetic mechanism. Its
three formal coefficient vectors are independent; the stated 120-element
Thomae group supplies no relation between different branches at this base
point. Six prescribed primitive eliminations all have absolute value >1.
No scaled parameter ray was tested. These are statements about this gate,
not a no-go theorem for the general multiparameter hypergeometric method.

1. Explicit transformation and proposed mechanism

Write h=(h0;h1,h2,h3,h4) and, where the series converges, set

 S(h)=sum_(t>=0) (-1)^t (h0+2t) Gamma(t+h0)/Gamma(t+1)
                 * product_(i=1)^4 Gamma(t+hi)/Gamma(t+1+h0-hi).

Whipple's transformation gives the normalized identity

 S(h)/product Gamma(hi)
  = 3F2(a1,a2,a3;b2,b3;1)/[Gamma(b2)Gamma(b3)Gamma(s)],       (1)

 a1=1+h0-h1-h2, a2=h3, a3=h4,
 b2=1+h0-h1, b3=1+h0-h2, s=b2+b3-a1-a2-a3.

Together with permutations of h1,...,h4, numerator permutations of the
3F2 generate the classical group of order120. For example, a1<->a3 is

 h -> (1+2h0-h1-h2-h4;
       1+h0-h2-h4, 1+h0-h1-h4, h3, 1+h0-h1-h2).           (2)

The exact equality for UNNORMALIZED sums is

 S(g h)= [product Gamma((g h)i)/product Gamma(hi)] S(h).     (3)

Thus derivatives of the gamma quotient must be retained. An identity
for normalized series is not an equality of unnormalized Taylor jets.

Assume h0,h4 are integers, h1,h2,h3 are half-integers, and the positive
integer lengths below hold. Moving a pair hi->hi+z,hj->hj-z gives

 R_ij(t,z)=(h0+2t)(t+1)_(h0-1)
  / [(t+hi+z)_d (t+hj-z)_d
     product_(k!=i,j)(t+hk)_(1+h0-2hk)],
 d=1+h0-hi-hj.                                            (4)

This is an exact gamma cancellation, not a truncated deformation. It
retains every integer numerator zero. Unequal half-indices give three
different rational branches. The intended new ingredient was a fixed
integral relation supplied by (1)-(3), cancelling the unwanted beta2
jet while retaining beta4, or genuinely forcing simultaneous coefficient
divisibility. Generic rational cofactors do not provide that ingredient.

2. The decisive prescribed fixture

Take h=(7;3/2,5/2,7/2,3), chosen before any theta evaluation. At z=0
all three branches equal

 R0(t)=512(t+1)(t+2)(t+5)(t+6)
       /[(2t+3)(2t+5)^2(2t+7)^2(2t+9)^2(2t+11)].

The alternating sum of R0 is -12773/720+(155/8) beta2.
Let L_ij=sum_(t>=0)(-1)^t [z^2]R_ij(t,z). In the order
(rational,beta2,beta4), exact partial fractions give

 L12: (-62552537/648000,  695/32,    155/2),
 L13: (-71583467/648000, 10637/288,  155/2),
 L23: (-2507141/25920,    2105/96,   155/2).               (5)

Reflection R_ij(-7-t,z)=R_ij(t,z) cancels odd beta orders. The fixed
factor 2t+7 cancels the central pole of the unsplit denominator, so the
z^2 jets have pole order at most4. All series here converge absolutely
and locally uniformly in z near0: R_ij=O(t^-4), and its z^2 coefficient
is O(t^-6). Termwise differentiation and partial-fraction summation are
therefore valid. Formula (5) is a formal coefficient computation; no
numerical independence of 1,beta2,beta4 is assumed or proved.

The determinant of the three coefficient vectors is25792/6075, not0.
This rules out a zero relation between these three FORMAL vectors only.
It does not rule out a relation between their numerical period values.

3. Actual group gate, retaining normalization

The c-parameter permutations used in group_gate.py are those of [Z02,
Section3], equivalently [KZ18, pp.5-7]. They generate exactly120 index
permutations and60 distinct ordered h-images at this fixture.

For each of the three entire affine lines h(z), every group element was
tested. Retain an image only if its base h0 is7, its base multiset of the
other four h's is {3/2,5/2,7/2,3}, and its tangent, after reordering to
the original distinct base entries, is +/- one of the three pair-split
tangents. The only resulting branch relations are each branch to itself
with the same direction and gamma quotient1. Its Taylor coefficients
are exactly(1,0,0). There is no cross-branch stabilizer relation in this
specified group and fixture. This is not a classification of all
hypergeometric identities, nor does it exclude arithmetic information
from transformed base points elsewhere in the orbit.

5. Verification and reproduction

 fixture.py: exact SymPy branch construction, direct parameter derivative
 and independent log-derivative formula, complete partial fractions.
 independent_check.py: separate standard-library Fraction implementation;
 direct truncated product division at20 points certifies each whole
 rational identity after a degree19 common-denominator bound. It rebuilds
 all three formal vectors, their determinant and pair eliminations.
 group_gate.py: exact120-element group closure, affine-line image tests,
 gamma-factor ratios and their first three Taylor coefficients.
 verify_arb.py: certified signed values from Hurwitz-zeta beta formulas.
