Let f(y)=1/(exp(2pi y)-1), w(y)=-y^4 f'''(y)/3>0, and
nu(R)=integral_0^infinity R(y^2)w(y)dy. Integration by parts and Hermite's
formula give
 nu(t^e)=(2e+4)! |B_(2e+2)|/[6(2e+2)!],
 nu(1/(t+a^2))=a^3 zeta(4,a)-1/(2a)-1/3.
The imported formulas being checked are DLMF25.11.E29 and E3. No theorem
from the recent claimed irrationality proofs is required for this gate.

Put a_j=(2j+1)/4, 0<=j<K, D_K(t)=product_j(t+a_j^2). Then
 zeta(4,a_j)=120 zeta4+128(-1)^j theta - finite rational tail.
Let S_K be the degree<K interpolation polynomial satisfying
 S_K(-a_j^2)=1/a_j.
All divided differences of (-t)^(-1/2) at negative nodes are positive.
Its Newton expansion therefore gives S_K(t)>0 for t>=0.

Candidate cancellation identity:
 sum_j a_j^2 (-a_j^2)^m / D_K'(-a_j^2)=0, 0<=m<=K-3.
Reason: D' introduces a factor (2j+1); after extending the sum to the
negative mirror nodes, this is an odd polynomial of degree2m+3 against
an alternating binomial coefficient of order2K-1. The full finite
difference vanishes when2m+3<2K-1, and the two halves agree.

If checked, H_ij=nu(S_K(t)t^(i+j)/D_K(t)) for 0<=i,j<r,
2r-2<=K-3, is a positive Gram matrix in Q+Q theta. Its theta coefficient
is itself definite: (-1)^j/D'_K(-a_j^2)>0. Thus det H has exact degree r
and is positive at theta. This is a structural gate, not an arithmetic
estimate; S_K has high degree and may destroy any presumed analytic gain.

Opening refinement before the determinant test: the cancellation above is
just Lagrange interpolation, not a special binomial identity! At the nodes
t_j=-a_j^2, a_j^2 t_j^m=-t_j^(m+1), and sum t_j^d/D'(t_j)=0 for d<K-1.
Thus it holds for ANY distinct positive rational a_j when the nuisance
period coefficient is a_j^3. The dense quarter grid is used to have exactly
the two periods zeta4 and theta, not to establish the cancellation itself.
