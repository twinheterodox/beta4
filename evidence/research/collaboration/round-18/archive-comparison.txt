1. Positive rank-one moment model and its first variation

The direct model has moments
 m_j=integral_0^1 x^(2j)(-log x)^3/[6(1+x^2)] dx,
and coefficient matrix (-1)^(i+j) for beta4. Its affine degree follows
exactly from rank one. Reducing the rationality denominator from b^n to b
is real for this model, but it is not a new principle in the project.

arithmetic-followup.txt Sections2.1-2.2 already develop positive rank-one
Gram/Hankel factors and show why raw capacity decay does not determine the
primitive form. deep-investigation.txt Section5.4 has a different inverse-
moment rank-one construction. These weights and grids are NOT identified
with the explorer's precise ordinary moment matrix. Their family-specific
obstructions must not be transplanted to it. The overlap is the mechanism;
the explorer supplied no new all-index arithmetic improvement for its variant.

The purportedly more interesting arcsine parameter family IS exactly in
the archive. In arcsine-contact.txt Section1 substitute u=a^2. Since
arcsin|t|-pi/2=-arccos|t| and cosine is even, the measures agree identically.
The rational moment recurrence, its first jet, and the boundary period
4 sum chi4(r)/(r^2-a^2) are identical. The archive additionally proves
the full boundary recurrences and a dilogarithm companion, and records
202 finite primitive determinant tests. This is an exact rediscovery,
not a conclusion from similar terminology.

The explorer's separate ordinary-moment Wronskian model has exact target
support. Its elimination aH'-a'H is the standard first-jet/cofactor
mechanism, also present with other coefficient families in round14.
I have NOT proved equality of its full coefficient sequence with any
archived sequence. Round14's particular multiplier obstruction and the
arcsine high-column prime theorem do not automatically cover it.

The one-row derivative observation does not by itself preserve the
divisibility of an undifferentiated determinant. The explorer provides
no arithmetic identity repairing that loss or an unpriced elimination
from a higher-degree polynomial in beta2.

Important correction to the assessment evidence

The explorer's numerical tables clear the least coefficient denominator D,
but do NOT remove the common integer numerator factor g. Therefore those
tables alone cannot support a statement about primitive forms. This was
checked rather than silently accepting the negative conclusion.

primitive_check.py reads the existing exact coefficients, clears D, removes
the full gcd, and evaluates the resulting A beta4+B with Arb at precision
chosen from coefficient height. No matrix size or family was added.
At n=12 the corrected normalized logarithms are

  model              before gcd       after full gcd
  direct Hankel       7.036743          6.016276
  first variation     6.271803          4.303072

The content corrections are substantial but do not reverse these finite
failures. At n=3,6,9,12 both primitive values are >1 by interval arithmetic.
All 24 existing forms have certified nonzero values. The full record also
retains n=1,2, which must not be misrepresented as irrationality evidence.
No general primitive-growth theorem follows from these examples.

A small structural observation for adversarial checking

There may be a clean all-index nonvanishing argument for the Wronskian,
so lack of such a theorem should not be used carelessly as the decisive
reason to reject it. The moment deformation has the positive weight
 w_u(x)=sinh(sqrt(u)*(-log x))/[sqrt(u)(1+x^2)],
with its analytic value at u=0. Its first derivative at zero is exactly
the positive weight-four density. For P of degree<n in x^2 with P(-1)=1,
the constrained minimum is H_n(u)/a_n(u). Differentiating the finite
positive quadratic minimization at zero should give
 W_n/a_n(0)^2=integral P_(n,0)(x^2)^2 w_0'(x) dx>0.
The minimizer is unique, and the derivative of its constraint is zero.
It also gives an error at least the direct weight-four constrained minimum.
This is an elementary proof candidate to check, not an arithmetic saving.
It explains why a smaller raw Wronskian determinant is not itself evidence
of a smaller scalar approximation error. The reviewer may verify or refute
this observation; it is not needed for the existing finite Arb enclosures.

2. Holonomy/top-extension jet

The proposed parameter functions J(a) and F(u) have infinitely many genuine
finite poles; they are not rational-coefficient linear-holonomic inputs in
those variables. This is a scoped elementary objection, not an objection
to finding another geometric variable or to arithmetic holonomy itself.

The raw x-system retains lower beta jets. However, the archive already has
a repair of the rational-jet problem: round12/geometry/findings.txt gives
C(x)=F0-log(x)F1+log(x)^2 F2/2-log(x)^3 F3/6, with C(1)=beta4 and
C'(x)=-log(x)^3/[6(1+x^2)]. Under beta4 rational its whole local jet at 1
is rational. Thus the diagnosis must go beyond saying lower jets cannot
be removed. The same record proves that changing the rational constant
does not improve the module/continuation data, and that the specified
linear differential removal of endpoint branching erases that constant.

Round13's level32 rank/cost comparison is a separate, narrowly scoped
failure of given functions, coordinates and estimates. None closes all
holonomy methods. The fresh seed supplies no new rationality-sensitive
module, geometric quotient or arithmetic/analytic comparison beyond these
known missing objects. No concrete proposal survives from this seed.

3. Cyclotomic distribution or period projector

The odd projector Li4(i)-Li4(-i)=2i beta4 is already the defining germ
F(x)=sum chi4(m)x^m/m^4 used throughout the archive (for example round12
geometry). The fresh seed does not specify an additional correspondence
or a new integral lattice. Earlier functional-equation, ladder, topological
parity and norm work in archive groups4,8,9,10 has specific results and
obstructions; these do not prove that every possible projector fails.
The present rejection is absence of a new specified mechanism or saving,
not an illegitimate use of those narrower no-go results.

Provisional verdict for stage3

No proposal currently justifies another construction campaign. The closest
candidate is the first-variation mechanism, whose exact calibration works
but whose arcsine family is already developed and whose explicit ordinary-
moment arithmetic test remains poor even after the normalization correction.
The fresh reviewer should attack this verdict and the comparison, including
the nonvanishing observation above, rather than merely endorse it. Any
retained proposal needs a concrete new arithmetic mechanism and cheap test;
an unquantified hope of additional gcds does not meet that threshold.
