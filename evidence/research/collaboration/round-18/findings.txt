ROUND 18: BOUNDED CREATIVE RESET — RECOMMENDATION TO PARK BETA(4)
31 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Verdict

Park the project after this assessment. None of the three proposed
mechanisms supplies a sufficiently concrete new ingredient to justify
another construction campaign. The strongest candidate has acquired a
complete analytic description, but no useful all-index arithmetic bound.
This is a research decision based on the present evidence. It is not an
impossibility theorem for these families, for determinant or holonomy
methods, or for proving the irrationality of beta(4).

The phase followed the specified order: a fresh explorer without the
failed-construction archive; coordinator comparison with the complete
archive map and the relevant underlying arguments; then a separate fresh
adversarial reviewer. The reviewer was asked to overturn the provisional
negative verdict if possible. Its mathematical derivations and independent
exact arithmetic, rather than agreement with that verdict, are the useful
evidence. The complete proof and reconstruction are in
adversarial/assessment.txt and adversarial/checks.py.

2. The closest candidate: exact target, sign, and analytic size

Write theta=beta(4). For |u|<1 define

 F(u)=sum_(k>=0) (-1)^k/((2k+1)^2-u),
 q_j(u)=1/((2j+1)^2-u),
 w_u(x)=sinh(sqrt(u)*(-log x))/(sqrt(u)*(1+x^2)).

The quotient has its analytic value at u=0. Then F(0)=beta(2),
F'(0)=theta, and the moments m_j(u)=integral x^(2j)w_u(x)dx satisfy
m_j+m_(j+1)=q_j. The integrals are analytic near zero. Positivity of
the density is used for 0<=u<1; it must not be asserted for negative u.

For d=n-1, let c_j=q_j and B_ij=q_(i+j)+q_(i+j+1), with 0<=i,j<d.
Put a=det B and r=c^T B^(-1)c, taking a=1,r=0 when n=1. In the basis
1,(1+t),(1+t)t,..., with t=x^2, the moment Gram matrix is

                         [ F  c^T ]
                         [ c   B  ].

Its determinant is therefore H_n=a(F-r). The first exterior derivative
eliminates beta(2) identically:

 W_n=a(0)H_n'(0)-a'(0)H_n(0)
    =a(0)^2 (theta-r_n'(0)).                              (1)

Here a,r and their derivatives are rational at zero. No independence
assumption on any beta values is used. At n=2, for example,
W_2=(2/81)(50 theta-49).

The coordinator proposed a variational sign argument, which the reviewer
checked and developed. For polynomials P of degree<n with P(-1)=1,

 H_n(u)/a_n(u)=min_P integral_0^1 P(x^2)^2 w_u(x)dx.

The unique minimizer P_(n,u) is analytic near zero. Differentiating the
minimum makes the variation of P disappear by stationarity. Thus

 E_n:=W_n/a_n(0)^2
     =integral_0^1 P_(n,0)(x^2)^2 (-log x)^3/[6(1+x^2)]dx
     >0                                                   (2)

for EVERY n. Nonvanishing is settled for this ordinary-moment model.
It is not a missing condition that may be invoked to reject the model.

Let lambda_(n,s) denote the analogous constrained minimum with density
(-log x)^(s-1)/[(s-1)!(1+x^2)], for s=2,4. The direct weight-four
model has scalar error lambda_(n,4), and (2) gives E_n>=lambda_(n,4).
The first variation is therefore no better analytically after its theta
coefficient is normalized to one. Its advantage in the examples is
arithmetic, not a smaller scalar error.

The reviewer supplied, and the coordinator checked, the elementary limit

 lim log(lambda_(n,2))/n = lim log(lambda_(n,4))/n
                        = lim log(E_n)/n
                        = -2 log(3+sqrt(8))
                        = -3.525494348... .                (3)

The proof uses a normalized shifted Chebyshev polynomial for the upper
bound. For the lower bound, restrict either positive measure to
[epsilon,1-epsilon] and apply the elementary Legendre evaluation bound
at -1, then let epsilon decrease to zero. The same norm bound controls
P_(n,0) on [0,1] from its weight-two norm on [0,1-epsilon], giving the
upper bound for E_n. All constants and limit orders are in the review.
No orthogonal-polynomial asymptotic theorem is imported.

Consequently the quadratic logarithmic decay of the RAW determinant is
not the scalar approximation budget: its rational theta coefficient
carries the same leading determinant scale. After normalization, the
available error is exponential in n. Older endpoint Christoffel bounds
do not apply to this model, whose constraint is at -1 outside [0,1].

3. The arithmetic correction and the precise unresolved condition

Write r_n'(0)=p_n/A_n in lowest terms, with A_n>0. The primitive form is

                   L_n=A_n theta-p_n=A_n E_n>0.            (4)

If theta=h/b were rational, b L_n would be a positive integer. An infinite
sequence with L_n tending to zero would therefore prove irrationality.
Equations (3)-(4) give

        log L_n=log A_n-2n log(3+sqrt(8))+o(n).             (5)

On the proposed subsequence, a necessary exponential-scale condition for
L_n->0 is limsup log A_n/n <= 2 log(3+sqrt(8)). A strict inequality is
sufficient. Equality is unresolved by the exponential rate alone: the
sublinear terms must also be controlled. No useful all-index reduced-
denominator bound of either kind has been established.

The explorer's first numerical table cleared the common coefficient
denominator but did not divide the common integer numerator factor.
That table was not primitive evidence. The coordinator removed the FULL
gcd and evaluated the resulting forms with Arb at height-based precision.
The reviewer then reconstructed a,b,a',b' and all primitive pairs from
rational Schur complements using Fraction Gaussian elimination, separately
from the explorer's determinant and row-replacement implementation.

The corrected values are:

 n    log(direct primitive value)/n^2    log(L_n)/n^2
 3                 1.467411                 1.427349
 6                 4.439663                 3.405164
 9                 5.512341                 4.003977
12                 6.016276                 4.303072

At n=12 the first variation has log A_n=660.303908 and
log E_n=-40.661541. The original uncorrected log value per n^2 was
6.271803; removing the full content changes it to 4.303072. That is a
substantial improvement, and the first variation is substantially better
than the direct primitive form. Nevertheless, all 20 forms at n=3,...,12
are certified greater than one. The four values at n=1,2 below one say
nothing decisive about the unknown rational denominator b.

These are finite failures, not a proof of quadratic-exponential reduced
denominator growth. The observed dyadic content and direct-model prime
pole saturation are also only finite observations. No integer-multiplier
obstruction, raw denominator bound, or statement from another coefficient
family is being promoted to a theorem about all primitive rescalings here.
The frozen explorer report is retained; this section supplies the needed
normalization correction without rewriting its history.

4. What was already in the archive, and what remains different

The arcsine parameter realization is EXACTLY arcsine-contact.txt Section 1
under u=a^2: arcsin|t|-pi/2=-arccos|t| and cosine is even. The measures,
moment recurrence, and boundary 4F(u) coincide. That record already has
further boundary identities and finite primitive tests. This is an exact
rediscovery, not a new mechanism supported merely by different notation.

The ordinary-moment first-variation sequence above is a distinct explicit
model. Its rank-one/Schur structure and first-jet elimination have archived
analogues, but no full coefficient-sequence identification was proved.
The arcsine prime obstruction and round14's cofactor multiplier theorem
have different hypotheses and do not dispose of this sequence. Its
remaining arithmetic possibility is real but currently unsupported.

Rank-one dependence improves a hypothetical rationality denominator from
b^n to b. In a raw n^2-scale comparison both logarithmic costs are lower
order, so that observation alone cannot repair the leading arithmetic
deficit. Differentiating only one determinant row likewise does not prove
that congruence divisibility survives target elimination.

5. The other two seeds

Holonomy: J(a)=integral x^(a-1)/(1+x^2)dx and F(u) have infinitely many
genuine finite poles. They are not rational-coefficient linear-holonomic
inputs in those parameter variables. This leaves other geometric variables
open. Moreover, the archive already repairs the simple lower-jet objection:
round12/geometry/findings.txt constructs C with C(1)=theta and
C'(x)=-(log x)^3/[6(1+x^2)]. If theta is rational, its full jet at 1 is
rational. Its differential span and continuation data, however, do not
gain from adding that rational constant. The archive's monodromy argument
also proves that its specified rational linear differential cleanup erases
the constant carrying theta. Those are scoped results. The fresh proposal
supplied no new rationality-sensitive system or quantitative advantage
beyond them, so there is no concrete next object to test.

Cyclotomic projection: Li_4(i)-Li_4(-i)=2i theta is the defining odd beta
component, already used throughout the archive. The complementary sum
is -7 zeta(4)/64. Removing that even component supplies neither a relation
with 1 nor an improved integral lattice. No additional correspondence or
arithmetic saving was specified. No general motivic or projector no-go
theorem is claimed.

6. Why stop, and what could justify reopening

The strongest case for continuing is the ordinary first variation:
exact target isolation, all-index positivity, sharp scalar decay, an
external evaluation point, and improved finite primitive arithmetic.
All of those favorable facts have been retained in this assessment.

What is absent is a specific identity, congruence, or other mechanism
controlling the REDUCED denominator in (4) at a plausible useful rate.
Simply restating the needed height lemma, expecting a larger future gcd,
changing rational basis, or computing larger determinants would repeat
the central difficulty. No substantially new mechanism survived the
fresh exploration and comparison. Thus no next investigation is retained.

Reopening would be justified by a concrete arithmetic identity with a
quantitative reason for favorable height, or a different construction in
which assuming theta rational produces a new exploitable constraint.
For the first variation, any proposed identity can first be checked
against the existing 24 exact forms. This is a criterion for evaluating
new information, not authorization for another search campaign.
