ROUND 18 — FRESH ADVERSARIAL ASSESSMENT
31 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No construction campaign.

VERDICT

Park the current beta(4) project. I tried to overturn that recommendation
and found useful corrections to its supporting argument, but no concrete
new mechanism with a justified inexpensive next investigation. This is a
decision about the present proposals, not a theorem that these methods or
beta(4) irrationality are impossible.

The strongest candidate, the ordinary-moment first variation, is better
understood than the explorer's report suggests. Its target is exactly
beta(4), its nonvanishing holds at EVERY index, and its scalar error has a
sharp exponential rate. Full primitive normalization also improves it
substantially compared with the direct moment model. These are real
advantages. They do not supply the missing primitive denominator saving.
The arcsine realization is an exact archived family; the ordinary-moment
sequence is not identified with an archived coefficient sequence.

The decisive remaining question is now precise: can the reduced
denominator of r_n'(0), defined below, have a strictly favorable exponential
rate on an infinite subsequence? No such arithmetic mechanism was supplied
or found here. The n<=12 results do not answer that all-index question.

1. TARGET CALIBRATION AND AN INDEPENDENT RATIONAL FORMULA

Write theta=beta(4), t=x^2, and

 F(u)=sum_(k>=0) (-1)^k/((2k+1)^2-u),
 q_j(u)=1/((2j+1)^2-u),
 w_u(x)=sinh(sqrt(u)*(-log x))/(sqrt(u)*(1+x^2)),
 m_j(u)=integral_0^1 x^(2j)w_u(x)dx.

At u=0 use the analytic value of the quotient. Expansion or the elementary
Laplace integral proves m_j+m_(j+1)=q_j, m_0=F, F(0)=beta(2), and
F'(0)=theta. All integrals and their first derivatives are analytic for
|u|<1; on a smaller closed disc the hyperbolic-sine majorant is integrable
at x=0. There is no factorial or extra factor of two in F'(0).

For n>=2 put d=n-1 and use indices 0<=i,j<d:

 c_j(u)=q_j(u),
 B_ij(u)=q_(i+j)(u)+q_(i+j+1)(u),
 a_n(u)=det B(u),
 r_n(u)=c(u)^T B(u)^(-1)c(u).

For n=1 take a_1=1 and r_1=0. The polynomial basis

 1, (1+t), (1+t)t, ..., (1+t)t^(n-2)

has determinant one relative to 1,t,...,t^(n-1). In this basis the moment
matrix is exactly

 [ F(u)    c(u)^T ]
 [ c(u)    B(u)   ].

Consequently

 H_n(u)=a_n(u)(F(u)-r_n(u))=a_n(u)F(u)+b_n(u),
 b_n=-a_n r_n.                                           (1)

This proves the rank-one target dependence without assuming any numerical
independence of beta values. For 0<=u<1 the weight is positive, so B is
positive definite and a_n(u)>0. The assertion that w_u is a positive
weight needs this restriction: for negative u it oscillates near zero.
That does not damage the argument. A right derivative at zero suffices;
alternatively, for each fixed n the finite Gram matrix stays positive
definite on a sufficiently small two-sided neighborhood by continuity.

Differentiation gives exactly

 W_n=a_n(0)H_n'(0)-a_n'(0)H_n(0)
    =a_n(0)^2 theta+a_n(0)b_n'(0)-a_n'(0)b_n(0),
 E_n:=W_n/a_n(0)^2=theta-r_n'(0).                         (2)

Thus beta(2) cancels identically. The one-row determinant derivative is a
valid calculus identity, but alone says nothing about the divisibility
left after this elimination. For a general polynomial P(u,F(u)), its
derivative still contains P_u(0,beta(2))+theta P_X(0,beta(2)); no free
beta(2) elimination has been provided in that higher-degree setting.

As a small explicit calibration, n=2 gives a=10/9, b=-1,
a'=82/81, b'=-2, and

 W_2=(2/81)(50 theta-49),  E_2=theta-49/50.

The direct weight-four model instead gives the primitive pair
82 theta-81 and scalar error theta-81/82.

2. VARIATIONAL NONVANISHING: THE COORDINATOR'S PROPOSAL IS CORRECT

Every polynomial of degree<n with P(-1)=1 has the form
P(t)=1+(1+t)Q(t), deg Q<d. Completing the square in the displayed block
matrix proves

 lambda_n(u):=H_n(u)/a_n(u)
   =min_(deg P<n, P(-1)=1) integral_0^1 P(x^2)^2 w_u(x)dx. (3)

The minimizer is unique; explicitly
P_(n,u)(t)=1-(1+t)sum_(j<d)(B(u)^(-1)c(u))_j t^j.
It is analytic in u near zero. Its derivative has value zero at t=-1
and therefore is an admissible constrained variation. Stationarity makes
the cross term vanish when (3) is differentiated. Since

 w_0'(x)=(-log x)^3/(6(1+x^2)),

equation (2) becomes the all-index identity

 E_n=integral_0^1 P_(n,0)(x^2)^2
                      (-log x)^3/(6(1+x^2)) dx > 0.      (4)

The inequality is strict because P_(n,0)(-1)=1, so the polynomial is not
identically zero. This proves W_n>0 for every n, independent of finite
numerics and independent of any rationality hypothesis.

Let lambda_(n,4) be the constrained minimum for the same weight-four
density, and lambda_(n,2)=lambda_n(0). Then

                    E_n >= lambda_(n,4) > 0.              (5)

The first variation is not analytically superior to the direct model
after normalizing its theta coefficient to one. In the independently
checked indices 2..12 inequality (5) is strict, by an exact rational
comparison; no numerical beta(4) evaluation is needed for that comparison.
No assertion of strict inequality for every n>1 is required here.

3. A SHARP SCALAR ERROR RATE, WITH AN ELEMENTARY PROOF

The variational observation also removes a possible misleading comparison
of raw determinant sizes. In the t variable the two measures are

 eta_2(t)dt=(-log t)dt/[4 sqrt(t)(1+t)],
 eta_4(t)dt=(-log t)^3 dt/[96 sqrt(t)(1+t)].

Their total masses are beta(2) and theta. Put rho=3+sqrt(8). Then

 lim_(n->infinity) log(lambda_(n,2))/n
 =lim_(n->infinity) log(lambda_(n,4))/n
 =lim_(n->infinity) log(E_n)/n
 =-2 log rho = -3.525494348... .                           (6)

Here is a proof; no asymptotic theorem about orthogonal polynomials is
being imported.

For either minimum, test
P(t)=T_(n-1)(2t-1)/T_(n-1)(-3). Its value at -1 is one, and its squared
supremum on [0,1] is 1/T_(n-1)(3)^2. Since
T_d(3)=(rho^d+rho^(-d))/2, this gives the upper exponent in (6).

For the lower bound, restrict to I_e=[e,1-e], 0<e<1/2, of length L=1-2e.
Each density has a positive minimum c_(s,e) there. The orthonormal
Legendre polynomials on an interval I of length L are

 sqrt((2j+1)/L) * L_j((2t-a-b)/L),  I=[a,b].

For |z|<=1, |L_j(z)|<=1; for |z|>1,
|L_j(z)|<=(|z|+sqrt(z^2-1))^j. These bounds follow directly from the
integral formula
L_j(z)=pi^(-1) integral_0^pi (z+sqrt(z^2-1)cos v)^j dv,
using parity for negative real z. The same formula with the imaginary
square root proves the inside-interval bound; it is a polynomial identity,
as can also be checked by expanding its generating function.

Expanding P in this orthonormal basis and applying Cauchy--Schwarz gives

 |P(t_*)|^2 <= (n^2/L) R^(2(n-1)) integral_I P(t)^2dt,     (7)

where R=1 inside I and R=|z_*|+sqrt(z_*^2-1) otherwise.
For t_*=-1 and I=I_e, z_*=-3/(1-2e). Thus

 lambda_(n,s) >= c_(s,e)(1-2e)/[n^2 R_e^(2(n-1))].

First take n to infinity, then e down to zero. This proves the lower
exponent and hence the first two equalities in (6).

For the upper bound on E_n, fix 0<e<1 and use I=[0,1-e]. We have
eta_2(t)>=(1-t)/8>=e/8 on I. Apply (7) to P_(n,0), uniformly for
t_* in [0,1]. The largest transformed argument is
s_e=(1+e)/(1-e), so with S_e=s_e+sqrt(s_e^2-1),

 sup_[0,1] |P_(n,0)|^2
    <= 8 n^2 S_e^(2(n-1)) lambda_(n,2)/[e(1-e)].

Integrating against eta_4 bounds E_n by theta times this expression.
The already proved lambda_(n,2) exponent gives
limsup log(E_n)/n <= -2 log rho+2 log S_e. Let e down to zero, so S_e
tends to one. The opposite inequality follows from (5). This completes
the proof of (6).

The direct Hankel determinant has quadratic logarithmic decay, but its
coefficient a_(n,4) carries the same leading determinant scale. The first
variation contains the square a_n(0)^2. These scalar factors disappear
when the approximation is put in primitive integer form. Equation (6),
rather than the raw n^2 determinant scale, is the relevant analytic
budget for the reduced denominator. The old endpoint Christoffel
obstructions must NOT be imported here: their constraint was at a support
endpoint, while the present constraint is at -1 outside the support [0,1].
That distinction is why an exponential, rather than polynomial, scalar
error is available here.

4. PRIMITIVE ARITHMETIC AND THE EXACT MISSING LEMMA

Write r_n'(0)=p_n/A_n in lowest terms with A_n>0. Equation (2) says that
the primitive pair is exactly

                       L_n=A_n theta-p_n=A_n E_n.          (8)

Equivalently, clearing the two rational coefficients by their least
common denominator D and then dividing their FULL integer gcd g yields
the same pair. Any nonzero rational rescaling of W_n leaves (8) unchanged
up to sign. In particular, multiplying the parameter family H_n,a_n by a
rational factor f(u) multiplies its exterior form by f(0)^2; it provides
no new primitive saving. A determinant basis change of that kind is not
an additional arithmetic mechanism.

If theta=h/b is rational, b L_n is a nonzero positive integer. Thus a
sequence with L_n->0 would prove irrationality. A single small value, or
the n=1,2 values below one, cannot exclude an unknown b.

From (6), along any infinite subsequence,

 log L_n = log A_n - 2 n log rho + o(n).                   (9)

A NECESSARY condition for L_n->0 is
limsup log A_n/n <= 2 log rho along that subsequence. This condition at
equality is NOT sufficient: the sublinear terms still matter.
A SUFFICIENT condition is a strictly favorable margin
limsup log A_n/n < 2 log rho. More generally one can prove directly
log A_n+log E_n->-infinity with quantified lower-order terms.
No all-index height estimate of either useful kind has been established.

The independent checks use Fraction Gaussian elimination on B, followed
by the identity

 r_n'(0)=2 c'(0)^T v-v^T B'(0)v,  v=B(0)^(-1)c(0),
 a_n'(0)=a_n(0) trace(B(0)^(-1)B'(0)).

This is separate from the explorer's difference-of-determinants and
replaced-row implementation. It reproduces all a,b,a',b', and both
primitive pairs at every existing index 1..12. Arb enclosures then verify
the signs and finite sizes; no new index or family was evaluated.

5. ARCHIVE COMPARISON AND THE OTHER SEEDS

Arcsine family. In arcsine-contact.txt, substitute u=a^2. Since
arcsin|t|-pi/2=-arccos|t| and cosine is even, the two measures agree
identically, not just to first order. The rational moment recurrence and
boundary 4F(u) agree as well. The u derivative equals the a^2 coefficient,
or one half the second a derivative at zero. Carreira's signed measure
is -2 times that coefficient measure: its boundary -8 beta(4) is
consistent with the present derivative boundary +4 beta(4). There is no
calibration discrepancy and no new parameter family here.

Ordinary moments. The rank-one/Schur-complement mechanism overlaps with
arithmetic-followup.txt, and exterior elimination overlaps with round14.
No full coefficient-sequence identification was established. The
inverse-moment model in deep-investigation.txt uses a different measure
and grid. The arcsine high-column prime statement assumes its own
arcsine entries, p>H, high columns, p-integral rows, and invertibility of
the beta coefficient matrix modulo p. It does not apply to B above.
Round14's multiplier divisibility and its fixed cofactor dyadic lower
bound are also family-specific and explicitly allow unbounded primitive
content. None establishes a general obstruction to this ordinary model.

Holonomy. The displayed parameter functions J(a) and F(u) have genuine
poles at infinitely many finite points, so they cannot be solutions of
linear differential equations over Q(a), respectively Q(u). That does
not exclude a different geometric variable. More significantly, the
simple lower-jet objection is not the last word. The archived function

 C(x)=F_0-log(x)F_1+(log x)^2 F_2/2-(log x)^3 F_3/6

does satisfy C(1)=theta and C'(x)=-(log x)^3/[6(1+x^2)]. Hence, under
theta rational, its entire Taylor jet at 1 is rational. The telescoping
calculation and the archive's rational-jet repair are valid.

The same archive explains why this repair alone supplies no holonomy
contradiction: adding a rational constant does not change its differential
span, continuation domains, or denominator growth rate. I checked its
stated linear-operator obstruction: continuation around zero changes
C by Delta with derivative
-[(log x+2pi i)^3-(log x)^3]/[6(1+x^2)]. Its residue at x=i is
31pi^3/24. Continuing again about i changes Delta by a nonzero constant.
Thus a rational linear differential operator killing the zero-endpoint
branch must kill 1 and therefore erase the constant carrying theta.
This is a valid scoped proof, not an obstruction to nonlinear expressions,
new systems, or all arithmetic holonomy. The fresh seed provides none of
the latter with a new rationality-dependent advantage.

Cyclotomic seed. The series identities
Li_4(i)-Li_4(-i)=2i theta and
Li_4(i)+Li_4(-i)=-7 zeta(4)/64 are correct. The odd projector is already
the defining beta germ. No additional correspondence, rational relation
with 1, or improved integral lattice was exhibited. Older specialized
functional-equation obstructions are not needed for this assessment.

6. THE STRONGEST CASE FOR CONTINUING, AND WHY IT DOES NOT WIN

The favorable case is concrete: the ordinary first variation has exact
target isolation, all-index positive nonvanishing, sharp exponential
scalar decay, and a substantial primitive improvement over direct
weight-four moments in the existing data. Its exterior evaluation point
also avoids the older endpoint-polynomial-decay obstruction. It would be
wrong to reject it for a missing sign theorem, claim its primitive form
is equal to an archived sequence without proof, or infer an asymptotic
arithmetic impossibility from the finite table.

Those improvements leave exactly the hard arithmetic question (8)-(9).
A specific new identity or congruence controlling the REDUCED denominator
of r_n'(0), with a plausible favorable rate, would justify an inexpensive
first check against the already available n<=12 pairs. No such identity,
congruence, or rationality-dependent divisibility mechanism is present.
Stating the needed height lemma, hoping for a larger future gcd, changing
rational basis, or requesting larger determinants does not itself supply
a new mechanism. The other two seeds similarly contain no new specified
object to test.

I therefore retain ZERO proposals for another investigation and recommend
parking the project. The bounded phase ends at this verdict. No further
construction, parameter search, external communication, or goal change
was undertaken.
