Coordinator review of the fresh Euler--Boole theorem
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. Model review, not formal verification.

The complete fresh/findings.txt was read. No substantive mathematical
gap was found in its stated scope. The result excludes the prescribed
endpoint/truncation family and products of its primitive factors, not
general sums, Padé constructions, or nonfactoring determinants.

1. Exact remainder and constants

The midpoint tail integral has prefactor1/12 because the ordinary
Laplace factor1/6 is multiplied by1/(2cosh t). Integrating its finite
Taylor polynomial gives exactly the displayed rational correction;
there is no auxiliary period. The mixed-boundary Fourier derivation
is valid at x=0 even though the function does not vanish at x=1.
Two integrations by parts give
  (t^2+a_k^2) integral f(x)cos(a_k x)dx=a_k(-1)^k.
The alternating partial fractions at t=0 converge, and the m>=1
geometric remainders converge absolutely.

For the remainder kernel, f_1/f_0<=3^(1-2m)<=1/3. This gives strict
positivity and the exact sign(-1)^(N+m). Restricting to t>=pi/2
gives the constant pi^2/(144N), independently of how m grows.
The m=0 case is correctly treated separately with sech(t)>=exp(-t).

The sharp asymptotic also checks. The first-pole integral has exponent
phi(t)=2a log(t/a)-2t, a=pi/2. Its stationary point is a, with curvature
-2/a. The amplitude at that point is a/2. Multiplication by pi/12
and the Gaussian factor sqrt(pi a/N) gives precisely
pi^3/(48 sqrt(2N)). Bounded shifts in the truncation index do not
change that leading constant. This is a genuine two-sided estimate.

2. Primitive denominator lower bound

For p^2>2N, multiplication by p^4 leaves only odd denominators pr.
Their character factors split as chi(p)chi(r), giving the exact
residue chi(p)S_J. Every Euler correction is p-integral when p does
not divide6N, regardless of m. A p-integral correction cannot cancel
a nonzero p^(-4) principal part. Thus the proof concerns the ACTUAL
reduced denominator, not a sufficient clearing factor.

For fixed K, all S_1,...,S_K are positive rationals, so their exceptional
numerator/denominator primes form a finite set. These are avoided by
the prime band as N tends to infinity. The loss from primes dividing
6N is at most log(6N). The prime number theorem gives rate
8(1-1/(2K+1)) uniformly in m. Letting K grow AFTER this liminf is
legitimate; no uniform exceptional-set estimate in growing K is used.
The simpler K=1 rate already exceeds pi.

3. Uniformity and nonlinear scope

For bounded N and increasing m, the positive remainder integral on
any interval strictly to the right of pi/2 grows geometrically in m.
Combining this with uniform growth at large N proves divergence off
finite subsets of the parameter lattice. Each form is nonzero, so
its absolute value has a positive global infimum over the lattice.

Gauss's lemma then gives the stated degree-normalized lower bound for
products of primitive factors. The note does not confuse ordinary
decay of powers of one small form with the stronger degree-normalized
decay needed to exclude every possible rational denominator.

The extension to fixed integer beta(s), s>=2, has the same signed
sech remainder and prime mechanism. Its correction coefficients have
denominators supported at a fixed finite set and at N (in fact the
factorial ratio simplifies further), so the rate2s is justified.
