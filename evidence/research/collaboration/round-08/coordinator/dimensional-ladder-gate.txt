1. A concrete positive family

In the five-edge two-loop graph recorded in two-loop-numerators.txt,
put every propagator exponent equal to b=n+1 and the real integration
dimension equal to Dspace=4b. Let U,F be its Symanzik polynomials at
u=1,v=2. Schwinger integration gives

  I_b = pi^(-Dspace) integral_(R^Dspace)^2 product_e q_e^(-b)
      = Gamma(b)^(-4) J_(b-1),
  J_n = integral_projective (product_e alpha_e)^n/(U F)^(n+1).

Here the q_e are the squared edge distances. This normalization comes
from Gamma(sum a_e-L Dspace/2)/product Gamma(a_e)=Gamma(b)^(-4).

In the previously derived outer-parameter gauge, the Jacobian is U^2,
F=U Dcube, and

  Dcube=r x(1-x)+(1-r)y(1-y)+t r(1-r)(x^2+y^2),
  A=t(1-t)r(1-r)x(1-x)y(1-y).

Consequently

  J_n=integral_[0,1]^4 A^n/Dcube^(n+1) dr dx dy dt.

All integrands are positive in the interior. Arithmetic-geometric mean
on the first two terms of Dcube gives

  Dcube >= 2 sqrt[r(1-r)x(1-x)y(1-y)],
  A/Dcube <= t(1-t)/2 * sqrt[r(1-r)x(1-x)y(1-y)] <= 1/64.

The same bound gives an integrable majorant for1/Dcube with integral
pi^3/2. Thus endpoints cause no convergence problem, independently of
the period evaluation J_0=6 beta(4). In particular

                    0 < J_n <= 6 beta(4) 64^(-n).

Root derived this inequality and the geometry worker independently
checked both the Schwinger normalization and the cube Jacobian.

3. A separate convergence observation, not the above graph family

The mistakenly proposed angular ray h=2b-1 is nevertheless a well-defined
rational series. Put F_b(z)=product_(j=0)^(b-1)(z-j)^(-3). The old S_b is

       S_b(m)=sum_(j=0)^(b-1) Res_(z=j) F_b(z) F_b(z+m).

Expand F_b(z+m) in1/m. Its coefficient at order m^(-3b-k) is a polynomial
in z of degree k. For k<3b-1, the sum of its finite residues against
F_b is zero because the product is O(z^-2) at infinity. At k=3b-1, its
leading coefficient is (-1)^(b-1)binom(6b-2,3b-1), and the coefficient
of1/z is precisely this number. Hence

  S_b(m) ~ (-1)^(b-1)binom(6b-2,3b-1)m^(-(6b-1)).

Since deg P_h=2h-1, P_(2b-1)S_b decays as m^(-(2b+2)). This proves
absolute convergence of the corresponding odd-grid angular series,
beyond the conservative sufficient cutoff2h<3b of the old implementation.
Root and discrete independently derived the residue argument. Discrete
checked exact rational functions for b1..4 and found orders5,11,17,23.

This is NOT the Dspace=4b all-propagator-b family. In the source convention
it would have Dspace=8b-4 and other propagator powers. No favorable
primitive arithmetic or new positive integral estimate follows from the
convergence observation alone. It remains a scoped extension of an old
family, not evidence that irrationality is close.
