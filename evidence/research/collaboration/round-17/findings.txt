ROUND17: INTERLEAVED BORDERS -- BOUNDED ARITHMETIC ASSESSMENT
31 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

Verdict

Do not continue this frame by enlarging the determinant computations or
optimizing the border width. The proposed four-layer cancellation mechanism
has a rigorous quantitative obstruction for all three prescribed widths.
One would need a substantially stronger theorem about the remaining
integral determinant or primitive content. No saving sufficient to close
the budget is supported by the prescribed tests.

This is NOT a theorem that every primitive use of this determinant family
fails. The result closes the proposed first-four-layer transfer, proves a
narrower integer-normalization obstruction at c=1/5, and identifies the
remaining all-index primitive-content question precisely.

1. What was proved

The complete derivation is local-structure.txt. It reuses the round-1
positivity and round-16 Cauchy energy without altering the family.

For p>3, p^2>4M, split each finite harmonic sum into its p-multiple and
p-unit terms. A character cancellation proves that the regular part of the
WHOLE divided-difference matrix is p-integral, including a==-b mod p and
the zero fibre. Thus the first four negative prime layers are determined
exactly by the singular part. The initially possible third-layer regular
term disappears; the preliminary note is superseded on that point.

The local blocks were derived for same-sign and opposite-sign lifts of a
squared residue, zero fibres and diagonal entries. The first pairing
depends on the two moments (sum y, sum y*a*H3(a)), and its second component
generally varies with the node. In a positive-length range for every width,
the one-dimensional surplus kernels have moments (7,-5 chi4(p)t).
Their cross-pairing, divided by p, is

                    -35 chi4(p)/[6(t+s)].

It is a Cauchy matrix of maximal possible rank for p>7. The hoped-for
proportional-moment transport therefore does not apply to these windows.
This is a systematic local effect, not a small exceptional prime.

More decisively, all four truncated Smith layers can be proved explicitly
for 2M+4<p<4M. Let h=max(0,r-floor(p/4)), N=r+m, M=r+2m.

 * If p<4r, the capped exponents of p^4 Phi are
       0 (h times), 1 (2m times), 2 (h times),
       4 (N-2h-2m times).
   The corresponding sufficient denominator exponent is 6h+6m.
 * If p>4r, let H=M-floor(p/4). The capped exponents are
       1 (H times), 4 (N-H times),
   giving sufficient exponent 3H.

The proof uses unit Cauchy minors, two successive Schur layers and the
exact rank of the singular matrix. It does not assume that finite rank
patterns continue, nor import Litsey's trigamma constants or certificates.

2. The quantitative comparison

The prime number theorem sums this large-prime contribution to
       (3+3c^2)r^2+o(r^2).
The established real determinant has logarithm -E(c)r^2+o(r^2).

  c        real decay E(c)    large-prime bound alone    shortfall
  1/5      1.6523070663       3.12                       1.4676929337
  1/2      2.0585984916       3.75                       1.6914015084
  1        2.8184512398       6                          3.1815487602

Even assigning zero cost to every OTHER prime leaves this four-layer
method without a favorable comparison. These are costs in a sufficient
bound, not lower bounds for the actual primitive multiplier. No sharpening
of the same four negative layers at these large primes can remove them:
the truncated Smith lists themselves are proved, not merely estimated.

There is also a uniform reason not to optimize the width inside 0<c<=1:
the nonnegative energy integrand and rho_c<=1 imply
 E(c)<=2(1+c)^2 log 2<=4(1+c^2)log 2<3(1+c^2).
Thus the same four-layer method misses the budget throughout this width
range. This analytical corollary required no additional parameter tests.

An all-primes sufficient denominator bound, including the cruder smaller
prime estimate, is (11+12c+7c^2)r^2+o(r^2): constants 13.68, 18.75, 30.
Primes p<=sqrt(4M), including 2 and 3 and their higher powers, cost only
O(r^(3/2) log r). They have not been silently omitted.

The polynomial degree is exactly r and its value at theta is strictly
positive by the inherited proof. If theta=u/v, the rationality denominator
cost is r log v=O(r); it neither changes the r^2 comparison nor supplies
an extra saving. The kernel real estimate is two-sided, so an undiscovered
extra order-r^2 analytic decay cannot rescue these fixed frames.

budgets.py evaluates the exact logarithmic formulas with 192-bit Arb
intervals. budgets.json contains the enclosures and the comparison.

3. An additional genuine integer-clearing obstruction

For 2M+4<p<4r, a specific coefficient of P has valuation exactly
-4h-3m. The proof isolates a unique high-node minor with a unit border
Cauchy determinant. It follows that
  log den P >= (2-c-c^2)r^2+o(r^2).

At c=1/5 this lower coefficient is 1.76>E(1/5), by more than 0.1076.
Consequently EVERY positive integer multiplier clearing the coefficients
produces growing values, using the inherited lower real bound.

This theorem allows no rescue by replacing a sufficient integer LCM with
a smaller integer denominator. It still allows rational primitive
rescaling, because a growing common numerator factor has not been bounded.
The lower comparison does not obstruct the other two widths by itself.

4. Exact finite evidence -- complete primitive normalization

The nine preselected cases were r=8,16,24 with c=1/5,1/2,1. No broader
parameter scan followed. At r=8,c=1/5 the floor gives m=0, so that case
is explicitly a principal-frame control; it is not evidence for a border.

For each case, the complete rational polynomial was computed as
  det Z * charpoly[-(U-V Z^(-1)W)],
where Z is the rational border block and U,V,W are the other blocks.
Z is invertible by the existing positivity theorem. Direct full-matrix
determinants at X=0,1,-1 check the construction (27 exact equalities).

Writing D=den P and g=gcd of the coefficients of D P, the primitive
multiplier is kappa=D/g. The full integer gcd was removed, including
all possible large factors; no numerical gcd guess or convenient LCM
was substituted. The saved records contain all rational and primitive
coefficients, D, g, primewise valuations and exact construction checks.

At r=24 the results are:

  c       m    log D/r^2   log g/r^2   log kappa/r^2
  1/5     2    11.454776    0           11.454776
  1/2     6    15.911382    0.334540    15.576842
  1       12   25.056399    1.665479    23.390921

The floor makes the first finite effective width 1/6, rather than exactly
1/5. All finite value comparisons use the ACTUAL node sets, not an
asymptotic E(c) substituted into a finite calculation.

The exact Cauchy comparison and Arb logs prove that the logarithms of
the three primitive values at r=24 exceed respectively 5624, 7730 and
11780. All nine prescribed primitive values exceed one. These are finite
failures, not an asymptotic primitive obstruction.

For all 187 tested prime/frame cases with p>3 and p^2>4M, the four-layer
bound equals the exact coefficient Gauss denominator valuation: no extra
residual saving was observed. The 88 large-prime patterns in the theorem's
range all match its exact lists. Numerator content in these examples is
supported only at 2; after removing primes <=4M, no content factor remains.
At r=24 the nontrivial contents are 2^278 and 2^1384 for c=1/2 and c=1.
These factors have already been fully removed in the primitive comparisons.
This observation is NOT an all-index content theorem. The small-prime
DENOMINATOR estimate does not bound possible positive numerator content;
even one fixed prime can in principle supply an order-r^2 saving.

Additional exact checks cover 122,280 regular-entry decompositions and
122,280 fibre reductions, 242 nonzero first pairings, 29 maximal-rank
pairing matrices, and 29 instances of the isolated coefficient valuation.
They check the symbolic claims against the full original rational kernel.

5. The precise remaining lemma, and why not to chase it here

After removing the negative local Smith factors, the determinant has
  P=p^(-tau_p) u_p(X) det T_p(X),
with u_p a Gauss unit and T_p integral. Therefore
  v_G(P)=-tau_p+t_p, t_p=v_G(det T_p)>=0.

A rescue needs a NEW uniform divisibility theorem making the weighted
sum of these t_p, or actual positive Gauss valuations at other primes
(including a proved sufficient asymptotic dyadic content), large enough
to beat the complete real/arithmetic budget. The displayed
shortfalls already arise before charging the other negative costs. A
theorem about further basis changes in the same four-layer calculation
would not change its Smith invariants.

This is a sharper missing lemma than 'find more cancellation': it concerns
the residual integral determinant, after the attempted mechanism is
exhausted. All 187 exact prime/frame tests give t_p=0 in the tested large-
prime regime. Nothing presently singles out a systematic arithmetic
identity capable of supplying the necessary saving.

Recommendation: stop this bordered-frame implementation here. Do not turn
the unresolved logical possibility of huge residual content into a reason
for an open-ended scan. Revisit it only with a concrete new divisibility
mechanism. No wider construction campaign is started in this phase.
