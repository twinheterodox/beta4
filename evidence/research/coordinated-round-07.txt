1. The complete fixed-size endpoint system

Let N>=1, a,b>=0, a+b=2N, and let D be an integer quadratic with
D(0)=1 and no zero on [0,1]. Set E=x*d/dx and prescribe

 G=R/D^N, deg R<=2N, R(0)=0,
 f=A/(1+x^2)+E^4G/x,
 f=O(x^a) at0 and O((x-1)^b) at1.

Four integrations by parts give exactly

 integral_0^1 f(x)(-log x)^3 dx/6=A*theta+G(1), theta=beta(4).

The entire rational source space is isomorphic to a four-by-five
kernel [-v|K], with Kg=Av and g_k=E^kG(1), k=0,1,2,3.
All entries are constructed from rational data, without fitting theta.
The proof includes the converse, singular K, and the b=0 Taylor boundary.

Explicitly, put F(x)=sum_(j odd>=1)chi4(j)x^j/j^4,
R_low=-trunc_(degree<=a)(D^N F), L(z)=product_(j=a+1)^(2N)(z-j),
and D^N=sum d_ell x^ell. Then

 K_(r,k)=sum_ell d_ell [t^k](ell+t)^r L(ell+t), r,k=0,...,3.

The inhomogeneous column is the endpoint jet of R_low minus the local
particular solution of E^4V=-x/(1+x^2). Its full formula and proof are
in arithmetic/findings.txt, Section1, under collaboration/round-07/.

Let O_a=lcm(odd positive integers<=a), C=2 O_a^4. Then K and Cv are
integral. At augmented rank4 the endpoint pair is

 (C det K, det K[column0 replaced by Cv]).

Its exact two-integer gcd gives the actual primitive pair. A zero pair
is not a witness, and deficient augmented rank requires the full kernel.
The n=1 circular case has A=0 and gives the primitive constant1.

Proofs: arithmetic/findings.txt Sections1-2; coordinator/endpoint_system.txt.

2. The arithmetic improvement and its limits

With d_b=lcm(1,...,b), the matrix

 Khat=K diag(1,d_b,d_b^2,d_b^3)/b!

is integral. Both endpoint determinants acquire the same factor
d_b^6/(b!)^4, so their primitive pair is unchanged. The exact common
scalar yields a proved divisor of the raw pair's content; the remaining
gcd is not discarded.

For the local solution V at x=1, four ordinary integrations have strict
increasing integration indices. Consequently, if

 W4(m)=product_(r=1)^4 lcm(1,...,floor(m/r)),

then 2^ceil((m-3)/2) W4(m) clears [z^m]V(1+z). For b>0 this gives

 Z_b=gcd(2 b!, 2^ceil(b/2) W4(b+3)),
 Chat=lcm(O_a^4,Z_b),

and Chat*v/b! is integral. For b=0 take Z_0=1.

The local postponement is sharp: for every prime p>=5, no coefficient
below degree4p has denominator divisible by p^4, whereas

 p^4 [z^(4p)]V(1+z) == -chi4(p)/48 mod p.

This is a theorem about the local germ. It does not prove that the final
primitive endpoint pair retains or eliminates any particular fourth layer.
The low-endpoint data still have their separate O_a^4 cost.

The best leading growth of this sufficient column bound occurs at
a/N=1/2, b/N=3/2, with rate13/4+(3/4)log2, compared with
4+(1/2)log2 at symmetry. This is not an optimization of the complete
primitive pair or of its actual error. No useful uniform primitive-height
upper or lower bound follows from the column bound alone.

Proof: arithmetic/findings.txt Sections3-8.
Separate review: analysis/arithmetic_review.txt.

3. Eventual rank and the actual signed Hermite error

For D=1+x^2 and a=b=N=n, the centered four-by-four rank matrix has

 det K_centered ~ product_(r=0)^3(n+r)! * (1+sqrt2)^(4n+10)
                     /[(2*pi)^2(2+sqrt2)^8 n^8].

It is therefore positive and invertible eventually. The source space is
then one-dimensional with A!=0. Small-index exceptions are retained.
The uncentered integer matrix in Section1 has determinant2^(4n) times
this determinant; the normalization is explicit throughout the packet.

Normalize the source to A=1 and put epsilon_n=theta+G(1). A separate
residue calculation and contour argument give the ACTUAL signed error:

 epsilon_n ~ (-1)^n C0 n^(5/2)(log n)^2 rho^n,
 rho=(sqrt2-1)/2,
 C0=pi*sqrt(pi)/48 * (2+sqrt2)^(7/2)
                         *(sqrt2-1)*log(1+sqrt2)^3
   =2.4193871616202196119... >0.

The proof eliminates the lower periods by determinant multilinearity.
It controls the complex phases, rather than substituting an absolute
upper bound. It proves eventual alternating sign and nonvanishing;
it does not give an explicit threshold for every finite index.

For the primitive pair a_n X+b_n with a_n>0, the conclusion is exactly

 log|a_n theta+b_n|=log a_n+n log rho
                 +(5/2)log n+2loglog n+log C0+o(1).

The remaining arithmetic threshold is log[2(1+sqrt2)] per n. A limsup
of log a_n/n strictly below it would suffice for irrationality by these
forms. No such bound is proved. A poor sufficient clearing bound does
not prove the opposite assertion.

The six prescribed finite measurements still give large primitive values.
At n=80 the relative error is roughly10^-48, but the primitive integer
form has size roughly10^320. This is finite evidence of the arithmetic
problem, not an asymptotic impossibility theorem for this family.

4. Third derivatives of continued-fraction quotients

The separate construction starts from

 S(a)=integral_0^infinity exp(-at)sech(t)dt,
 q_0=1, q_1=a, r_0=0, r_1=1,
 (n+1)q_(n+1)=a q_n+n q_(n-1),

and the same recurrence for r_n. Its exact error is

 q_n S-r_n=(-1)^n integral exp(-at)sech(t)tanh(t)^n dt.

For N>=1 and T_N=sum_(k=0)^(N-1)(-1)^k/(2k+1)^4, define

 alpha_(n,N)=T_N+(-1)^(N+1)/12
                         * (d/da)^3(r_n/q_n)|_(a=2N).

These are rational theta-only approximants. For each FIXED N,

 theta-alpha_(n,N) ~ (-1)^(N+n) K_N n^(-2N)(log n)^3,
 K_N=2^(-2N)Gamma(N+1/2)^2/12 >0.

At even depth n=2m, let d_(2m,N) be the actual reduced denominator.
Explicit integer polynomials A_N and C_N of degree N satisfy

 v_p(d_(2m,N))=8+4v_p(A_N(m))

whenever m<p<2m, p>2N and p does not divide C_N(m). This equality
comes from a unique p-unit term in the third quotient derivative. It
already accounts for common numerator content.

The exception is necessary: four saved excluded cases have exponent4.
For fixed N, the total log size of excluded primes is only O_N(log m).
The prime number theorem and the actual error asymptotic therefore give

 liminf_(m->infinity) log|d_(2m,N)theta-b_(2m,N)|/(2m) >=4,

where alpha=b/d is reduced. This closes each fixed-N EVEN-depth
subfamily. It does not close odd depths, growing N, other combinations,
or continued fractions generally. No growing-N extension was attempted
in the closing pass.
