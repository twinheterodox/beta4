COORDINATED BETA(4) RESEARCH: ROUND11
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. Goal remains active.

Outcome

No irrationality proof or credible complete route emerged. The strongest
retained result is conditional nonvanishing for an unsquared quarter-pole
determinant family. Its tested primitive values remain much too large.
Separate algebraic-norm and assumption-dependent Niven constructions also
fail their specific gates. These conclusions concern the stated families.

1. Conditional nonvanishing and exact period cancellation

The symmetric imaginary-line Bernoulli functional has pole value
 Lambda(1/(x+a))=a^4 zeta(4,a)-1/2-a/3.
At a_j=(2j+1)/4, ordinary Lagrange interpolation cancels zeta(4),
leaving entries in Q+Q theta without the previous high-degree positive
interpolation numerator. Its theta coefficient matrix is positive;
positivity of the evaluated matrix is not asserted.

Let V=product_(m=1)^n(x-m)^4, h=2n, K=8n+4, D=product_(j<K)(x+a_j),
and H_ij=Lambda(V x^(i+j)/D), 0<=i,j<h. If theta=c is rational and
p=8n+5 is prime outside its denominator, then
                  v_p(det H(c))=-8n.
After multiplication by p^4, exactly h finite-tail nodes survive modulo
p and form a square Vandermonde Gram matrix with unit weights. An
elementary argument supplies infinitely many such primes. This fills the
nonvanishing requirement under hypothetical rationality, but says nothing
favorable about real size after primitive normalization.

The auxiliary-prime mechanism is adapted from Lai's work; the proof here
is explicit. See collaboration/round-11/coordinator/unsquared-nonvanishing.txt
and norms/auxiliary_prime_review.txt for provenance, proof and model review.

All seven prescribed primitive determinants are large. Their logarithmic
absolute values range from 8.14 to 144.78. Dilation identifies the source
with ordinary-plus-alternating half-pole derivative sums; the ordinary
rational correction cannot be dropped. This is a change of source geometry,
not a wholly new functional. The bounded gate does not prove a general
asymptotic obstruction. Details: fresh/findings.txt and norms/unsquared_review.txt.

2. Nonlinear cyclotomic norms

For an odd prime p and h=pi/(2p), define
 alpha_p=sin(h)^4 sum_(n odd,0<n<p) chi_(-4)(n)cos(nh)^3/sin(nh)^4.
Then 8 alpha_p is integral, its degree is p-1, and
 theta-alpha_p ~ pi^2(4theta+5beta(2))/(24p^2)>0.
This avoids the old rational-trace parity obstruction and calibrates
unnormalized beta(4). But almost all conjugates are large. The primitive
minimal polynomial P_p has
              log|P_p(theta)|=4(p-1)log p+O(p).
Its complete content can save only exp(O(p)). The family therefore fails
the rationality contradiction, including the v^(p-1) cost if theta=u/v.
This is a proved failure of this particular norm family, not of all norms.
Details: collaboration/round-11/norms/findings.txt.

3. Rationality-dependent positive integrals

For F(x)=sum chi_4(m)x^m/m^4, assume theta=F(1)=u/v and set
A_n=[vF(u-vF)]^n/n!. The associated positive integrals tend to zero,
but endpoint rationality does not make their derivatives integral.
For each odd p>n not dividing uv, the origin derivative of order p+n-1
has valuation -3. Changing to F as coordinate gives only the rational
beta integral u^(2n+1)n!/[v(2n+1)!]. A positive rational-endpoint control
retains rational forcing and logarithmic monodromy, showing why those
generic features alone cannot supply the missing argument. No general
Niven impossibility theorem is claimed. Details: rationality/findings.txt.

4. The previous dense grid's analytic comparison

After scalar normalization, the positive interpolated kernel on the dense
quarter grid lies between polynomial-in-K multiples of 1/(t+1/16).
Its r-by-r Gram determinant therefore differs by only O(r log K) in the
logarithm. This prevents counting the denser grid as a free leading-order
analytic improvement. It is not a primitive-content or polynomial-degree
comparison. Details: coordinator/dense-grid-comparison.txt and
rationality/dense-grid-review.txt.
