1. Nonuniform Green-kernel graph

Let theta=beta4, G=beta2 and

 J_n=integral_[0,1]^4 P^n/D,
 P=t^4 r^2(1-r)^2 x(1-x)y(1-y),
 D=r x(1-x)+(1-r)y(1-y)+tr(1-r)(x^2+y^2).

This comes from a five-edge graph in dimension8n+4 with four outer
propagator powers n+1 and central power4n+1. The latter is the Laplacian
Green exponent. This is not the uniform-index ray mistakenly proposed
in round08. A Jacobian test proves P is not pointwise a function of
the two old external-moment coordinates. Broader graph equivalence and
literature priority have not been classified.

The integrals are positive and

 0<J_n<=6theta*256^-n,
 J_n~pi^(3/2)/(12sqrt2) n^(-5/2)256^-n.

A terminating Gegenbauer expansion diagonalizes the central Green kernel;
both radial integrations are elementary. The exact rational source has
poles of order at most4 and a reflection symmetry. Consequently, for
EVERY fixed integer n>=0,

             J_n belongs to Q+QG+Qtheta+Qlog2+Qzeta3.

An independent proof bounds the absolute integrated harmonic terms by
O_n(k^(-4n-3)); no unjustified boundary exchange is used. This proves the
period identity, not a uniform arithmetic bound as n grows.

The new ingredient is the terminating-harmonic reduction and internal

3. Multiplicative construction

Powers of beta4 retain fourth-power Dirichlet denominators, but polynomial
degree is a genuine rationality cost. The maximal elementary coefficient
gap gives (theta-1)^D and cannot handle arbitrary rational denominators.

A signed divisibility matrix K_(d,f)=chi(lcm(d,f))/lcm(d,f)^4 has

 det(XK-ee^T)=X^(r-1)det(K)(X-c_D), c_D=e^T K^-1 e.

For factor-closed D, c_D=sum mu(d)^2/h(d), h=(chi(n)n^4)*mu.
A full Boolean divisor basis reproduces the old finite Euler product.
Other prescribed finite bases give no useful primitive improvement.
There is no positivity assumption or general asymptotic no-go theorem.
