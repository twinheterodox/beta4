ROUND10: ARITHMETIC-FIRST GATES AND A FRESH TOPOLOGICAL TEST
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished.

No irrationality proof or credible complete route emerged. Three fresh
workers and the coordinator tested four concrete ideas, checked the
archive first, and stopped unsuccessful constructions at prescribed
tests. The useful tools below are progress in understanding constructions,
not evidence that beta4 irrationality is close. The goal remains active.

1. A sharp local arithmetic tool

For tau(P)=-E(P''')/12, E(x^n)=Euler_n(0), its exact norm on polynomials
of degree<=n that are integer-valued on Z_p is p^H_p(n), p>=5, where

 H_p(n)=max{h:m_p(h)<=n},
 m_p(3q+r)=(3-r)p^q+r p^(q+1), r=0,1,2.

This follows from sum tau(binomial(x,j))z^j
=-log(1+z)^3/[12(1+z/2)]. The first three denominator layers start
at p+2,2p+1,3p, and are attained exactly. The statement concerns the
polynomial functional, not its rational pole extension or a final
primitive determinant. It does not improve any already computed
primitive value by changing its basis.

Finite full-rank congruence restrictions on a source lattice multiply a
Gram determinant by a rational square and leave its primitive polynomial
unchanged. A proper-subspace test with
F_p(t)=t(t^((p-1)/2)-(-1)^((p-1)/2))^2 did change the polynomial,
but the prescribed p5,p7 cases were worse than matched central controls.
Both choices have the same best leading-coefficient p-integrality at
degree2p and the same tested raw determinant valuation -16.

2. Positive cancellation on a denser quarter grid

Let a_j=(2j+1)/4, D_K(t)=product_(j<K)(t+a_j^2), and let S_K be the
degree<K polynomial with S_K(-a_j^2)=1/a_j. It is positive for t>=0.
For the positive Hurwitz functional

 nu(R)=int_0^infinity R(y^2)[-y^4 f'''(y)/3]dy,
 f(y)=1/(exp(2pi y)-1),
 nu(1/(t+a^2))=a^3 zeta(4,a)-1/(2a)-1/3,

the matrix nu(S_K t^(i+j)/D_K), 0<=i,j<floor((K-1)/2), has entries
in Q+Q beta4. Lagrange interpolation cancels the zeta4 component exactly.
Both the integral matrix and its beta4 coefficient matrix are positive
definite. Its determinant is positive and has the full target degree.

The primitive polynomials at K3,5,7 have logarithmic values
4.72051,23.30580,61.97903. The first is4050 beta4-3893. Three separate
exact algorithms agree, including an independent worker review; Arb
certifies the signs. No further scan or general obstruction was inferred.
The high-degree interpolating numerator has real and arithmetic costs;
the denser poles are not a free gain.

3. Hypergeometric transformation gate

Three rational pair-splitting jets of one nonsymmetric Whipple fixture
were computed exactly. Their formal vectors in(1,beta2,beta4) have
determinant25792/6075. The 120-element Thomae group supplies no
cross-branch stabilizer relation at this prescribed base. Six primitive
beta2 eliminations all have absolute value>1. This does not exclude other
base points, transformations or arithmetic effects; no scaled ray was run.
Root independently reconstructed all three rational jets and period vectors.

4. A different starting point: integer topological signatures

A primary TQFT paper supplies integer signatures through cubic cotangent
sums. The direct even-power extension has a beta4 limit, but it lies in a
cyclotomic component negated by an involution fixing the rationals. Every
nonzero such sum is nonrational; rational trace projections, including
rational combinations across odd levels, annihilate it. Its leading limit
is also beta4/pi^4 and includes a beta2 lower term. The proposed direct
integer-sequence contradiction therefore fails. This is a scoped result
about that extension, not all topology or geometric methods.
