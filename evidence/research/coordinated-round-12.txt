COORDINATED BETA(4) RESEARCH: ROUND12
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. Closed for the user's review.

Outcome

There is no irrationality proof and no credible complete route. Three
fresh workers tested different implications of rationality and a recent
recurrence framework. The useful outcome is an exact distinction between
two calibrated beta4 constructions and identification of why the desired
two-formula arithmetic argument does not yet apply. The other gates close
specific shortcuts. These are advances in family understanding, not evidence
that a proof is near.

1. A new paired-formula transfer test

Leibtag et al., New Directions in Irrationality, arXiv:2610.27799v1,
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.27799v1, use two exact presentations of the
same approximants to obtain complementary prime divisibility estimates.
We selectively read that mechanism; we did not audit the whole paper.

The quarter-grid adaptation has an exact period space Q+Q beta3+Q beta4,
and a rational projective coordinate calibrated to beta4. Its coefficient
denominator inherits the original positive integer binomial sum. But the
proposed forward and dual numerators are different, already at n=1.
Both nevertheless define exact pure-beta4 forms; they are two sequences,
not two presentations of one sequence. Their first prescribed primitive
errors are negative, with log absolute values 166.9597 and 224.0707.
These finite failures are not an asymptotic impossibility theorem.

This differs from Carreira's archived recurrence scan, whose chosen
quotients mostly approached pi-expressions and did not target beta4.
It also differs from simply rescaling earlier hypergeometric forms.
The missing ingredient is an exact paired identity with useful arithmetic.

Details: collaboration/round-12/fresh/findings.txt. Root independently
reconstructed both kernels and both rational numerators, including their
nonzero mismatch. The finite signs/sizes are certified with Arb; no formal
verification or human audit is claimed.

2. A rationality-dependent Lambert series

The integer-coefficient source is the classical Jouhet--Mosaki beta_q(4).
Assuming theta=u/v, subtracting the rational polar part produces an
integer-coefficient function tending positively to zero as q approaches1.
This is a valid shrinking source, but its values are not integers.
At q=(p-1)/p with truncation p^2, its exact reduced denominator is p^(p^2),
and its numerator grows like c p^(p^2-2). An independent worker checked
the argument and complete finite denominators.

The all-roots average reduces by an exact divisor recurrence to other
q-values, supplying no algebraicity or integer bridge. The raw counting
sequence also fails the proposed polynomial-plus-o(1) remainder test.
These statements concern the displayed constructions only.
Details: arithmetic/findings.txt and geometry/lambert-review.txt.

3. A regularized arithmetic germ

Canceling lower endpoint periods gives the log-cube primitive
C'(x)=-log^3(x)/[6(1+x^2)]. Under rational theta its entire germ at1 is
rational. But adding any rational constant to the same G-function gives
the same differential module, so the basic holonomy test detects no gain.
More precisely, every rational linear differential operator making this
primitive meromorphic at0 also kills its additive constant. Its local
monodromy, continued around i, supplies the proof. This does not close
nonlinear constructions, other modules or arithmetic holonomy in general.
Details: geometry/findings.txt; root reviewed the monodromy argument.

A separate elementary argument closes rational gauges of the ordinary
two-state beta4 tail as a way to obtain polynomial unimodular transfers.
Its precise scope is in fresh/tail_gauge_gate.txt.
