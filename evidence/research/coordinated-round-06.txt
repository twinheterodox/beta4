COORDINATED BETA(4) INVESTIGATION, ROUND 06
29 October 2026. Local and unpublished. No irrationality proof.

The main surviving construction is a prescribed rational Hermite family.
It has exact beta(4)-only endpoint forms and admits fixed-size residue
and rank reductions. Its required primitive height and nonvanishing
estimates remain open. This round also constructed an integral modular
pole multiplier and an exact algebraic tetralogarithm presentation, while
proving a true primitive obstruction for a new full-rank Gram family.

Three workers and the coordinator prepared separate derivations and
cross-checks. The reviews below are model cross-checks, not human audits,
proof-assistant verification, or claims of novelty. Nothing was published.

1. A selective integral modular pole multiplier

Use the reviewed eta coordinate t of round05, with degree two on X0(32)
and degree one on its Fricke quotient. For an odd prime p put m=p^2,
N=32p^2, t1=t(q), t2=t(q^m), delta1=D_q t1,
delta2=D_q t2=p^2 v, and

 B_c=(delta1+delta2)^2-(c/p^2)delta1 delta2
    =(delta1+p^2 v)^2-c delta1 v in Z[[q]].

Its leading term is q^2. Let

 C_p={4j^2:1<=j<p, j^2=p^2 mod16}
       union {32r^2:1<=r<=floor(p/sqrt8)},
 b_p=|C_p|=(1/4+1/sqrt8)p+O(1),  W_p=product_(c in C_p)B_c.

The ordinary t1=t2 orbit has derivative-ratio invariant
R+R^-1=4j^2/p^2-2; its trace is possible exactly at the displayed
congruence classes. The shifted Fricke orbit has invariant
R+R^-1=32r^2/p^2-2, independently of its other matrix entries. Thus
W_p cancels every ordinary and every shifted r!=0 denominator-zero
family. A local ramification argument proves that denominator zeros
have order at most two and that the cancellation includes branch points.
No period values at the canceled poles need to be evaluated.

The weight can be removed without introducing primes or interior poles.
With Theta_a=theta3(2a tau)^2 and F_p=Theta_1 Theta_(8p^2),

 U_p=W_p/F_p^(2b_p) in q^(2b_p)+q^(2b_p+1)Z[[q]]

is a weight-zero Fricke-invariant modular function, holomorphic in the
upper half-plane. F_p is a zero-free integral unit series and transforms
by -N tau^2. This is a convolution of coefficient pairs, not a global
scalar rescaling. Its initial zero coefficients are not witnesses.

Multiplying round05's alternative remainder by U_p preserves its
beta(4)-only coefficient pairs. The remaining candidates are the new
Fricke fixed points tau=c/d+i/(sqrt32 p d), ad=1+32p^2c^2. The theta
character automatically cancels those with d=3 mod4. Genuine surviving
poles with d=1 mod4 were separately interval-certified at

 (p,c,d)=(3,1,17),(5,1,9),(7,2,9),(11,2,9),(13,1,9),(17,4,9).

They give a raw-coefficient limsup obstruction for these fixed primes.
They do not establish a primitive obstruction, an all-index lower bound,
or a uniform growing-prime theorem. The multiplier is a positive
cancellation construction, not a successful irrationality sequence.

2. A genuine weight-four identity and precise module limitations

Write D4(z)=Im[Li4(z)-log|z|Li3(z)+log^2|z|Li2(z)/3]. Exact distribution
and prime deletion hold for this completed function. Applied to the
non-torsion Broadhurst ladder, however, deletion changes the endpoint to
additional values R(q^d); it does not retain a rational multiple of theta.

Two scoped limits of simple functional repairs are now proved. First,
primitive10p roots create genuine non-real-analytic singularities of
R(t^p). A fixed rational-function module cannot absorb them; added
arguments need total degree at least4(p-1)/3. An explicit divisor-symbol
contraction also retains a coefficient with a p denominator. This is a
parametric/symbol statement, not a statement about all numerical periods.

Second, for a=1+i and distinct odd primes p, the integers
N_p=Norm(a^p-1) are odd, greater than one, and pairwise coprime; every
prime divisor ell satisfies ell=1 mod4p. Thus log|1-a^-p| introduces
one new rational-log direction per p, with log N_p=p log2+o(1).
This closes the proposed fixed rational S-unit/log module, not every
larger functional construction or a primitive approximation family.

A constructive pivot specializes Gangl's weight-four relation using
X={w,b,c}, Y={2/3,2/3,-1/3}, w=(1+i)/2, and b,c the roots of
2x^2-(1-i)x-1. It yields an exact presentation of theta as21 completed
tetralogarithms at specified quartic arguments, all inside the unit disk.
The coefficient l1 norm is39, maximum modulus0.98100176286..., and the
least common integer scale to algebraic integers is216. The complete
exact argument table and coefficient list are saved, not fitted by PSLQ.

Source audit matters here. Gangl's printed definition includes an extra
Li0 term if read literally with the standard convention; it does not
satisfy the intended identity. The packet records that discrepancy and
proves the needed standard-normalization identity through an exact
symbol certificate and Zagier's criterion. A second worker independently
checked a different one-variable certificate and fixed the additive
constant. A small numerical residual alone is not used as the proof.

The original uncompleted relative trace into Q(i) is valid. Inverting
arguments individually to place them inside the disk breaks that orbit;
the naive traced Taylor series then has radius below1/6. Completion also
requires log|b| in the compositum with the conjugate field. A coupled
algebraic/logarithmic rational approximation construction remains missing.

collaboration/round-06/identities/findings.txt and adjacent scripts/data.

3. A positive full-rank Gram that really fails after primitive reduction

Using the previously derived cyclotomic kernel
J(P)=integral P(u,v) log^2(uv)/(sqrt(uv)(1+uv)) du dv, define

 mu_m=J((u-v)^(2m))/96=binom(2m,m)theta+B_m,
 P_n(X)=det[binom(2(i+j),i+j)X+B_(i+j)]_(0<=i,j<n).

The real moments have positive support on[0,1], whereas their theta
coefficient is the arcsine moment sequence on[0,4]. Consequently
P_n has exact degree n and leading coefficient2^(n-1), with
P_n(theta)>0 and log P_n(theta)=-(log4)n^2+O(n log n).
This genuinely avoids the elementary rank-one collapse.

Nevertheless, for every prime p>4n, its ACTUAL primitive multiplier obeys

 v_p(kappa_n)=3 max(0,2n-2-floor(p/4)).

The first nonzero third-layer residue produces an anti-triangular block;
an actual polynomial coefficient attains the maximal denominator, so
uncomputed global content cannot erase it. The prime range(4n,8n)
alone gives log kappa_n>=6n^2+o(n^2). Combining true value and true
primitive lower bounds proves

 log[kappa_n P_n(theta)]>=(6-log4)n^2+o(n^2).

Thus this principal Gram family fails even the degree-normalized
small-value criterion. Other frames, weights, and arithmetic filters
are not ruled out. A separate binomial/Mellin construction in the
arithmetic packet also retains p^4 on an infinite prime subsequence;
that latter fact alone is not an actual-value obstruction.

Independent mathematical review: coordinator/gram_review.txt.

4. Prescribed rational Hermite sources: the live construction

For D=1+x^2 or1+x-x^2, prescribe G=R/D^n, deg R<=2n, G(0)=0, and

 f=A/(1+x^2)+E^4G/x, E=x d/dx,

with zeros of order n at both0 and1. Signed integer minors, with a
specified RREF fallback at deficient rank, choose the source independently
of theta. Four integrations by parts give exactly

 integral_0^1 f(x)(-log x)^3 dx/6=A theta+R(1)/D(1)^n.

The rational endpoint is dyadic for the circular denominator and integral
for the endpoint-unit denominator. Exact augmented-determinant formulas
retain both source content and final pair content. Merely having only
dyadic endpoint denominators is not a primitive arithmetic saving: the
integer source coefficients may be large.

The residual numerator has fixed degree: f=x^n(1-x)^n P/D^(n+4), deg P<=6,
in the circular case; with the extra factor1+x^2 in the denominator it
has deg P<=8 in the endpoint-unit case. The independent analytic follow-up
reduces the circular source condition to six local logarithmic-residue
equations in seven coefficients. Its zero-A subspace has exactly the
nullity of an explicit4-by-4 binomial derivative matrix. These are proved
equivalences, not observations from finite rank calculations.

Both reductions extend to asymmetric endpoint orders with total2n.
The denominator D_c=1+c x(1-x), c>0, has an analogous eight-residue
description in nine coefficients and still has integral endpoint pairs.
The coordinator's exact real optimizer gives the sufficient budget
kappa H rho(c,a/n)^n; in the symmetric case rho=1/(4+c).
It does not omit the unknown source height H or primitive content.

The n=1 circular source has A=0 and primitive value1, explicitly accounted
for. Finite exact checks reproduce complete source and primitive pairs;
the tested nonzero-A primitive values all exceed one. Sturm calculations
prove nonzero source integrals for circular n=3,...,8, with differing
signs after A-positive normalization. There is no uniform rank,
nonvanishing, or favorable primitive-height theorem yet.

The next useful algebraic step is to extend the fixed4-by-4 homogeneous
rank test to an inhomogeneous formula for the actual endpoint pair.
That could make joint content and real-error analysis substantially
more explicit. It is a research target, not a completed consequence.

5. Evidence, preservation, and continuation

Exact and interval checks were reproduced during integration, including
the two independent symbol certificates, full primitive Hermite pairs,
Gram residues/exponents, modular trace and theta identities, and selected
period residuals. Finite checks are kept separate from all-index proofs.
The archived baseline comprised650 files; no pre-round file was changed
before integration. Only the root README and manifest are updated as
the index of the new packet; preceding proofs and computations remain
unchanged. The integration record stores the verification and hashes.

Continue autonomously with the Hermite endpoint/content problem and a
fresh construction search. Do not infer that any current family succeeds
from its structural reductions or fails merely from poor finite values.
The objective remains an irrationality proof of beta(4), and is not yet
achieved.
